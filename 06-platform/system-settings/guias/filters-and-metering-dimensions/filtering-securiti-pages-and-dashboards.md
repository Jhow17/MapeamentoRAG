# Filtering Securiti pages and dashboards

You can use filters on various Securiti dashboards and other pages to quickly focus your view of the current data based on various criteria. Where available, the filter tools are always located in the upper right corner of the page, as shown below.

![filter_tool.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2bf04940-b33a-3ed6-07c0-6dd63137a53d(3).png)

Once you apply filters, you can save them and later load them.

To apply filters:

1. On the page or dashboard, click the **Filter** icon near the upper right corner of the page to open the filters panel, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2022-12-05_at_2_42_06_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-57e31a84-78d0-b767-0106-fd7ad2686fc8(3).png)
2. Expand one or more areas on the filter panel and use the tools there to choose or enter criteria on which to filter the page.
3. Near the bottom of the filter panel, click **Apply** to apply your filters to the page, eliminating those items or elements that do not meet the criteria.

![Screenshot_2022-12-05_at_2_42_50_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-5574696a-2788-4fab-9195-9d62ecca37e2(3).png)

Securiti also shows you a count of the active filters at the top of the filters panel.

If you collapse the filters panel while filters are active, Securiti shows a check mark on the filter icon to alert you that your view of the page is filtered.

To clear individual filters:

- Expand the active filters area at the top of the filter panel and click close (X) for the filters, then click **Apply**.

To clear all filters:

- Click **Options** (three dots) at the top of the filters panel and choose **Reset all filters**, then click **Apply**.
