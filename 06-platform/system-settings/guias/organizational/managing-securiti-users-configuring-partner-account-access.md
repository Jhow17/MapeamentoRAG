# Configuring partner account access

You can grant access to your Securiti account by users from managed services partners who have email addresses outside your corporate domain. Such partners might be companies with staff members who use Securiti on your behalf to conduct assessments or process DSR requests.

To create a partner account, you must first contact Securiti support to request that a partner domain be added to your Securiti account. Let us know the exact domain name and extension for the partner.

After we let you know that we have added the partner domain, you can create user accounts for partner staff members as you would for your internal users. However, you use the new User Type menu as shown below to set the user type to **Partner**. When you create the new user, Securiti checks to make sure that their email address is within either your domain or your partner domains.

![settingsConfigPartnerAccess.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-0bf57c8c-b610-69cf-cd98-8adabf5c74c7(3).png)

For more information on creating Securiti users, see [Managing Securiti Users](/v1/docs/configuring-access-management-managing-securiti-users).
