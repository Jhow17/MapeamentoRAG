# Configuring the Securiti self-service portal

**Scenario**

As a Data Security Officer, you need a way for employees and external partners to report breaches quickly. Without a portal, reports come through emails or calls, making tracking inconsistent. With the Self-Service Portal, anyone can securely log incidents or start assessments, even without a Securiti account. This gives you structured, trackable reports and ensures no breach or risk goes unnoticed.

With Securiti's self-service portal, members of your organization who do not have their own Securiti accounts can:

- Initiate and conduct assessments
- Initiate and track data breach incidents
  - Report data breach incidents with attachments as evidence
  - Delete data breach incidents from their portals

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti lets the data subjects delete their reported breach incident irrespective of the breach progress state.

To customize and publish the portal:

- In Securiti, click **Settings** , and choose **Organizational > Self Service Portal** , as shown below. Make sure you enable editing mode.

![homepage_service_portal.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-14434cab-09dd-2db3-edef-af67678c28c4(1).png)

You can use the tools on the page to edit the name and insert a logo or image.

### Customize the Home View

You can modify the header, footer, and upload your organization's logo within the Self-Service portal.

1. In the Self-Service portal, at the middle of the page, click **Home** (icon).

![select_home_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-aef9048b-7f25-d006-d2a4-91c95dfa735e(1).png)
2. Under the Header section, use the tools to upload your organization's logo, enter the Site Title to rename the Self-Service portal, and upload the icon for the home page.

![edit_header_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-578df704-c9f1-0703-c96f-7cbad9264690(1).png)

You can also customize the tagline and body text of the home page based on the specific goals and messaging you want for your Self-Service portal.

- In the **Home** section, click **Headline Section**. Enter the headline and body text that you want to use. You can also keep the text to default by clicking **Reset to Default** option.

![headline_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f8f2a779-e5bd-d7b9-949f-2a41d91e1a68(1).png)

Additionally, you can utilize toggles to either show or hide headings or text from the Self-Service Portal.

You can also customize the footer of the home page.

- In the **Home** section, click Footer and enable/disable the toggles to hide or show sections as per your need. You can also customize the **Copyright Text** shown in the footer according to your requirements.

![footer_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6f146e88-9053-1602-2923-aece277c5910(1).png)

Once done, at the upper right corner of the page, click **Save** to save the view

#### Customize text and button Styling

You can change how text and buttons look in the Self-Service Portal. This means you can pick colors, fonts, and button shapes to make the interface look consistent and user-friendly.

#### Colors

In the Colors section, the **Primary Color** denotes the button color, and the **Secondary Color** represents the text color in the self-service portal.

To change the color:

1. On the right side in the middle of the page, expand **Colors** under Universal Page Styling and enter the hexadecimal color code for Primary and Secondary fields respectively.
2. You can also select the color for text on buttons in the self-service portal. Either choose Base White and enter hexadecimal color code or select Secondary color so that button text adopts the text color defined in step 1.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If you don’t enter the color code for the Base White, the button text automatically takes on the white color.
3. Enable **Custom Hyper link** and enter the color code for the hyperlink.
4. To enhance button accessibility and outline them when in the active state, enable **Custom Focus Color** and enter the color code.
5. Once done, click **Save Changes**.

![modify_colors_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f9305959-bef5-34d8-0fce-530903af617f(1).png)

#### Fonts

You can change the font for customizable text on all pages.

#### Button Shape

You can also choose the button shape as per your preference.
  1. Expand **Fonts** under Universal Page Styling and further choose a color from the **Font Family** menu and click **Save Changes**.
  2. Further, at the middle of the page, activate **Preview** toggle to preview its appearance to observe how it looks when published.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> This Font setting applies to all the text within the Self-Service portal. If you want to change the font of just a particular heading or text, use the formatting options available in the tip tap text editor as shown below. Similarly, you can also change the color of some particular text within the self-service portal using these options.
> 
> ![editing_tool_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-95ad22e5-ca5f-b6aa-d008-561acd134621(1).png)
  - Expand **Button Shape** under Universal Page Styling and further choose from the options available, and click **Save Changes**.

### Customize the Assessments View

You can customize the Assessments page in the Self-Service Portal by adjusting your headline and body text. Additionally, you can include or exclude published templates that guest users use to create assessments. You can also use the preview option to see how assessments appear on the Self-Service Portal.

To customize the Assessments view:

1. In the Self-Service Portal, in the middle of the page, click **Assessments**, or click **Customize** on the Assessments card.

![customize_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a517f34f-16c7-130a-8fe9-fb4e33e867d8(1).png)
2. Enter the headline and body text that you want to show on the Assessments page. You can also keep the text to default by clicking **Reset to Default** option.
3. Once done, at the upper right corner click **Save**.

To add assessments templates:

1. On the **Assessments** section, near the middle right corner in the Assessment Templates section, click **Edit Assessments Templates List**.

![edit_assessment_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a418d1c5-9a68-c0c1-548a-36a45738483d(1).png)
2. At the upper right corner, click **Inactive**, select templates from list that you want to add. Further, at the upper left corner, click **Activate Selected**. Once done, click **Save Assessment Templates**.

![Screenshot_2023-12-29_at_2_13_27_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-39648dea-febb-0fe6-6805-7b1d6c9881a9(1).png)

All selected templates appear in the **Active** list.

You can also exclude templates that are no longer required.

1. On the **Active** list, select the templates you want to deactivate and click **De-activate Selected**.
2. Once done, click **Save Assessment Templates.**

#### Disable Assessments from Self-Service Portal

You can also disable the assessments page from the Self-Service Portal.

- In the Self-Service Portal, switch the toggle on the **Assessments** card as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-12-29_at_2_15_38_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f2e4fb7b-41ac-21c7-5ea0-9bad04e612fd(1).png)

### Customize Incidents View

You can customize the Incidents page in the Self-Service Portal by adjusting your headline and body text as per the need.

To customize the Incidents view:

1. In the Self-Service Portal, at the middle of the page, click I**ncidents**. Or click Customize on **Incidents** card.

![incidents_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b962f9a7-46d6-817f-5f9f-2dc35bfde436(1).png)
2. Enter the headline and body text that you want to show on Incidents page. You can also keep the text to default by clicking the **Reset to Default** option.

You can also disable the incidents page from the Self-Service Portal.

- In the Self-Service Portal, switch the toggle on **Incidents** card

### Preview the Self-Service Portal

While configuring the self-service portal, you can preview its appearance to observe how it looks when published.

1. In the Self-Service Portal, at the middle of the page, activate **Preview** toggle.
2. Further, switch among **Home, Assessments** or **Incidents** pages to see how each page would appear once the Self-Service Portal is published.

![preview_content_selfservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-92a403b3-0e25-8e0c-2bdc-9b0ec9ed68c3(1).png)

### Publish the Self-Service Portal

1. Near the upper right corner of the page, click Publish to publish the portal. When prompted, click **Yes** to confirm.
2. Use the **Hosted Link** tools at the top of the page to copy the URL, as shown below.

![hosted_link.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9ade4553-67bf-b44b-0536-a30155f214a0(1).png)

You can publish this URL on a wiki or an internal page for your organization members to see.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If the Manage Org Units premium feature is enabled for your Securiti tenant, and you belong to more than one organization, each organization has a unique SSP login URL. For more information on multi-org feature, see [Managing Organizations.](/v1/docs/system-settings-managing-organizations)

When a user visits the above URL, Securiti prompts them for their email address to access the portal, as shown below.

![SSP_login.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-26192be9-8a74-5ace-a025-c9a530742ded(1).png)

Securiti then sends them an email, as shown below.

![email_link_to_SSP.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-812bfdf1-e65f-0be2-caf9-cdf614600cf9(1).png)

Assuming that [advanced link control](/v1/docs/system-settings-configuring-securiti-general-settings) is not enabled for your tenant, when they click the link in their email, it redirects to the self-service portal. The toolbar allows only limited access to tools for non-Securiti users.

![ssp_login_as_guest_user.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-56ec8b84-7f01-07c2-4f56-21e1bac81ea6(1).png)
