# Logging in as a partner user

If you work for a managed services partner and use Securiti on behalf of another company, use this procedure to log in as a partner user to that company's Securiti account:

1. Log in to your organization's Securiti account as normal.
2. In Securiti, click **Settings** and choose **Partner Access > Securiti Partner Access**.
3. On the Securiti Partner Access page, choose the tenant account to log in to, then click **Log in as Partner** as shown below.
