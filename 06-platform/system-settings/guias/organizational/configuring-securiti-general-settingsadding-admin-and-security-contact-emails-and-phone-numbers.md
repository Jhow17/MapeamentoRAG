# Adding admin and security contact emails and phone numbers

You can designate one or more admin and security contact phone numbers for your organization that we can use to contact you in case of a security breach or incident.

To add this information for your organization, open a ticket with Securiti support.

To view the contact phone numbers currently configured for your organization:

1. Click **Settings**.
2. On the Organizational > General page, scroll down to the areas for **Admin Contact** and **Security Contact** as shown below.
