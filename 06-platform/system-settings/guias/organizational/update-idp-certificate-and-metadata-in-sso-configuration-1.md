# Update IDP certificate and metadata in SSO configuration

If you have already configured and are using Single Sign-on (SSO), you might need to update your IDP certificate or metadata details.

To modify the information and validate your SSO metadata:

1. In Securiti, from the upper left corner of the **Dashboard**, click the **Jewel** icon.
2. From the Platform Services section, open **Settings**, and choose **Integrations > Single Sign-On**.
3. Update your **Force SSO Login**, **SSO Enforced Domain(s)**, and **Enable JIT User Provisioning** under the Single Sign-On Configuration section, and click **Update**, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/update-idp-certificate-and-metadata-in-sso-configuration-image-9orm9eh6.png)

1. From the Single Sign-On Configuration section:
  1. Disable your **Single Sign-On** toggle to switch off your configured SSO and see the options, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/update-idp-certificate-and-metadata-in-sso-configuration-image-yhdncxqq.png)
  1. From the right side of your configured SSO, click **Options** (three dots), which you switched off in the previous step, and select **Edit**.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/update-idp-certificate-and-metadata-in-sso-configuration-image-nf4vi9tb.png)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/update-idp-certificate-and-metadata-in-sso-configuration-image-3n2ayedq.png)
2. Modify your required details to suit, and click **Validate Metadata** to verify that the details are correct, as shown below.
3. Once done, click Save Configuration, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/update-idp-certificate-and-metadata-in-sso-configuration-image-g0aeeqsn.png)

Securiti successfully updates your SSO configurations, including the IDP certificate and other metadata details.
