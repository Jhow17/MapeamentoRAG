# Using the Securiti Messages & Notifications panel

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can use the Securiti Messages & Notifications panel to interact with the co-owners and collaborators only for the following Securiti modules:
> 
> - Data Subject Rights
> - Privacy Notice Management
> - Assessment Automation
> - Enterprise Data Catalog

The Securiti Messages & Notifications panel is a secure communication platform that lets you interact with other stakeholders involved in your Organization’s processes. For instance, you can interact with the co-owners and collaborators of:

- DSR requests related tasks and subtasks of your data subjects
- Privacy notice(s) for your organization’s website(s)
- Assessment questions to assess the readiness of your organization

### Messages & notifications functions

Use the following functions of the **Messages & Notifications** panel to either converse with other stakeholders, search the content of your messages, or see system notifications:

### Messages

You can also respond to your messages via the Messages & Notifications panel directly from the Securiti dashboard. To communicate with your co-owners using this panel:

1. In Securiti, near the bottom of the left navigation bar, click the **Messages & Notifications** (message) icon.
2. From the left messages navigation bar, click the title of your DSR request, privacy notice, or assessment question in which you are already engaged in communication with your co-owners.
3. Type your reply and if you want, also tag your co-owner in the message as shown below.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti does not let you initiate a conversation over the DSR requests of your data subjects, your privacy notice(s), and assessment questions with your co-owners from the **Messages & Notifications**. You can only converse back and forth with your co-owner(s) and collaborator(s) with whom you have already initiated conversations.

See the following topics on how to communicate with co-owners and collaborators of different modules:

- [Communicating with co-owners of DSR request tasks or subtasks](/v1/docs/communicating-with-dsr-and-subtask-owners)
- [Privacy notice communication between co-owners](/v1/docs/communicating-with-privacy-notice-co-owners)
- [Assessment questions communication between collaborators](/v1/docs/collaborating-using-question-message-channels)

### Notifications

You can use this functionality to view the list of system notifications generated in:

- DSR requests when you are tagged in any DSR task or subtask by another co-owner. You receive notifications when a stakeholder who does not have appropriate permissions tries to access your tasks or subtasks related to a DSR request. You also receive notifications when the DSR automation fails for any data system.
- Privacy notice when the privacy notice owner adds you as a co-owner and tags you in a message.
- Assessment questions when the collaborator assigns you a question to answer in the assessment and when the collaborator tags you in a message.
- Pod deployment status and when pod deployment fails.

### Search box

The search box near the upper right corner of the **Messages & Notifications** lets you search for any notification. It is a free text search functionality that lets you enter keywords to search for notifications from the list as shown below.

For example, each organization has its own data systems and connectors, its own assessments, its own DSR forms and DSR requests, its own workflows, and its own data maps and data catalog.
