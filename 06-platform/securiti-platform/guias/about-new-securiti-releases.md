# About new releases from Securiti

New product updates from Securiti are rolled out in phases and may become available at different times depending on your tenant and cloud environment.

To check **when new updates will be available for your tenant**, specifically for your **production cloud**, refer to the relevant **status pages** listed below. These pages provide the most up-to-date information on release schedules, rollout progress, and service availability by cloud region.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Status pages are the source of truth for update availability. If an update is not yet visible in your Securiti tenant, it may still be in the process of being rolled out to your production cloud.
