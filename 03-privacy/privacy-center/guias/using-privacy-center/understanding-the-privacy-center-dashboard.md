# Understanding the Privacy Center Dashboard

The Privacy Center **Dashboard** provides an overview of all your Privacy Centers, including the total number of Privacy Centers and their current states. It also lists all the Privacy Centers you have created and lets you create and manage them from one place. You can create, clone, or delete a Privacy Center, and perform bulk customization across multiple Privacy Centers directly from the dashboard.

## Creating a new Privacy Center

To create a new Privacy Center, near the upper-right corner of the Dashboard, click **+ New Privacy Center**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total privacy centers, published, and draft counts with action buttons.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_5.png)

Securiti opens the initial setup page for the Privacy Center.

For detailed instructions on creating, configuring, and customizing your Privacy Center, see [Build your privacy center in a day](/latest/docs/build-your-privacy-center-in-a-day).

## Viewing Privacy Center metrics

Privacy Center Dashboard also lets you view high-level metrics for your Privacy Centers, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total privacy centers, published, and draft counts with detailed entries.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_1(1).png)

The high-level metrics show the total number of Privacy Centers and the number of Privacy Centers in **Published** and **Draft** states.

## Searching Privacy Centers

Near the lower right of the high-level metrics, from the search bar, you can search the Privacy Center by name, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total privacy centers, published, and draft counts with search functionality.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_4.png)

## Understanding Privacy Center table

Below the Privacy Center high-level metrics, the Privacy Center table lists all your Privacy Centers, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy centers with details on publication and ownership.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashbaord.png)

The Privacy Center table includes the following details:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Shows the Privacy Center name. |
| Published Date | Shows the Privacy Center published date. > [!NOTE] > Note > > Securiti displays **Never Published** if the Privacy Center is still in the **Draft** state. |
| Owner | Shows the owner name of the Privacy Center. |
| Privacy Center Links | Provides options to view the published Privacy Center and copy its published or embedded links, as shown below. ![Dashboard displaying privacy centers with details on publication and ownership.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_2.png) |

## Managing Privacy Centers

From the Privacy Center Dashboard, you can clone or delete existing Privacy Centers.

To clone a Privacy Center:

1. In the Privacy Center table, near the far right of the row, click **Clone** (+ icon), as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total privacy centers, published, and draft statistics with action buttons.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_7(1).png)

Securiti displays the **Privacy Center** setup page, as shown below.

![Privacy Center setup form with company details and contact information fields displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_setup_screen.png)
2. Provide or modify the mandatory details.

For more information, see [Cloning a Privacy Center](/latest/docs/cloning-a-privacy-center).

To delete a Privacy Center:

1. In the Privacy Center table, near the far right of the row, click **Options** (three dots).
2. From the menu, select **Delete**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total, published, and draft privacy centers with action options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_3.png)

## Customizing Privacy Centers in bulk

From the Privacy Center Dashboard, you can apply style customizations to multiple Privacy Centers at once. Bulk customization lets you modify interface and text colors, customize button shapes, and select a font for your Privacy Centers.

To customize Privacy Centers in bulk, near the upper-right corner of the page, next to the search bar, click the **Bulk Actions** menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying total privacy centers, published, and draft counts with action options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_center_dashboard_bulk_actions.png)

For more information on bulk customization, see [Customizing Privacy Centers in bulk](/latest/docs/customizing-privacy-centers-in-bulk).
