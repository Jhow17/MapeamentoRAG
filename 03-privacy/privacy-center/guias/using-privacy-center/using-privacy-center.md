# Using Privacy Center

Privacy Center lets you stand up and deliver features related to privacy, security, and compliance to your data subjects with minimum effort. Privacy Center is a centralized location where you can access several features of the Securiti modules.

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This module is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

It is a singular place to try out many features that ensure global privacy and compliance for your organization. You can avail these functionalities by designing, customizing, and deploying your own Privacy Center in a hassle-free way.

![Screenshot_2023-02-27_at_3_38_54_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-97b7fe4d-f8c1-2bd7-573f-5aa321357067.png)

To get yourself familiar with the initial setup process, see [Getting started with Privacy Center](/v1/docs/getting-started-with-privacy-center).
