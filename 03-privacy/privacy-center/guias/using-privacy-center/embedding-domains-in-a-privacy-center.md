# Embedding domains in a Privacy Center

Securiti lets you embed your Privacy Center in a domain. This domain can either be the subdomain of your base domain or any other external domain associated with your organization. For instance, subdomains of your base domain (`www.securiti.ai`) could be `app.securiti.ai` and `cloud.securiti.ai`. You can add as many domains in which you want to embed your Privacy Center.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> You must publish your existing Privacy Center again if you want to embed any domain in it.

To embed your Privacy Center in a domain:

1. In the main dashboard of your Privacy Center, click the **Settings** (gear) icon as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with options for data requests and preferences.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embed_domain_1.png)
2. On the **Privacy Center Settings** popup, add your domain or subdomain as shown below, and click **Save**.

![Privacy Center settings showing embedded domains and options for customization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embed_domain_2.png)
3. Click **Embedded** to view your embedded Privacy Center directly from your web browser.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with options for data requests and customization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embed_domain_3.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti displays an error message with an appropriate error code if the embedded Privacy Center ID is missing or incorrect, as shown below.
> 
> ![pc_error_message.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9ded1cb5-7c5c-44d7-8567-595ff6d5bbfd.png)
4. Click **Link** (copy) icon to copy the Embedded Link from the Hosted Link section as shown below and use it in your website’s `index.html` file to embed the Privacy Center.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with embedded button options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embed_domain_4.png)

You can also copy the embedded link from the list of the Privacy Centers as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-04-07_at_11_39_55_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-8dd37481-b588-d9cc-62f8-25d4e62c8a0a.png)

Once you embed your Privacy Center in your domain, click the **Review Cookies** button under the **Cookie Preferences** tab of the this Privacy Center, the cookie preference center directly opens up in the website for you interact with it right away.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You must integrate Securiti’s cookie consent SDK in your domain for this functionality to work.

Securiti lets you embed your Privacy Center into a domain. This domain may be a subdomain of your base domain or any other external domain linked to your organization. You have the flexibility to include as many domains as you wish for embedding your Privacy Center.

After you build and fully configure your Privacy Center, Securiti lets you copy a link from the Privacy Center dashboard. You can use this hosted link to host the newly created PC in your domain.
