# Customizing your Privacy Center

This module lets you make your Privacy Center bespoke before you can publish it on your website. For all five main features, you can customize:

| Feature | Customization |
| --- | --- |
| Organizational Logo | You can upload the logo for your organization in **PNG**, **JPG** and **GIF** formats. > [!NOTE] > Note > > Use a logo size of at least **96 × 96 px** and at most **480 × 480 px** to prevent errors. |
| Privacy Center Title | The title for your custom Privacy Center to host on your website. |
| Homepage Icon | The main banner to be displayed under the title of your Privacy Center. |
| Header (toggle) | Enable this toggle to display header on your Privacy Center. This lets you add your organization's logo and the title of your privacy center. You can also change the homepage icon of your privacy center. |
| Headline Section (toggle) | Enable this toggle to display headline section on your Privacy Center. You can add a header text to convey the prime message of your organization, add headline banner image and text to describe the main section of your Privacy Center. |
| Jump Cards (toggle) | Enable this toggle to display jump cards on your Privacy Center. You can customize four default jump card sections by adding heading, body text and call to action (CTA) button of your choice. > [!NOTE] > Note > > Securiti supports up to 100 characters for Jump Card CTAs to better accommodate multi-language text lengths. For instance, **Manage Your Privacy Preferences** (31 characters) becomes **Gérer vos préférences de confidentialité** in French Canadian, which is 40 characters. |
| Features List Section (toggle) | Enable this toggle to display feature list section on your Privacy Center. This lets you add custom image and text to feature a list of main services you offer via your Privacy Center. |
| Footer (toggle) | Enable this toggle to display footer on your Privacy Center. This lets you add custom copyright text and a call to action button that reroutes your data subjects to your base domain (main website). |
| Cookie Preference Center | You can rename the button and configure your own cookie preference center via Securiti’s **Cookie Consent Management** module. For more information, see [How to customize a cookie banner and preference center](/v1/docs/customizing-your-cookie-banner-and-preference-center). |
| Data Request Form | You can configure the DSR form to suit through Securiti’s **Data Subject Rights** module. For more information, see [How to build a DSR form](/v1/docs/creating-and-publishing-dsr-request-forms). |
| Privacy Notice Content | You can configure and customize the privacy notice for your organization through Securiti’s **Privacy Notice** **Management** module. For more information, see [How to build a privacy notice](/v1/docs/creating-a-new-privacy-notice). |
| Universal Preference Center | Lets you custom build a preference center with consent and processing purposes via Securiti’s **Universal Consent Management** module. For more information, see [How to build a universal preference center](/v1/docs/building-a-universal-preference-center). |
| Do Not Sell or Share | Customize the **Do Not Sell** switch title, body text and the title for the Confirm button based on the legislation of your region. |

### Customizing the Privacy Center dashboard

To open the Privacy Center, in Securiti, open **Privacy Center**, then choose **Dashboard**.

You can customize your Privacy Center to suit your organizational requirements using the individual module sections as shown below. The name of your privacy center is also configurable. Each main module feature has its own toggle switch to make it visible in the hosted privacy center.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with various data management options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_pc_1.png)

You can utilize the privacy center in both preview and edit modes along with different viewing modes as shown below.

![Privacy Center page displaying data management options and user preferences.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_pc_2.png)

![Settings for uploading logos and customizing the Privacy Center interface options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_pc_3.png)

When you create your Privacy Center, Securiti starts the domain scan immediately in the servers and databases at the backend. The **Force Refresh** button lets you force refresh the status of your domain scan only if the **Cookie Preferences** tab status is **Action Required** as shown below. When customizing the dashboard of your Privacy Center, you check the status of your domain scan with this button. After force refreshing, the status of the tab changes to **Ready** if the domain scan is completed.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center showing cookies preferences and data request options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_pc_4.png)

### Styling options for Privacy Centers

You can change the Privacy Center colors, fonts and button shapes from the **Universal Styling Page**. To access the **Universal Styling Page**, enable **Edit** mode, and open the **Home** tab, as shown below.

![Overview of Privacy Center with options for data preferences and customization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_1.png)

In the **Colors** menu, **Primary Color** changes the color of UI elements such as button links, while **Secondary Color** changes the text color. You can also customize the page background color, hyperlink color, button color, and focus color separately in their respective sections, as shown below.

![Settings interface displaying logo upload, color options, and privacy center title.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_2.png)

To match the brand guide line you can select the font or upload the custom font from the **Fonts** menu, as shown below.

![Settings for the Privacy Center, including logo upload and font family selection options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_3.png)

To upload the custom font, open the **Font Family** menu and click the **+ Add Custom Font**, as shown below.

![Settings interface for a Privacy Center with options for logo and font customization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_4.png)

> [!WARNING]
> **Caution**
> 
> It is your responsibility to ensure that any custom fonts you upload are properly licensed.
> 
> Before uploading a custom font, you must:
> 
> - Verify that you have the necessary rights or licenses to use the font for your intended purpose.
> - Review and comply with the font's End User License Agreement (EULA) or any applicable usage terms.

In the **Custom Font Upload** popup, click **+ Add Another Font Set** to upload custom font files in Normal, Semi Bold, and Bold weights, as shown below.

![Custom font upload interface for configuring font settings in the Privacy Center.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_5n.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti currently supports only the `.woff2` font format.

You can also modify the button shape to **Rectangle**, **Rounded Corners**, or **Pill** from the **Button Shape** menu, as shown below.

![Settings for a Privacy Center, including logo upload and button shape options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pc_styling_66.png)

To apply the styling, click **Save Changes**. To revert to the original styling, click **Revert**.

Securiti also lets you apply color and styling in bulk from the Privacy Center dashboard. For more information on bulk configuration, see [Customizing Privacy Center in Bulk](/latest/docs/customizing-privacy-centers-in-bulk).

### Privacy Center Status

There are two statuses of your privacy center. An unpublished privacy center has a status of **Draft** and once you publish it, the status changes to **Published** as shown below.

![Customizing_your_Privacy_Center-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6c62ca9f-f85c-9bd1-f4bf-ea829e582354.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Once you publish your Privacy Center, you can also share the URL of any tab to directly navigate to that tab using a different webpage.

### Viewing hosted Privacy Center

You can view how your Privacy Center looks like in the hosted version after completely configuring it. From the dashboard of your Privacy Center, click **View Published** as shown below. Clicking this link immediately opens up the hosted version of your Privacy Center in the next tab of your web browser.

![Customizing_your_Privacy_Center-3.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-904f57ef-79eb-4bb5-dd37-2649c4560915.png)

When you click the **Review Cookies** button under the **Cookie Preferences** tab on the hosted version of your Privacy Center, it redirects you to the domain associated with your Privacy Center and opens up the cookie preference center as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-06-21_at_12_36_31_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-0384d67b-5208-b174-d809-a2a8cfbf467b.png)

![Screenshot_2023-06-21_at_12_41_50_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3fb1fbe1-0285-28b5-c1d8-d54307423db5.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can only see the cookie preference center if one exists on the domain.

On the published privacy center, your data subjects can enable accessibility settings to enhance content visibility and readability. Near the bottom right corner of your privacy center, they can access these settings by clicking the **Web Accessibility** icon. From the **Accessibility Settings** popup, they can enable **Enable high contrast for increased visibility** toggle, and click **Apply Settings** to switch the content to high contrast mode, as shown below.

![PC-accessibility-1-RN_png.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3c04e9e1-4480-1814-0e5b-d9193ce4cb22.png)

![PC-accessibility-2-RN.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6780cb8c-c648-aa60-fe4b-5fd3c5f8d80a.png)

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) mandates organizations to prominently feature a Do Not Sell My Personal Information link. This link provides guidance on opting out of the sale of data of individuals such as data subjects or website visitors etc. Using Privacy Center, you can enable or disable this option for your data subjects.
