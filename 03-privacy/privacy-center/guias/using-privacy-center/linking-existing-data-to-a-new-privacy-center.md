# Linking existing data to a new Privacy Center

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you often need to update privacy components such as privacy notices, cookie banners, or request forms within the privacy center without affecting existing records. However, replacing them manually can lead to data loss or inconsistencies. For instance, when launching a new Privacy Center, you may want to replace old cookie banners or request forms while keeping past consent data intact. Securiti’s Privacy Center lets you link existing data to new privacy centers, so you can make updates easily without losing history or breaking existing records.

Securiti lets you link the existing data on your Securiti tenant with your newly created Privacy Centers. This adds flexibility and the ability for backward compatibility. For instance, you can now replace your existing Cookies Preferences, Consent Preferences, Privacy Notice or *DSR form* with new ones from within the module. This functionality is available for all subscription types.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> You must publish your Privacy Center again to reflect the new changes. Only **Pro, Enterprise** and **Business** tier versions support this functionality.

To link existing data with a newly created Privacy Center:

1. In Securiti, open your existing Privacy Center or if you have not created a Privacy Center before, click **+ New Privacy Center** from the upper right corner of the main dashboard to create a new Privacy Center.
2. After configuring the registration form and creating your new Privacy Center as defined in [Building multiple Privacy Centers](/v1/docs/building-multiple-privacy-centers), open your Privacy Center.
3. Choose any section with which you want to link existing data. For instance, click **Privacy Notice** and make sure to enable the toggle to **Edit** mode as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with options for customization and data requests.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_1.png)
4. From the **Use Existing Privacy Notices** section on the right side of the page, enable the toggle adjacent to the title as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-05-15_at_12_08_06_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9ae978f6-b1da-0428-4e7b-9088b47e617a.png)
5. Click the menu and select any existing privacy notice to link with this newly created Privacy Center as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-05-15_at_12_11_20_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c5476c0d-fd30-054a-d2af-50fa45bf8684.png)
6. Once you select any existing privacy notice, Securiti automatically links that privacy notice with your newly created Privacy Center and shows you a success message.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with linked resources and customization options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_2.png)
7. After linking the other remaining sections such as Cookie Preferences, Individual Data Requests etc., click **Publish** on the upper right corner of the main dashboard to publish your Privacy Center as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with publish option.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_3.png)

Cookies preferences are user settings that dictate how websites use and store data through cookies, providing control over tracking, personalized content, and site functionality. In the Privacy Center module, you can use the Cookies Preferences section to build and customize a cookie banner and preference center for your domain(s). Cookie banner and preference center enhance the data subject’s control over data, ensuring personalized online experiences while respecting privacy.

Consent preferences refer to the specific choices data subjects make regarding the collection, processing, and use of their personal information. Data subjects actively decide on data permissions, indicating their agreement or refusal for specific purposes, such as targeted advertising or analytics. Respecting and managing these preferences are vital for organizations to align with privacy regulations and establish transparent, user-centric practices in handling sensitive information.

Privacy notice is a formal statement or document that communicates how the platform collects, processes, stores, and shares user data. It serves as a transparent disclosure of your organization’s privacy practices, detailing the types of information collected, the purposes for which it is used, and the security measures in place to protect the personal data of your data subjects.

A Data Subject Request (DSR), also referred to as a Customer Data Request, is a formal inquiry made by an individual, known as the data subject or customer, under contemporary privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. Depending upon their locations, the data subjects can request access to their data being processed or held by organizations or businesses. The data subjects can request access to their data, ask for data deletion, or request to stop processing their personal data.
