# Understanding the Privacy Center

Privacy Center offers five distinct features to help you cover all areas of data security and compliance.

### Cookie Preferences

Cookies Preferences lets you provide notice and choice to your data subjects regarding the third-party tracking technologies on your domain (website). This feature of your privacy center empowers your data subjects by letting them choose to opt in or opt out.

You can rapidly deploy a cookie banner with reverse IP detection to comply with the global consent requirements. It lets you leverage Securiti’s auto-blocking script to honor consumer preferences without additional coding.

### Individual Data Requests

Individual Data Requests lets your data subjects exercise their privacy rights and take control of their data. This feature of your privacy center reinforces handling of the right to **access**, **delete**, or **correction** of the information and data of your data subjects.

With the Individual Data Requestes you can leverage robotic assistance to automatically handle data subject requests efficiently and in a timely manner. This feature lets you protect your data subjects against identity fraud and personal information (PI) theft through automatic identity verification and by utilizing the built-in regulatory guidance for compliant fulfillment of data subject requests.

### Privacy Notice

You can create and publish a global privacy notice in minutes with the Privacy Notice section. It offers meaningful transparency to your consumers. You can minimize privacy risks by keeping real-time updated privacy notices for your organization.

Privacy Notice lets you dynamically update your notice through integrations with Securiti's Cookie and Universal Consent Management, Data Subject Rights, and Data Mapping Automation modules.

### Consent Preferences

Consent Preferences lets you create and publish a preference center to capture consent from any collection point including websites, forms, mobile apps, and SaaS systems. It lets you experience the scalability of your organization and its performance in a robust manner. Data subjects have the right to provide or withdraw consent to marketing preferences and consent preferences provide granular options to avoid nuclear opt-outs and keep opt-in rates high.

### [](/v1/docs/securiti-pods-and-elastic-pods)Do Not Sell or Share

CCPA requires organizations to display a **Do Not Sell or Share** **My Personal Information** link prominently, with guidance on how to opt-out of the sale of their data. Compliance with CCPA regulations to enhance privacy rights and honor consumer requests is imperative for organizations globally.

This section of the privacy center lets you display an eminent accordion banner in accordance with CCPA. You can toggle the switch on and off to suit your preferences.

Cookies preferences are user settings that dictate how websites use and store data through cookies, providing control over tracking, personalized content, and site functionality. In the Privacy Center module, you can use the Cookies Preferences section to build and customize a cookie banner and preference center for your domain(s). Cookie banner and preference center enhance the data subject’s control over data, ensuring personalized online experiences while respecting privacy.

A Data Subject Request (DSR), also referred to as a Customer Data Request, is a formal inquiry made by an individual, known as the data subject or customer, under contemporary privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. Depending upon their locations, the data subjects can request access to their data being processed or held by organizations or businesses. The data subjects can request access to their data, ask for data deletion, or request to stop processing their personal data.

Privacy notice is a formal statement or document that communicates how the platform collects, processes, stores, and shares user data. It serves as a transparent disclosure of your organization’s privacy practices, detailing the types of information collected, the purposes for which it is used, and the security measures in place to protect the personal data of your data subjects.

Consent preferences refer to the specific choices data subjects make regarding the collection, processing, and use of their personal information. Data subjects actively decide on data permissions, indicating their agreement or refusal for specific purposes, such as targeted advertising or analytics. Respecting and managing these preferences are vital for organizations to align with privacy regulations and establish transparent, user-centric practices in handling sensitive information.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) was the first state-level comprehensive privacy law in the United States. It applies broadly to for-profit businesses that collect personal information from California consumers, creating certain rights for consumers and obligations for businesses in relation to personal data processing. It was amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) in 2020.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) mandates organizations to prominently feature a Do Not Sell My Personal Information link. This link provides guidance on opting out of the sale of data of individuals such as data subjects or website visitors etc. Using Privacy Center, you can enable or disable this option for your data subjects.
