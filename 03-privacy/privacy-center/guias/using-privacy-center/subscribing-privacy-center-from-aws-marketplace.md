# Subscribing Privacy Center from AWS marketplace

Securiti’s Privacy Center module is also available on the [AWS marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-g3keyjg2vmlhm?sr=0-1&amp;ref_=beagle&amp;applicationId=AWSMPContessa). You can subscribe to the module directly from the marketplace using your Amazon account for a hassle-free and quick deployment of the Privacy Center. All payments for the Privacy Center module subscriptions are processed securely through Amazon's trusted payment infrastructure.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> AWS marketplace also manages all aspects of Privacy Center subscription **renewals**, **upgrades**, **downgrades**, and **cancellations**.

To subscribe the Privacy Center module from the AWS marketplace:

1. Log in to your Amazon account.
2. If you don't have an Amazon account, sign up by creating a new user account.
3. Navigate to this [AWS marketplace link](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-g3keyjg2vmlhm?sr=0-1&amp;ref_=beagle&amp;applicationId=AWSMPContessa).
4. From the upper right corner of the page, click **View purchase options** as shown below.

![Overview of PrivacyCenter.cloud features for automated compliance and cookie consent management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/aws_privacy_center.png)
5. Review the available subscription options to determine which best suits your business needs, then purchase the Privacy Center under your chosen subscription.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Once Amazon successfully processes your payment, you receive a link via email that redirects you Securiti's [public website](https://securiti.ai/privacy-center/) where you can register for the Privacy Center.
6. If you opt for **Basic** or **Pro** version, once your purchase is complete, Amazon redirects you to Securiti's public website where you must fill out a registration form, and click **Create Privacy Center** as shown below.

![AWS_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-58a962b5-04d3-fd8e-20b0-58f60744d13f.png)
  1. Once done, you receive a verification email. Access your email, and verify the email. This action redirects you to Securiti platform as a logged in user.

![AWS_3.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e4afd242-8fa2-efe4-dd15-9f09b468ed87.png)
  2. Accept Securiti's **Terms and Condition** to proceed.
  3. You can then create your Privacy Center as explained in [Build your Privacy Center in a day](/v1/docs/build-your-privacy-center-in-a-day).
7. If you opt for the **Business** version, once your purchase is complete, Amazon redirects you to Securiti's public website where you must fill out a registration form, and click **Create Privacy Center** as shown below.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If you opt for the **Business** version of Privacy Center, we don't require verification via email from you.

![AWS_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-58a962b5-04d3-fd8e-20b0-58f60744d13f.png)
8. Once done, you receive a confirmation email including a link to set up a password for your new Securiti account.

Click the link in the email, and it instantly redirects you to Securiti platform.
  1. Set up the password for your Securiti tenant, and log in to Securiti platform.
  2. Accept Securiti's **Terms and Condition** to proceed.
  3. You can then create your Privacy Center as explained in [Build your Privacy Center in a day](/v1/docs/build-your-privacy-center-in-a-day).
