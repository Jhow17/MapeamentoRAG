# Managing your Privacy Center subscriptions

You can upgrade or downgrade your Securiti tenant in the following ways:

- Free to Pro version
- Pro to Business version
- Business to Enterprise version

You can also manage your subscriptions from the main dashboard. To upgrade your Privacy Center:

1. Click **Manage Subscription** on the upper right corner of the main dashboard of the module as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-03-09_at_12_25_58_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-49f91087-ac0f-92ac-e2b8-1a32ffe86c40.png)
2. On the **Manage Subscription** page select the most suitable version for your organization and follow the instructions to upgrade to the new version.

![Screenshot_2023-03-09_at_12_50_43_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d8b2556b-9339-e375-b3ed-f13aab743ad0.png)

To cancel your Privacy Center subscription:

1. Click **Manage Subscription** on the upper right corner of the main dashboard as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-03-09_at_12_25_58_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-49f91087-ac0f-92ac-e2b8-1a32ffe86c40.png)
2. On the **Manage Subscription** page, click **Cancel Subscription** to cancel your Privacy Center subscription.

![Screenshot_2023-03-09_at_12_26_40_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-da02d889-1fff-a258-ed63-e1035fe6a76d.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> You can always prevent your Securiti tenant from being permanently deleted even after canceling the subscription at any time by contacting your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative.

After canceling your subscription for the trial period, you can still use your Privacy Center for the remaining days of the trial period. Securiti deletes your tenant permanently after the trial period ends.

For the paid versions of Privacy Center such as **Pro** or **Business** tiers, we offer you a grace period of additional **30 days** from the day you cancel your subscription.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti sends out an email immediately after you cancel your subscription to let you know that your subscription is canceled. We also send you warning emails as the time of your tenant termination approaches to let you know about the permanent termination of your Securiti tenant.
