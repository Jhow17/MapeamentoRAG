# Building multiple Privacy Centers

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you often manage privacy requirements across multiple brands or regions, each with different regulatory needs and data subject expectations. Using a single Privacy Center can make it difficult to tailor notices, language, and preferences appropriately. For instance, you may need one Privacy Center for your EU customers with GDPR-specific disclosures and another for your U.S. audience with different consent requirements. With Securiti’s Privacy Center in the Business or Enterprise tier, you can create multiple customized Privacy Centers, letting you address regional or brand-specific needs while maintaining clarity, consistency, and control across all customer experiences.

Securiti lets you create multiple Privacy Centers for your Securiti tenant as shown below. Only **Enterprise** and **Business** tier versions of the Privacy Center module support this functionality.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If this feature is not available in your Securiti tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan.

You can also create a Privacy Center in any existing organizational unit of your Securiti tenant. From the bottom left corner of the page, hover over your profile avatar and select a different organization from the list of organizations as shown below. Now, create a new Privacy Center specifically for the selected organization after switching to it.

![Screenshot_2023-08-04_at_2_40_11_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-33cbc25d-f27e-19cc-5672-45e6ead0f505.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> Securiti displays data only to the users who have access to that particular organization in which you created a Privacy Center.

### Creating a new Privacy Center

To create a new Privacy Center:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Center and click **+ New Privacy Center** on the upper right side of the page.
2. Fill out the Privacy Center registration form with appropriate details as shown below.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> When creating a new Privacy Center with a domain that you have already scanned in the Cookie Consent Management module, Securiti automatically links that domain and all its associated configurations with the new Privacy Center that you are creating. This ensures a swift and seamless setup, maintaining the continuity of your domain settings when setting up a new Privacy Center.

![Screenshot_2023-07-13_at_10_10_10_AM_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-42cd6e47-456e-4022-e632-acc7fd4c95fe.png)
3. Click **Submit** to create a new Privacy Center.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> Securiti only lets you associate one domain with a Privacy Center. You must use a different domain during the creation of another Privacy Center in the registration form.

### Deleting Privacy Centers

You can also delete your Privacy Center(s) along with all associated entities such as your organization’s **Privacy Notice**, **Cookies Preferences**, **Universal Preferences** and **Individual Data Requests** when you delete your Privacy Center.

To delete your Privacy Center:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Center, click **Options** (three dots) on the right side of the Privacy Center you want to delete and select **Delete**.

![Screenshot_2023-02-27_at_3_20_06_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-db04cbbc-dde4-6731-d9d8-9f59701a54b6.png)
2. Click **Yes, continue** on the confirmation popup as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-02-27_at_3_24_12_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4034cc59-8e83-c193-d160-a621b53f5f3a.png)

You can also delete your Privacy Center by clicking **Delete** on the upper right side of the Privacy Center’s main page. On the confirmation popup click **Yes, continue** to delete your Privacy Center as shown below.

Privacy notice is a formal statement or document that communicates how the platform collects, processes, stores, and shares user data. It serves as a transparent disclosure of your organization’s privacy practices, detailing the types of information collected, the purposes for which it is used, and the security measures in place to protect the personal data of your data subjects.

Cookies preferences are user settings that dictate how websites use and store data through cookies, providing control over tracking, personalized content, and site functionality. In the Privacy Center module, you can use the Cookies Preferences section to build and customize a cookie banner and preference center for your domain(s). Cookie banner and preference center enhance the data subject’s control over data, ensuring personalized online experiences while respecting privacy.

A Data Subject Request (DSR), also referred to as a Customer Data Request, is a formal inquiry made by an individual, known as the data subject or customer, under contemporary privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. Depending upon their locations, the data subjects can request access to their data being processed or held by organizations or businesses. The data subjects can request access to their data, ask for data deletion, or request to stop processing their personal data.
