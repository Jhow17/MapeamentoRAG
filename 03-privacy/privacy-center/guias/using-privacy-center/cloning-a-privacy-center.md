# Cloning a Privacy Center

Securiti lets you clone your existing Privacy Center(s) within the same organizational unit. All existing metadata of your Privacy Center such as **selected language** and **module tabs** are cloned with the same existing configurations.

With this functionality, you can quickly and seamlessly create a duplicate Privacy Center for either your different domain or subdomain.

To clone your Privacy Center:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Center.
2. On the Privacy Center dashboard, from the **Privacy Center links** column, click the **clone** icon as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-10-02_at_5_38_28_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4349109d-6aab-34d9-78ff-e36edcfb8033.png)
3. Once you click clone, Securiti redirects you to the Privacy Center registration page.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> All text fields on the registration form are pre-filled for you, except the **Website URL** field. This is because you cannot create a new Privacy Center with an existing domain being used with another Privacy Center.
4. In the **Website URL** field, enter the address of your new domain with which you want to associate your Privacy Center, and near the bottom of the registration form, click **Submit** as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-10-02_at_5_46_10_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1cb9d8dd-4189-81bb-1403-43c05618809f.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> You can update all of the pre-filled fields on the Privacy Center registration form except the **Language** option upon cloning the Privacy Center. Your cloned Privacy Center is always created in the same language as the reference Privacy Center.
5. Securiti creates a duplicate Privacy Center with a name “Copy of <existing Privacy Center name>” in the dashboard as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with status and customization options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/clonning.png)
6. Click the **edit** (pencil icon) adjacent to the name of your cloned Privacy Center to rename it.
7. Make adjustment to your Privacy Center module tabs and publish your Privacy Center.
