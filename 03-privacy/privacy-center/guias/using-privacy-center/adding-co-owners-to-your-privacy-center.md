# Adding co-owners to your Privacy Center

Securiti lets you add co-owners to your Privacy Centers for better collaboration. You can add co-owners to both new and existing Privacy Centers. Only **Super Admins** and **existing Privacy Center owners** can add new co-owners to a Privacy Center. You can make the following Securiti user role as a co-owner:

- Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Securiti lets you add co-owners from the same organization. For instance, users from Organization A can be co-owners of a Privacy Center belonging to Organization A. Securiti does not let you add co-owners between different organizations.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> When you add users as co-owners to your Privacy Center, Securiti also makes them an owner in the associated Privacy Notice and DSR form. However, to remove co-owners from these modules, you must first remove them from the Privacy Center.

Securiti also lets you promptly add new users as co-owners. To do so:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Center.
2. On the Privacy Center dashboard, click the Privacy Center column to which you want to add a co-owner.
3. In your Privacy Center, near the upper middle Co-Owners section, click **Edit** (pencil icon), and from the menu, search or select the user, as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with options for adding co-owners.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/add_coowner_1.png)

![Dashboard view of a Privacy Center with user management options and data requests.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/add_coowner_2.png)
4. To add a new user directly from the Co-Owners section, near the bottom of the menu, click **+ Add new users**, as shown below.

![Dashboard view of Privacy Center with options to customize user settings and preferences.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/add_coowner_3.png)
5. On the **Add New Users** popup, enter the **first name**, **last name**, and **email** of the user in the corresponding fields. From last menu, select **DPO** role, as shown below.

![User addition form showing fields for first name, last name, email, and role selection.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/add_coowner_4.png)
6. Near the bottom of the popup, click **Save**, as shown below.

![User addition form in Privacy Center with fields for name, email, and role.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/add_coowner_5.png)

Securiti instantly adds the new user as a co-owner, and shows a success message near the upper right corner of the page.

A key role within your organization responsible for overseeing and ensuring compliance with data protection laws and regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union. The primary responsibility of the DPO is to monitor the organization's data processing activities, assess potential risks to data subjects' privacy rights, and implement measures to mitigate those risks.
