# Customizing Privacy Centers in bulk

Securiti lets you customize privacy centers in bulk, maintaining consistent branding and reducing manual effort and saving time.

### Styling privacy centers in bulks

To apply style in bulk:

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Center**, click on **Bulk Actions** to the right of the search bar, and choose **Configure Style** from the menu, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(179).png)
2. Mark the checkboxes to select the privacy centers you want to customize, then click **Configure Style** near bottom right of the search bar, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(182).png)
3. You can update the colors, fonts, and button shapes from the **Universal Page Styling** popup, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(183).png)
4. After making changes, click **Save Changes** to apply them across all selected privacy centers.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The Universal Page Styling popup always opens with default styling options due to multiple selections and varying style configurations.

### Reverting privacy center styling in bulk

To remove the custom styling from privacy centers in bulk, and set it to back to default:

1. In the **Privacy Center,** click on **Bulk Actions** to the right of the search bar, and choose **Configure Style** from the menu.
2. Mark the checkboxes to select the privacy centers you want to revert to original settings, then click **Configure Style** near bottom right of the search bar.
3. From the **Universal Page Styling** popup, click **Reset to default**, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(184).png)
4. Click **Save Changes** to apply them across all the selected privacy centers.
