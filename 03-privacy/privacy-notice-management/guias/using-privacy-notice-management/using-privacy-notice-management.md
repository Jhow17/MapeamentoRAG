# Using Privacy Notice Management

The Securiti **Privacy Notice Management** module lets you quickly create privacy notices for your website. These custom notices comply with applicable regulations for your location.

To create a new privacy notice using a template, wizard or an existing file such as PDF, DOCX, or TXT:

- See [Creating a new privacy notice](/v1/docs/creating-a-new-privacy-notice).

To open an existing privacy notice and view or edit it:

- On the **Privacy Notices** dashboard, click on the card for the privacy notice.

To filter the Privacy Notices:

- Near the upper left corner of the **Privacy Notices** dashboard, click **+** (plus icon) and use the tools to filter the privacy notices by the available criteria. See [Using top-level filters](/latest/docs/using-top-level-filters).

To delete an existing privacy notice:

- On the **Privacy Notices** dashboard, find the card for the privacy notice and at its right edge choose **Options** (three dots) and then **Delete Notice**.

To preview the currently published version of the privacy notice:

- Open the privacy notice for viewing or editing, then on the header at the top of the page, click **Preview** and then **Preview** **Published Version** from the menu.

To preview the version of the privacy notice in its current state while you are working on it:

- Near the upper right corner of the notice editor, click **Preview** and choose **Preview Draft** from the menu.

To publish the privacy notice:

- Near the upper right corner of the notice editor, next to **Preview**, click **Save & Publish,** and then **Publish Notice**.

To create a privacy notice template:

- See [Creating and managing privacy notice templates](/latest/docs/creating-and-managing-privacy-notice-templates-3).
