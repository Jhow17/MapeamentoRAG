# Assigning custom metadata

You can assign custom metadata to privacy notices and templates based on your business requirements using the following methods:

- Through the **Custom Metadata** dashboard
- Through the **Privacy Notices** or **Templates** dashboards
- Through the privacy notice or template editors

## Assigning custom metadata through the Custom Metadata dashboard

To assign custom metadata to privacy notices or templates from the **Custom Metadata** dashboard:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Custom Metadata**.
2. Next to the custom metadata name in the custom metadata list, mark the checkbox to select it, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing selected items and their details for assignment.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assigning_custom_metadata_1.png)
3. You can also select custom metadata in bulk:
  1. Near the upper left corner of the custom metadata list, next to the **Name** column, click the checkbox, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface displaying required and optional fields for data entry.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assigning_custom_metadata_2.png)
  2. From the menu, choose either **Select all across all pages**, or **Select all on this page**, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing options for selecting metadata across pages.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assigning_custom_metadata_3.png)

Choose **Select None** to deselect all.
4. Near the upper left corner of the custom metadata list, from the custom metadata action bar, click **Assign**, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing selected items and assignment options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assigning_custom_metadata_4.png)
5. From the **Assign Custom Metadata** popup, select one of the following:

| Selection type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| All Notices & Templates | Lets you assign selected custom metadata to all new privacy notices and templates. |
| Custom Selection | Lets you assign custom metadata to selected privacy notices and templates. |
6. Select the **All Notices & Templates** card, and near the bottom of the **Assign Custom Metadata** popup, click **Assign**.
7. Or select the **Custom Selection** card, and near the bottom of the **Assign Custom Metadata** popup, search privacy notices or templates.
  1. Click the search box, and from the menu, mark the checkboxes next to the privacy notices or templates you want to include, as shown below.

![Assign custom metadata fields to selected notices and templates in the management interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assign_custom_metadata_notice_templates.png)

Click the **Notices** or **Templates** tab at the top of the menu to switch between the notice and template lists, as shown below.

![Assign custom metadata to selected notices and templates in the management interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assign_custom_metadata_notice_templates_switch_tabs.png)
  2. After the selecting the notices and templates, near the bottom of the **Assign Custom Metadata** popup, click **Assign**, as shown below.

![Assign custom metadata fields to selected notices and templates in the workspace interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assigning_custom_metadata_5.png)
8. Near the far right of the custom metadata row, click **Options** (three dots). From the menu, select **View Details** to view the custom metadata details, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing required and optional fields for data entry.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/view_details_custom_metadata.png)

The **View Details** popup displays information about the custom metadata, including the **Owner**, **Data Type**, **Requirement**, and **Organization** details. It also lists the linked privacy notices and templates, as shown below.

![Details of a custom metadata entry including name, data type, and organization information.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/view_details_custom_metadata_2.png)

### Auto-assigning custom metadata

Auto-Assign automatically assigns the selected custom metadata field to all newly created notices and templates.

To auto-assign custom metadata to all new privacy notices and templates:

1. Next to the custom metadata name in the custom metadata list on the dashboard, mark the checkbox to select it.
2. To select custom metadata in bulk:
  1. Near the upper left corner of the custom metadata list, next to the **Name** column, click the checkbox, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing required and optional fields for data entry.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/assign_custom_metadata_notice_templates_select.png)
  2. From the menu, choose either **Select all across all pages**, or **Select all on this page**.
3. Near the top of the custom metadata list, from the custom metadata action bar, click **Enable Auto-Assign**, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing options for auto-assign and required fields.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/auto_assigning_1.png)
4. From the **Update Auto-Assign for Custom Metadata Fields** popup, click **Enable Auto-Assign** to auto assign the custom metadata, as shown below.

![Popup message for enabling Auto-Assign on custom metadata fields in a management interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/auto_assigning_2.png)

The **Auto-Assign** column in the custom metadata list shows the auto-assign indicator for each custom metadata field, as shown below.

![Custom metadata settings with required fields and options for data types displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/auto_assigning_4.png)
5. To disable the auto-assignment, select custom metadata from the custom metadata list, and from the custom metadata action bar, click **Disable Auto-Assign**, as shown below.

![Custom metadata management interface showing selected items and options for assignment.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/auto_assigning_5.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The **Disable Auto-Assign** option in the custom metadata action bar appears only when you select one or more auto-assigned custom metadata fields.

## Assigning custom metadata through the Privacy Notices or Templates dashboards

You can also assign custom metadata to individual or multiple privacy notices and templates through the **Privacy Notices** or **Templates** dashboards.

To apply custom metadata to your privacy notices or templates:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Privacy Notices** or **Dashboards > Templates**.
2. Next to the privacy notice or template name, mark the checkbox to select it, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying published notices and their details for management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_1.png)

You can also select multiple privacy notices or templates.
3. To select privacy notices or templates in bulk:
  1. Near the upper left corner of the custom metadata list, next to the **Name** column, click the checkbox.
  2. From the menu, choose either **Select all across all pages**, or **Select all on this page**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying published notices and selection options for management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_2.png)
4. Near the top of the privacy notices or templates list, from the action bar, click **Apply Custom Metadata**, as shown below.![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options for editing and applying metadata.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_3.png)
5. From the **Apply Custom Metadata** popup, search for custom metadata, and mark the corresponding checkboxes to select them, as shown below.

![Custom metadata fields for privacy notice management with selected options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_4.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The **Apply Custom Metadata** popup does not show existing custom metadata.
6. To add or update the value of the custom metadata, click the textbox, as shown below.

![Applying custom metadata fields for privacy notice management in a dashboard interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_5.png)
7. To overwrite the existing custom metadata values on the selected privacy notices or templates, mark the **Overwrite existing custom metadata in the current scope** checkbox.

This replaces the existing values with the new values you are assigning, as shown below.

![Custom metadata fields for privacy notice management with options to apply changes.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_6.png)

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> By default, Securiti merges newly applied custom metadata with the existing metadata on the selected notices and templates. It retains existing values and adds new custom metadata fields. To replace existing values with new ones, mark the **Overwrite existing custom metadata in the current scope** checkbox.
8. Near the bottom of the **Apply Custom Metadata** popup, click **Apply**.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> When you click **Apply**, Securiti prompts you to confirm that you want to proceed with the new values for the selected custom metadata fields.
> 
> ![Prompt to overwrite existing metadata in a privacy notice management system.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_7.png)

## Assigning custom metadata through the privacy notice or template editors

You can assign custom metadata to an individual privacy notice or template through the respective editor, making it more convenient to add and manage metadata information.

To apply custom metadata to an individual privacy notice or template:

1. Open any privacy notice or template or create a new one.
2. Near the upper left corner of the notice or template editor, click **More** (three dots icon), and from the menu, select **Custom Metadata**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying Privacy Notice sections and options for managing data collection.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_1.png)

An **Apply Custom Metadata** popup appears. It shows the assigned custom metadata.
3. From the **Apply Custom Metadata** popup, search for custom metadata, and mark the corresponding checkboxes to select them, as shown below.

![Custom metadata fields for privacy notice management with selected options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_4.png)

You can also provide value for custom metadata or modify the existing value.
4. To add or update the value of the custom metadata, click the textbox, as shown below.

![Applying custom metadata fields for privacy notice management in a dashboard interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_bulk_5.png)
5. After adding the desired metadata, near the bottom of the popup, click **Save** to successfully assign the custom metadata.

You can view the assigned custom metadata from the **Details** card, as shown below.

![Privacy notice document outlining information collection and usage details for users.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/apply_custom_metadata_2(1).png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti does not let you publish privacy notices if the **required** custom metadata fields have missing values.
> 
> To add the missing custom metadata details, near the top right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish** and then click **Missing Custom Metadata**, as shown below.
> 
> ![Privacy notice dashboard showing sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/missing_custom_metatdata_1(3).png)
> 
> Enter a value for each required custom metadata field.
