# Creating a new privacy notice

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you need to publish updated privacy notices for different business units and regions. Creating each notice manually from scratch is time-consuming and often leads to inconsistent language and outdated layouts. For instance, when your marketing team launches a new customer data collection campaign, you must quickly create a region-specific notice that aligns with existing policies. In Securiti, you can duplicate an existing notice, upload a draft PDF, or use the guided wizard to build the new notice faster. This helps you standardize content, reduce rework, and publish accurate privacy notices with less effort and risk.

Securiti provides four methods to create privacy notices tailored to organizational requirements, including templates, the guided wizard, a blank notice, or uploaded files. You can also duplicate an existing notice to create a new privacy notice, providing flexibility in selecting the preferred approach and layout.

To create a privacy notice in Securiti:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and near the upper right corner of the **Privacy Notices** dashboard, click **+ New Notice,** as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to filter and add new notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_1.png)
2. Select the appropriate method from the following four options, and start creating a new privacy notice in Securiti:
  1. [Creating a privacy notice using a template](/latest/docs/creating-a-privacy-notice-using-a-template)
  2. [Creating a privacy notice using a wizard](/latest/docs/creating-a-privacy-using-a-wizard)
  3. [Creating a privacy notice from a blank notice](/latest/docs/creating-a-privacy-notice-from-a-blank-notice)
  4. [Creating a privacy notice using PDF, DOCX, or TXT files](/latest/docs/creating-a-privacy-notice-using-pdf-docx-or-text-files)

Or create a new privacy notice by duplicating an existing one and then editing it to suit. To create a new privacy notice using the existing notice:

1. On the Privacy Notices dashboard, next to the existing privacy notice in the notice list view, click **Options** (three dots) and then from the menu, select **Duplicate**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to edit, duplicate, and manage statuses.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_new_notice_1.png)
2. From the **Duplicate Notice** popup, select the organization, and click **Duplicate Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to duplicate and manage them.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_new_notice_2.png)

To filter privacy notices and narrow the results, see [Using top-level filters](/latest/docs/using-top-level-filters).
