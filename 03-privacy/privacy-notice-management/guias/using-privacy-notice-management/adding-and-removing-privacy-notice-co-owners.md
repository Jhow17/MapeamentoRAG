# Adding and removing privacy notice co-owners

**Scenario**

As a Data Protection Officer, you are responsible for keeping privacy notices current across regions. When updates are needed, relying on a single owner can delay reviews and approvals. With Securiti, you add multiple co-owners, like module owners and operators, to share responsibility for each privacy notice. This speeds up collaboration, ensures privacy notices stay accurate, and reduces bottlenecks during updates.

You can add multiple co-owners to a privacy notice. Based on your role, you can also add yourself as a co-owner, and remove other co-owners from the privacy notice. You can make the following Securiti user roles co-owners:

- Data Protection Officer (DPO)
- MODULE OWNER
- OPERATOR
- ORGANIZATION ADMIN
- SUPER ADMIN
- USER
- USER Cloned User Profile ROLE

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Users with the following roles can add themselves as co-owners and remove existing co-owners:
> 
> - Super Admin
> - Data Protection Officer
> - Operator

## Adding co-owners

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Notice** **Management.**
2. On the privacy notices page, click a privacy notice.
3. In the **Co-owners** section near the upper right corner, click **Edit** (pencil icon), and from the menu, search or select the user, as shown below.

![Dashboard view of PDPL Privacy Notice with highlighted data processing activities section.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_1(1).png)

![Dashboard displaying data processing activities and user information collection options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_2.png)
4. To add a new user directly from the **Co-owners** section, near the bottom of the menu, click **+ Add new users**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying data processing activities and user information collection options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_3.png)
5. On the **Add New Users** popup, enter the **F****irst Name**, **L****ast Name**, and **E****mail** of the user in the corresponding fields. From the last menu, select **Role** that you wish to assign to the co-owner, as shown below.

![User addition form showing fields for name, email, and role selection options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_4.png)
6. Near the bottom of the popup, click **Save**, as shown below.

![User addition form displaying fields for first name, last name, email, and role.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_5.png)

## Removing co-owners

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Notice** **Management.**
2. On the privacy notices page, click a privacy notice.
3. In the **Co-owners** section near the upper right corner, click **Edit** (pencil icon) to display co-owners list. Next, click **Delete** (cross icon) next to co-owner’s name to remove the co-owner(s) from the list.

![Dashboard displaying data processing activities and user information collection options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/adding_removing_privacy_notice_co_owners_6.png)

A key role within your organization responsible for overseeing and ensuring compliance with data protection laws and regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union. The primary responsibility of the DPO is to monitor the organization's data processing activities, assess potential risks to data subjects' privacy rights, and implement measures to mitigate those risks.

Co-owner(s) in the Privacy Notice Management module refers to stakeholder(s) who share ownership or administrative privileges for creating and updating the privacy notice(s) of your organization. Co-owners have the same permissions and responsibilities as the primary owner in the Privacy Notice Management module.
