# Configuring the AI privacy notice template

The AI template for privacy notices helps organizations inform data subjects about the use of AI tools and essential aspects, such as the organization’s role in providing the AI system (as a provider or deployer), AI technology details, potential impacts of the AI system, and risk mitigation measures implemented by the organization. This structured approach ensures clear communication with data subjects about how the organization uses AI, supporting compliance and transparency in AI deployment.

Securiti has provided default text in multiple sections of the AI notice template to help you comply with the EU AI Act. You can review the default language provided by Securiti and customize it to suit your business needs. You can create multiple AI systems depending on the number of AI systems that your business supports.

Securiti also lets you:

- Add custom sections in the AI template.
- Change section names to suit.
- Hide sections so they don’t appear when you publish your AI privacy notice, as shown below.

![Menu options for managing AI Notice Template, including Hide Section and Delete features.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_1.png)

In the **Form** view, use the table below to configure the AI notice template sections:

| Section | Description |
| --- | --- |
| AI System Name | Rename the title here to capture the specific name or title of the AI system for clear reference and documentation, as shown below. ![AI Notice Template showing contact details and roles for ChatGPT and OpenAI.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_2.png) |
| Role in Provision of AI System | Describe your role in providing the AI system and include the provider's contact details for reference, as shown below. ![AI Notice Template showing roles and contact details for AI system providers.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_3.png) |
| Description of AI and technology | Provide an overview of the AI system’s use cases, data specifications, training objectives, and technical capabilities, including output details and methods for achieving results, as shown below. ![AI Notice Template detailing AI technology, use cases, and data collection criteria.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_4.png) Mark the **Yes** radio button if the AI system in order to communicate to the data subjects if you are involved in one of the following: Mark the **Yes** radio button if the AI system: - Interacts with natural persons. - Produces synthetic/artificial content. - Recognizes emotions and/or categorizes biometric information. ![AI notice template with questions about system interactions and data handling practices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_5.png) |
| AI Impact and Risk Mitigation | You can inform the end user on the potential impacts of the AI system on user well-being, measures for bias mitigation and data security, performance limitations, and safeguards against harmful errors, as shown below. ![AI notice template outlining health impacts, safety guidelines, and data collection measures.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_6.png) |
| Data Security Measures | Detail the data security measures in place, addressing potential risks to accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, and ongoing maintenance of the AI system, as shown below. ![Overview of data security measures and AI tool maintenance instructions in a document.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_7.png) |
| Instructions of Use | Provide clear usage instructions for operating and interacting with the AI system, as shown below. ![Instructions for using AI tools and ensuring human oversight in decision-making processes.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_8.png) |
| Human oversight | Describe the level of human oversight in AI outputs and detail mechanisms for data subjects to exercise rights or provide feedback on AI decisions, as shown below. ![Instructions for AI usage and human oversight in decision-making processes outlined in the document.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/ai_privacy_notice_template_9.png) |
