# Enabling tiled layout for a privacy notice

Securiti lets you display privacy notices as tiles, providing a structured way to present content and improve navigation. You can also upload custom icons for each tile to align with organizational branding and enhance visual representation.

To enable tiled view for your privacy notice:

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Notice** **Management**. Create a new privacy notice or open an existing one for editing.
2. Near the upper-right corner, next to the **Preview**, click the **More** and then choose **Settings** from the menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying company information and privacy notice settings for PNM.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/enabling_tiled_entry_page_1.png)
3. In the **General** tab of **Notice Settings**, mark the **Enable thumbnail view for sections** checkbox, as shown below.

![Settings menu showing options for enabling thumbnail view and collapsible sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/thumbnail_view.png)

Scroll down and hover over a tile and click **Change Icon** to upload the custom icon, as shown below.

![Settings menu showing options for managing data processing and privacy settings.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/thumbnail_view_2.png)

You can reset an uploaded icon at any time. Hover over the tile and click **Reset**, as shown below.

![Settings interface showing options for managing languages and data processing activities.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/thumbnail_view_3.png)
4. Click **Save Changes** to save your settings, as shown below.

![Settings page for customizing privacy notice and layout preferences with options listed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/enabling_tiled_entry_page_3.png)

This adds **Card** and **List** view options at the top of the privacy notice, as shown below.

![Overview of privacy statement sections including data sharing and security information.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/enabling_tiled_entry_page_4.png)
