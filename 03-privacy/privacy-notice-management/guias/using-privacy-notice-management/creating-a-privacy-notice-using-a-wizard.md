# Creating a privacy notice using a wizard

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you must ensure that your organization publishes privacy notices that comply with different regional regulations while keeping the information clear and consistent for data subjects and site visitors. However, managing separate privacy notices for multiple jurisdictions can be complex and time consuming, especially when regulations such as **PDPL** in the **UAE**, **LGPD** in Brazil, or global privacy standards require specific disclosures with different legal verbiage. For instance, if your organization operates in both the UAE and Brazil, each region requires different legal language and transparency requirements in the privacy notice. Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management lets you use prebuilt templates and customize them to meet regional regulatory needs. This helps you maintain compliant, consistent, and up-to-date privacy notices across regions while reducing manual effort and compliance risk.

You can create privacy notices using a guided wizard-based approach. Securiti provides LDPD, PDPL, Global, and AI wizards that simplify privacy notice creation through structured steps and compliance-focused guidance.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an European Union (EU) regulation on information privacy. It aims to ensure the protection of personal information through a human rights-centric approach, and imposes obligations onto organizations anywhere, so long as they target or collect data related to people in the EU.

The Data Privacy Framework (DPF) constitutes the EU-U.S. DPF, the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF, and the Swiss-U.S. DPF. These frameworks provide U.S. organizations with reliable mechanisms for personal data transfers to the United States from the European Union, United Kingdom, and Switzerland while ensuring compliance with EU, UK, and Swiss data protection laws.

The Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA) along with the UK GDPR constitutes the legislative framework applicable to processing of personal data in the UK. The DPA controls how personal data of individuals is used by organizations, businesses or the government.

Data retention is the practice of storing and managing data for a specific period before either deleting or archiving the data for future. The data retention period may be determined by legal, regulatory, or business requirements. Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management module lets you inform the data subject of your organization's data retention policy.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) was the first state-level comprehensive privacy law in the United States. It applies broadly to for-profit businesses that collect personal information from California consumers, creating certain rights for consumers and obligations for businesses in relation to personal data processing. It was amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) in 2020.

The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) is the Canadian federal privacy law for private organizations. It sets out the ground rules for how businesses must handle personal information in the course of their commercial activity and provides the individuals with the right to access and rectify their personal data. Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management module lets you create a PIPEDA compliant privacy notice(s) for your organization.

In the Privacy Notice Management module settings, you can include details about embeddable domains. Here, you can enter the list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice(s).
