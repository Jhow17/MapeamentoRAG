# Using Messages

The **Messages** feature in Privacy Notice Management lets you communicate and collaborate with internal stakeholders directly within the notice editor. You can use it to ask for feedback, tag reviewers, and track updates while working on privacy notices.

To access and use Messages:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Notice Management and either create a new privacy notice or open an existing one.
2. Open **Messages** using one of the following options:
  1. From the lower left corner of the page, above the Disclaimer, click Messages, as shown below.

![Overview of AI system usage and data privacy in language models.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_1.png)
  2. From the upper right corner, click **More** (three dots) next to **Save & Publish**, then from the menu, select **Messages**, as shown below.

![Dashboard view of privacy notice management with highlighted messages option.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_2.png)
3. To tag an approver, co-owner, or reviewer, type @ followed by the stakeholder’s name, then type your message in the message box, as shown below.

![Dashboard view for managing privacy notices with user interaction options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_3.png)

Press Enter or click the arrow button in the message box to send your message, as shown below.

![Dashboard view for managing privacy notices with options to edit and publish.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_4.png)
4. To edit the message, click **Options** (three dots) right next to the message and select **Edit** from the menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard view showing privacy notice management with comments and editing options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_5.png)

Click **Save** after editing the message, as shown below.

![Dashboard view showing a privacy notice with user comments and actions.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_6.png)
5. To delete a message, click **Options** (three dots) next to the message and select **Delete**.

Messages also display system-generated updates, such as when you add a co-owner or approver, or send a notice for approval, as shown below.

![Dashboard showing privacy notice awaiting approval and messages from users.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/pnm_messages_7.png)
