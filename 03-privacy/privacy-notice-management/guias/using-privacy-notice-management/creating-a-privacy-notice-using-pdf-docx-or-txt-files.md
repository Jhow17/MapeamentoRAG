# Creating a privacy notice using PDF, DOCX, or TXT files

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy or Data Compliance Officer, you are responsible for setting up and managing your organization’s privacy notices in a centralized system. When using Privacy Notice Management for the first time, you may already have existing privacy notices stored in different PDF, DOCX, and TXT files across departments. **Import From File** feature within Privacy Notice Management lets you to bulk import multiple privacy notices at once, even when they exist in different formats and structures, helping you migrate notices faster, reduce manual effort, and manage all privacy notices from one centralized location. For instance, you can upload an employee privacy notice in DOCX format, a customer privacy notice in PDF format, and a vendor privacy notice in TXT format together instead of creating each notice manually.

You can migrate your existing privacy notice files to Privacy Notice Management by importing supported file formats, including PDF, DOCX, and TXT. After importing the files, you can manage and customize the notice content within a centralized workspace.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If your website embeds Securiti's privacy notice using an `iframe`, you can dynamically adjust the `iframe` height on the parent page using the following script:
> 
> ```javascript
> const iframe = document.getElementById('iframe_id');
> window.addEventListener("message", function(event) {
> 	if(iframe && event && event.data && event.data.type === "IFRAME_CONTENT_SIZE" ) {
> 		iframe.style.height = event.data.payload.height + 'px';
> 	}
> });
> ```
> 
> In the parent page script, you must replace the `iframe_id` with the ID of your embedded `iframe`.
> 
> The script on the parent page sets the embedded `iframe` ‘s height using the content height that Securiti’s `iframe` sends, ensuring a scroll-free experience.

## Configuring privacy notice settings

You can use general settings to manage the notice header title, allowed domains, and the display layout of the privacy notice.

To configure the general settings:

1. From the upper right corner to the page, click **More**, and from the menu, select **Settings**.
2. From the **General** tab, configure the following settings:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy Notice Header Title | Enter the title that appears at the top of the privacy notice. |
| Embeddable Domains | To build a list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice, must type the full URLs of domains with prefixes such as `https://`, `http://`, and `www`, etc. to add to the list, and press **Enter**, as shown below: - Acceptable domain URLs: - `https://www.example.com` - `http://www.example.com` - `www.example.com` - Unacceptable domain URLs: - `acme.com` - `example.com` - `social.com` ![Settings page for customizing privacy notice and embeddable domains with options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_1(2).png) |
| Notice View | Customize the layout and display settings of the privacy notice page through the following options: - **Enable collapsible mode​​** **for sections** displays the privacy notice in an accordion-style layout, making navigation easier. - **Hide table of content for mobile view** hides the table of contents on mobile for a cleaner, more focused experience. - **Hide table of content for desktop view** hides the table of contents on desktop. - **Enable thumbnail view for sections** to enable a tiled layout, making content easier to browse. ![Settings for privacy notice customization including collapsible sections and content visibility options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_2(2).png) |
3. To reset the notice settings to the default module-level settings, near the bottom left corner of the **Notice Settings**, click **Reset to Default**, as shown below.

![Settings page for data protection notice with options for collapsible sections and visibility.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_1.png)
4. From the **Reset to** **App Settings** popup, click **Reset Settings**, as shown below.

![Settings menu displaying options for privacy notice and app settings reset.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_2.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti applies only the **Color & Styling** changes when resetting the notice settings to the default module-level settings. Update the following settings manually if needed:
> 
> - Added languages
> - Font selections
> - Translation sources

To configure the default settings, see [Customizing privacy notices](/latest/docs/customizing-your-privacy-notices).

In the Privacy Notice Management module settings, you can include details about embeddable domains. Here, you can enter the list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice(s).
