# Using privacy notice versioning

Securiti maintains a version history for each privacy notice. Each time you publish the notice, it creates a new version of the notice. You can view and revert to earlier versions.

## Viewing available versions

1. If you have not already done so, in Securiti open **Privacy Notice** **Management**. Then click a privacy notice.
2. Near the upper-right corner, next to the **Preview**, click the **More** (three dots) and then choose **Version History** (clock icon) from the menu, as shown below.

![Screenshot of a privacy notice management dashboard with various options and sections displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_privacy_notice_versioning_1.png)
3. From the **Version History** panel, choose the version you want to view, as shown below.

![Overview of PDPL Privacy Notice with version history and company information displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_privacy_notice_versioning_2.png)

## Reverting to a previous version

1. Open the version you want to revert to as described in the [Viewing available versions](/latest/docs/using-privacy-notice-versioning#viewing-available-versions).
2. Click on the **Edit** (three dots) icon and **Restore this version** from the menu, as shown below.

![Overview of the PDPL Privacy Notice with company information and version history options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_privacy_notice_versioning_3.png)
3. Click **Yes** from the **Restore Version** pop-up to restore the version, as shown below.

![Prompt to restore a previous version of the PDPL Privacy Notice document.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_privacy_notice_versioning_4_1.png)
