# Configuring the UAE PDPL privacy notice template

United Arab Emirates's (UAE) Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) is a federal data protection law that applies to individuals who live in or have a place of business in the UAE. It ensures that organizations handle personal data responsibly and securely.

In **Form** view, Use the following table to configure the Privacy Notice Sections:

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Company & Website Information | Fill in basic information for your company website (company name, URL, etc. Also indicate whether your company has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO), and if so, their email address. |
| Definitions | Provides boilerplate definitions of the various used terms such as Privacy Notice, Service Provider, Personal Data, and Legal Basis in the privacy notice. You can customize the section by editing its contents. Switch to **Editor** view, and edit the text. When you are done, click **Save & Publish > Save Changes**. |
| Data Processing Activities | Add, remove, and customize the various data processing activities you perform on visitors' personal data. - To remove an activity, choose **Options** (three dots) **> Delete**. - To add an activity (if not all activities are already present), click **Add from built-in library**. Mark or unmark the checkbox next to the **Built-in Library** header to show or hide its information in the preview mode, as shown below. ![Overview of data processing activities in a privacy notice management dashboard.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_1.png) For each activity, fill in the necessary information. If necessary, make sure you enter a value for how long you store information collected by the website. You can manually add activities, and also add processes from your Data Mapping Automation module if you subscribe to it: - To manually add an activity, click **Add Manually** and fill in the fields. - To add a process from the Data Mapping Automation module, click **Import from Data Maps** and choose one of your processes. Securiti automatically populates the fields of the processing activities entry with information from the process you chose. You also have the option of having Securiti automatically update the privacy notice if the process is changed in the Data Mapping Automation module. Near the upper right corner of the process, choose **Options > Auto-Update** as shown below. The update is placed in Draft state, and does not change your live notice without your approval. ![Dashboard displaying data processing activities and options for privacy notice management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_x.png) |
| Minors/Child | You can configure content regarding the collection of information from children and minors. Under the GDPR, the default age for minors to 16 years. |
| Automated Processing | Use automated systems to analyze or evaluate personal data to make decisions about data subjects, and ensure compliance with relevant data protection laws. |
| Cookies | Fill in information about the use of **Essential** and **Non-Essential** cookies on your website. You can add cookie categories and also import the results of cookie category scans from your **Cookie Consent Management** module, if you are subscribed to it. You can also have Securiti automatically update the listed cookies whenever an updated cookie policy for the domains is published. For more information, see [Embedding cookies in the privacy notice](/v1/docs/embedding-cookies-in-the-privacy-notice). (Optional) You can hide cookie categories so that they are not listed in the **Cookies** section of the privacy notice. On the tile for a cookie category, click its **Options** menu and choose **Hide** as shown below. ![Privacy notice dashboard showing cookie preferences and categories for website compliance.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_2.png) Also,If you have created a preference center in the Securiti Universal Consent Management module, you can import it directly using the preference center tools shown below. ![Privacy notice dashboard showing cookie categories and consent preference manager options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_3.png) (Optional) For each cookie you can configure details including the retention period and lifespan. In a cookie category click a specific cookie to expand it, then configure its details to suit as shown below. ![Dashboard view of PDPL Privacy Notice with sections for cookie details and management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_4.png) |
| Data Sharing | Fill in information about how you share information collected by your website. ![Form for specifying establishments sharing personal data under PDPL Privacy Notice.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_uae_pdpl_privacy_notice_5.png) |
| Data Retention | Provides a description of the default data retention policy. This section also contains content describing to the data subject the criteria on which data retention periods for various data processing activities can be determined. You can customize the section by editing its contents. Switch to **Editor** view, and edit the text. When you are done, click **Save & Publish > Save Changes**. |
| Data Security | Describes how you protect the personal information collected by your website. You can edit the text in the Data Security section, and also select specific security measures (including custom measures) from a list. Switch to **Editor** view, and edit the text. When you are done, click **Save & Publish > Save Changes**. |
| Your Rights | Describes website visitors' rights regarding the handling of their personal data. You can edit the text in the Your Rights section, and also include the URL for your data subject rights portal, if you are subscribed to the Securiti Data Subject Rights module. To edit the text, switch to **Editor** view. When you are done, click **Save & Publish > Save Changes**. |
| Updates to this notice | Provides a boilerplate description of the process you use when you update the privacy notice. To edit the text, switch to **Editor** view. When you are done, click **Save & Publish > Save Changes**. |

The Personal Data Protection Law, 2021 (PDPL) is United Arab Emirates’ (UAE) federal data protection law that became effective on 02 January 2022. It applies to individuals who reside in UAE or have a place of business in UAE, organizations established in the UAE, and organizations established outside UAE if they process personal data of individuals in UAE.

Privacy notice sections show different customizable components that Securiti's Privacy Notice Management module offers you. You can customize these sections to suit. They include Company and Website Information, Definitions, Sensitive Personal Data Processing, and more.

A key role within your organization responsible for overseeing and ensuring compliance with data protection laws and regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union. The primary responsibility of the DPO is to monitor the organization's data processing activities, assess potential risks to data subjects' privacy rights, and implement measures to mitigate those risks.

Definitions is one section for the privacy notice(s) that lets you add definitions for terms that are specific to your organization. You can add all of the definitions in one section for the ease of the data subject.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an European Union (EU) regulation on information privacy. It aims to ensure the protection of personal information through a human rights-centric approach, and imposes obligations onto organizations anywhere, so long as they target or collect data related to people in the EU.

Data retention is the practice of storing and managing data for a specific period before either deleting or archiving the data for future. The data retention period may be determined by legal, regulatory, or business requirements. Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management module lets you inform the data subject of your organization's data retention policy.
