# Creating and managing privacy notice templates

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you need to create privacy notices for multiple regions while keeping language, branding, and structure consistent. Managing each notice separately often causes duplicated work, inconsistent wording, and formatting errors. For instance, when your company expands services into France and Germany, updating notices manually for each region becomes challenging and time-consuming. With reusable templates in Securiti, you can create one approved notice structure, reuse common sections, add translations, and apply consistent branding across all notices. This helps you publish accurate, standardized privacy notices faster while reducing manual effort and compliance risks.

Privacy notice templates in Securiti lets you create standardized and reusable notices. These templates let you quickly create new privacy notices, keep them consistent, and make updates in one place instead of editing each notice separately. You create a template once, and use it multiple times saving time and effort.

## Creating a privacy notice template

To create a new privacy notice template:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Templates**.
2. At the upper right corner of the page, click **+ Create New Template**, as shown below.

![Overview of privacy notice templates with options to create and manage them.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_template_1.png)
3. In the **Template Information** popup, provide the **Template Name**, **Description**, and **Organization** details, then click **Create Template**, as shown below.

![Template creation interface for a privacy notice with fields for name and description.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_template_2.png)

Securiti opens a template editor with a empty default section.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The maximum character limit for a privacy notice template name is **64 characters**.

In the template workspace, you can add and manage privacy notice template content based on your organization’s requirements, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template section detailing information collection and data sources.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_template_33.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> To format the content of your template, customize its appearance, see [Understanding privacy notice formatting tools](https://helpcenter.securiti.ai/docs/understanding-privacy-notice-formatting-tools).

## Adding and managing template sections

To add a new section to the template:

1. On the left side of the template workspace, in the section navigation panel below the default section, click **+ Add New**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template section detailing information collection and data sources.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/section_management_1.png)

Use the template workspace to add details about the notice section, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template outlining information collection and usage details for users.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/section_management_2.png)
2. At the top of the template workspace, use the toolbar to format text, change and apply fonts, and insert elements such as tables, images, dividers, hyperlinks, and code blocks, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template displaying sections on information collection and data usage.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/section_management_3.png)

For more information, see [Understanding privacy notice formatting tools](https://helpcenter.securiti.ai/docs/understanding-privacy-notice-formatting-tools).
3. To rename or duplicate a section, next to the section name, click **Options** (three dots). From the menu, select **Rename** to update the section name, or **Duplicate** to create a copy of the section, as shown below.

![Document editing interface showing options for managing sections in a privacy notice template.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/section_management_4.png)

Similarly, you can also **Hide Section**, or **Delete** a section.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> You can only **Hide** or **Delete** a section if there are two or more sections.

## Linking sections

To link different sections of your privacy notice template:

1. In the template editor, navigate to any section.
2. Select the text, and click **Add Link** (chain icon) on the toolbar, as shown below.

![Document outlining information collection, usage, and data sharing policies for privacy management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_sections_1.png)
3. From the **Add Link** popup, select **Anchor Link**.

![Dialog box for adding a link in a privacy notice template with options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_sections_2.png)
4. From the **Select Section** menu, choose the section you want to link, and then click **Submit**, as shown below.

![Interface for adding links in a privacy notice template with highlighted sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linking_sections_3.png)

Securiti navigates you to the linked section when you click on the link.

You can also link to external URLs using the **New Tab** or **Same Tab** options, as shown below.

![Interface for adding links with options for opening in new or same tab.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_linking_sections.png)

## Syncing with privacy notices

You can sync your template with the linked privacy notice after updating the template.

To sync the updates:

1. Near the upper right corner, next to **Preview**, click **Save & Sync**, and select **Sync to Linked Notices,** as shown below.

![Privacy notice template showing sections on information collection, use, and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/syncing_template_with_notices_1.png)
2. From the **Sync Changes to Linked Notices** popup, mark the checkbox next to the notice with which you want to sync the new content, as shown below.

![Sync changes to linked notices in the privacy notice template interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/syncing_template_with_notices_2.png)
3. In the **Sync in Progress** popup, wait for syncing process to complete, and then click **Continue to Publish**, as shown below.

![Sync in progress for privacy notice template with options to publish notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/syncing_template_with_notices_3.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> During syncing, Securiti replaces any custom modifications made directly to the privacy notice with the latest template updates. Review your custom changes before syncing.
4. In the **Publish Synced Notices** popup, click **Publish Notices** or **Publish Later** depending upon your need.

![Dialog box for publishing synced privacy notices with options to select and publish.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/syncing_template_with_notices_4.png)

Securiti synchronizes any template changes with the selected privacy notice.

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> Securiti does not let you sync templates to the linked privacy notices if the **required** custom metadata fields have missing values.
> 
> To add the missing custom metadata details, near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Sync** and then click **Missing Custom Metadata**.
> 
> ![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection and data usage.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/missing_custom_metatdata_templates.png)
> 
> Enter a value for each required custom metadata field.

## Adding language translations

You can add language translations in the template to create a multilingual template.

To add a language translation to a template:

1. Near the upper right corner of the template editor, to the left of the **Details**, click **English**, and from the menu, select **Manage Languages**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template outlining information collection, usage, and data sharing policies.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_1.png)

Or near the upper right corner, next to **Preview**, click **More** (three dots), and from the menu select **Settings**.

![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection and data usage details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_2.png)

From the **Template Settings**, click **Manage Languages**, as shown below.

![Settings page for managing languages and public language preferences in a template.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_settings_language_menu.png)
2. Click **Add a language** menu to add a new language translation to the template, as shown below.

![Settings page for managing languages and preferences in a template interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_3.png)

You can choose from two types of language options in the menu:

| Language type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| System Generated | Automatically translates the default language. |
| User Generated | Lets you add translations manually. |

If you select a **System Generated** language, Securiti automatically translates the default language into the selected language. A **User Generated** language, on the other hand, lets you add translations manually.
3. When you select a language and click **Add**, it appears in the language configuration table, as shown below.

![Settings page for managing languages and fonts in a template configuration.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_4.png)
4. You can also provide your own translation for a **System Generated** language. Switch the **Translation Source** from **System** to **User** within the language configuration table, as shown below.

![Settings page for managing languages and preferences in a template interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_5.png)
5. To assign a specific font to a selected language, click the **Fonts** menu in the language configuration table and select a font, as shown below.

![Settings page showing language management and font selection options for templates.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_7.png)

You can search for a font or click **Manage Fonts** to open the **Font Repository**. For more information on managing fonts, see [Managing the Font Repository](/latest/docs/managing-the-font-repository).
6. You can also manage language preferences from the **Public Language Preferences** section:
  1. Mark the **Language Intelligence** (Browser language detection) checkbox to enable or disable automatic detection of a website visitor’s language based on browser settings.
  2. Mark the Language Selection for Visitors checkbox to enable or disable a language selector on the privacy notice that lets visitors to manually choose their preferred language.
  3. Mark the **Enable Section Title Translation** checkbox to enable or disable translation of privacy notice section titles for each configured language.
7. To apply the translation and language preferences, click **Save Changes,** as shown below.

![Template settings for managing languages and public language preferences in a user interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_languages_8.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> - The **System Generated** translation takes up to one hour to sync.
> - For all published and draft notices, the status updates to **Syncing Translations** when additional languages are added to the notice.
8. Once the translation syncs, next to **Co-owners**, click **English** (language menu) and select the translated language to view or edit it in the editor, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection and language options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/template_lang.png)

## Using top-level filters

The top-level filter lets you combine different properties of the privacy notice templates and apply filters accordingly. It works from the top down and determines the result values in the grid view.

To use the top-level filter:

1. Near the upper left corner of the **Templates** dashboard, click **+**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Management interface displaying templates, filters, and synchronization status.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/top_level_filter.png)
2. From the filters panel, apply one or more filters using the following properties.

| Property | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | Filters templates by unique template ID. Use the **Is equal to** operator to match a specific template ID. |
| Organization | Filters templates by organization, such as **Default Organization** or a user-defined organization. Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more organizations. |
| Owner | Filters templates by owner to show templates based on the template owner, such as `sam@democo.com` or `bob@democo.com`. Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more owners. |
| Created Date | Filters templates by **Created Date** to show templates based on their creation date. Use the **between** operator to specify a date range. |
| Status | Filters templates by **Status** to show privacy notices templates based on status, such as **Ready to sync** or **Synced**. Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more statuses. |

For instance, if you want to filter templates by **Status** (property), from the left pane of the filter panel, select **Status**, then from the right pane mark the checkboxes next to the status names.
3. Repeat the same step for other properties according to your search needs. Once you apply all required properties, near the bottom of the filter panel, click **Apply**, as shown below.

![Filter options for Privacy Notice Management with highlighted 'Apply' button.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/top_level_filter_2.png)

Securiti builds a query based on your selected properties and displays the filtered results.
4. To clear the top-level filter, from the upper right corner, click **Clear Search**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Management interface displaying templates, statuses, and options for syncing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/top_level_filter_3.png)

To read more about saving and using filtered views, see [Using top level filters](/latest/docs/using-top-level-filters).

To save a view for your set filters and search results:

1. On the **Templates** dashboard, near the upper right corner of the page, click **More** (three dots), and choose **Save View** from the menu, as shown below.

![Privacy notice management interface showing template status and organization details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/save_view_notice_templates_1.png)
2. On the **Save View** panel, enter a name and description, and select a group of views or add a new one. Then, at the bottom of the panel, click **Save View** to save your view, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Management interface showing templates ready to sync and their details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/save_view_notice_templates_2.png)

### Using the saved views

You can access and apply your saved views at any time through the Saved View panel.

1. Near the upper right corner of the **Templates** dashboard, click **More** (three dots), and choose **Saved View** from the menu, as shown below.

![Privacy notice management interface showing templates and their statuses for organization approval.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/saved_views_notice_templates_1.png)

Clicking **Saved View** opens a panel for saved filters on the left side. The panel displays saved groups and views. Click any group to view the list of saved views for that group, as shown below.

![List of templates ready to sync with their status and organization details displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/saved_views_notice_templates_2.png)
2. Click the view name, or next to the view name click **Options** (three dots), and choose **View** from the menu to apply the saved filter, as shown below.

![List of templates with statuses including 'Ready to Sync' and 'Pending Translations'.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/saved_views_notice_templates_3.png)

### Managing the saved views

To update a saved filters, their group, or the names and descriptions associated with them:

1. Near the upper right corner of the **Templates** dashboard, click **More** (three dots), and from the menu, select **Saved View.**

Clicking **Saved View** opens up a panel for saved views on the left side of the page. The panel displays custom groups of saved views.
2. Next to the group name, click **Options** (three dots), and choose **Rename** or **Delete** from the menu to perform the desired action, as shown below.

![List of saved views with options to rename or delete templates displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/editing_views_1.png)
3. Similarly, click any group to view the list of saved views. Next to the view name, click **Options** (three dots), and choose **Edit** or **Delete** from the menu to perform the desired action, as shown below.

![List of saved views with options to view, edit, or delete templates.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/editing_views_2.png)
