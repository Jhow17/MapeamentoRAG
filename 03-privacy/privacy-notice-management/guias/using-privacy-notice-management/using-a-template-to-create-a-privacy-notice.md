# Using a template to create a privacy notice

You can use a template in the Privacy Notice Management module either from the **Templates** dashboard or from within the template editor using the available options.

To use a template:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Templates**.
2. From the templates list, next to the template name, click **Options** (three dots), and from the menu, select **Use Template**, as shown below.

![Overview of privacy notice templates with their statuses and actions available.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_a_template_1.png)

To use template from the editor:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Templates**.
2. Create a new or open an existing template.
3. Near the upper right corner of the template workspace, click **Details**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template outlining information collection, usage, and data sharing details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_a_template_2(1).png)
4. From the **Details** panel, click **Use Template**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection and data usage details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_a_template_3.png)
5. Or near the upper right corner of the template editor, next to **Preview**, click **More**, and then from the menu, select **Use Template**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection, use, and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_templates(1).png)

To use template from the **Privacy Notices** dashboard, see [Creating a privacy notice using a template](/latest/docs/creating-a-privacy-notice-using-a-template).
