# Archiving your privacy notice

You can archive the privacy notices of your organization and business instead of deleting them to retain the record. Archiving a notice changes its existing status to **Archived**.

Securiti lets you unarchive your privacy notice anytime without altering the previously published URL.

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This feature is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

## Archiving a privacy notice

To archive a privacy notice:

1. In Privacy Notice Management, go to the **Privacy Notices** dashboard and open an existing privacy notice.
2. From the upper right corner of the page, next to **Preview**, click **More** (three dots).
3. From the **More** menu, click **Archive**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice dashboard displaying sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/archive_notice_1(1).png)
4. On the **Archive Privacy Notice** popup, click **Archive Notice** to confirm, as shown below.

![Prompt to archive a privacy notice with options to learn more or cancel.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/archive_notice_2.png)
5. Securiti turns the privacy notice status to **Archived**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice sections detailing information collection, usage, and data sharing practices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/archive_notice_5(1).png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti returns a **404** error when you access hosted or embedded links for archived privacy notices, as shown below.
> 
> ![Error message indicating a 404 page not found issue on a website.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/404_error.png)

When a privacy notice is in the **Archived** state, Securiti does not let you:

- Publish
- Revert
- Sync translations
- Update settings
- Update notice content
- Approve or submit for approval
- Add co-owners
- Restore versions

## Unarchiving a privacy notice

To unarchive a privacy notice:

1. In Privacy Notice Management, go to the **Privacy Notices** dashboard and open any existing archived privacy notice.
2. Near the upper right corner of the page, next to **Preview**, click **More** (three dots).
3. From the **More** menu, click **Unarchive**, as shown below.

![Privacy notice dashboard showing sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/unarchive_notice_6.png)
4. On the **Unarchive Privacy Notice** popup, click **Unarchive Notice** to confirm, as shown below.

![Prompt to unarchive a privacy notice with options to cancel or proceed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/unarchive_notice_7.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> - If the privacy notice has approvers, Securiti moves it to the **Needs Approval** state instead of **Ready to Publish** after unarchiving.
> - Securiti retains the original published link, so you can restore the notice at its original URL after unarchiving.
