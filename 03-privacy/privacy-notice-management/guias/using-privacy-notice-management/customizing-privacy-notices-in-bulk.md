# Customizing privacy notices in bulk

Securiti lets you customize privacy notices in bulk, maintaining consistent branding and reducing manual effort and saving time.​

## Styling privacy notices in bulk

To apply style in bulk:

1. In Securiti open Privacy Notice Management.
2. From the privacy notice table on the **Dashboard**, mark two of more checkboxes to select multiple privacy notices, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with statuses and modification dates for management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_1(1).png)

To the left of the the **ID** column, click on the **Arrow** (caret icon) to select multiple privacy notices using one of the options from the menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to select and manage them.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_2(2).png)
3. After selecting the privacy notices that you want to apply style, next to selected notices badge, click **Edit** **Styling**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to edit and manage their statuses.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_(1).png)
4. You can update the **Page Color Settings**, **Language** **Dropdown Settings,** and **Organization Logo** from the **Edit Styling for Selected Notices** popup, as shown below.

![Editing privacy notice settings including color options and organization logo upload.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_x.png)

From **Page Color Settings** section, you can customize the following settings:

The **Primary Color** changes key UI elements such as buttons, links, and toggles. The **Secondary Color** changes the text color, while the **Background Color** updates the page’s background.

From the **Drop Down Settings** section, you can change the **Background Color** and **Border Radius** of the language selector on the privacy notices.

Additionally, the **Organization Logo** section lets you update the organization logo across multiple privacy notices, as shown below.
  - Primary Color
  - Secondary Color
  - Background Color
5. After making changes, click **Save Changes** to apply them across all selected privacy notices, as shown below.

![Editing privacy notice settings including colors, language options, and organization logo upload.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_y.png)
6. To preview the changes, open the privacy notice with the newly applied style and near the upper right corner of the page, click **Preview**, and then **Preview Draft**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying data processing activities and options for privacy notice management.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_66.png)
7. To publish the privacy notice, near the upper right corner, click **Save & Publish**, and then **Publish Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying PDPL Privacy Notice with data processing activities and user information fields.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notices_in_bulk_77.png)
