# Embedding cookies in the privacy notice

Securiti lets you embed cookies of your scanned domain in your privacy notice to let you be more transparent with your website visitors, data subjects, and end-users. Embedding cookies in your privacy notice offers clear information about the types of cookies used, and their purposes.

To import cookies into your privacy notice:

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Notice** **Management** and either create a new privacy notice or open an existing one.
2. From the left section navigation bar, select **Cookies** option, and then switch to the **Form** view. In the **Form** view, click **Import from Cookie Scan Results** as shown below.

![Privacy notice section focusing on cookie usage and categories for website compliance.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-1.png)
3. On the **Import from Cookie Scan Results** popup, from the **DOMAIN GROUP** section, mark the checkbox next to the domain group which your scanned domain is a part of. From the **DOMAIN** section, mark the checkbox next to the domain(s), and near the bottom of the popup, click **Select Cookie Categories**, as shown below.

![Importing cookie scan results with selected domain group and highlighted URL.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-2.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> - You must have a successfully scanned domain with cookies in the Cookie Consent Management module before importing cookies from that domain to your privacy notice.
> - Securiti lets you search the domain group and domain by name using the search bars located at the top of respective sections.
4. On the **Import from Cookie Scan Results** popup, either select all or select cookie categories from which you wish to import cookies to your privacy notice.
  1. To select all cookie categories, near the upper right corner of the popup, mark the **Select All** checkbox.
  2. To select specific cookie categories, from the **CATEGORY** section, mark the checkboxes next to the cookie categories. Once done, near the bottom of the popup, click **Select Cookie** as shown below.

![User interface for importing cookie scan results with various categories and options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-3.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Tip**
> 
> To filter out cookie categories, click the search bar, type the category name, and press enter as shown below.
> 
> ![Importing cookie scan results with essential category selection in privacy management dashboard.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/tip_embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice.png)
5. On the **Import from Cookie Scan Results** popup, from the **COOKIES** section, either select all cookies or specific cookies that you wish to import to your privacy notice.
  1. To select all cookies, from the upper right corner of the popup, mark the **Select All** checkbox, as shown below.

![Importing cookie scan results with options to select all essential cookies.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-4.png)
  2. To select specific cookies, mark the checkbox next to each cookie that you wish to import. Once done, near the bottom of the popup, click **Import Cookies.**

> [!NOTE]
> **Tip**
> 
> Securiti also lets you filter out specific cookies by their name. To search for cookies, from the search bar, type the cookie name, and press enter as shown below.
> 
> ![Cookie management interface displaying various cookie consent options for selection.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/tip_embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-6.png)
6. To preview the successfully imported cookies from the scanned domain, from the notice setup section, switch to the **Editor** view, and preview your privacy notice with recent changes, as shown below.

![Privacy notice detailing cookie categories, providers, and their purposes for data usage.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-7.png)
7. When you are satisfied with the imported cookies on your privacy notice, near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish** and then select **Save Changes**.
8. Once your changes are saved, from the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish**, and **Publish Notice** from the menu, as shown below.

![Privacy notice dashboard showing cookie categories and options to publish changes.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-8.png)

After publishing your changes, Securiti shows you a confirmation message, as shown below.

![Privacy notice published successfully with links to company information and policy details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/embedding_cookies_in_privacy_notice-10.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti may take up a few minutes to reflect these changes on your deployed privacy notice.
