# Creating periodic reminders for privacy notices

You can send automatic reminders (emails) regarding which privacy notices are due for the next periodic/annual review.

1. In Securiti, open **Privacy Notice** **Management** and create a new privacy notice or open an existing one for editing.
2. Near the upper right corner, next to the **Preview**, click the **More** (three dots). From the menu, click **Schedule Reminder**, as shown below.

![Document interface showing AI notice template with options for editing and publishing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_periodic_reminders_privacy_notices_1.png)
3. Select the **Interval**, **Frequency**, and **Start On** date, then click **Schedule Reminder** to enable email notifications for notice review, as shown below.

![Reminder scheduling interface for automated email notifications regarding document reviews.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_periodic_reminders_privacy_notices_2.png)
