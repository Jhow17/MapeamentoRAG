# Creating a privacy notice from a blank layout

You can create a privacy notice using a blank notice and customize the content according to your organization’s requirements.

To create a privacy notice from a blank notice:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Privacy Notices**.
2. Near the upper right corner of the dashboard, click **+ Add Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to filter and add new notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_1.png)
3. From the **Create New Privacy Notice** popup, click **Start From Blank** card, and then **Start**, as shown below.

![Options to create a new privacy notice, including starting from a blank canvas.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_2.png)
4. In the **Start From Blank** popup, update or enter a new **Notice Name**, add **Description**, **Choose Organization**, and click **Create Notice**, as shown below.

![Form for creating a personal data collection notice with required fields and options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_3.png)

Securiti opens a notice with a blank section where you can add content for your notice.

(Optional) You can also rename the notice within the notice editor.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The maximum character limit for a privacy notice name is **64 characters**.
5. Near the left edge of the header, next to the privacy notice name, click **Edit** (pencil icon), edit the name to suit, then click **Save** (checkmark icon).

If you do not rename the privacy notice, Securiti assigns the notice a default name based on the date you create it.
6. On the left side of the notice editor, from the section panel, click **+ Add New** to add a new section, as shown below.

![Overview of personal data collection notice and types of data collected.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_4.png)

Use the editor’s toolbar to add details and modify sections in the notice workspace, as shown below.

![Overview of personal data collection notice with highlighted section on information collection.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_5.png)

Create sections based on the requirements of your notice.
7. To add tables to a section, from the toolbar, click **Table** (grid icon), and select the number of rows and columns as shown below.

![Document section on information collection detailing data types and sources collected.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_6.png)
8. To add images, code snippets, code blocks, dividers, or quotes, near the upper left corner of the notice workspace, click **+ Insert Block** and from the menu select the desired tool, as shown below.

![Dashboard view of a personal data collection notice with editing options visible.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_7.png)

Or, click **+** from within the notice workspace to insert a block, as shown below.

![Document section on personal data collection with an option to add new content.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_8.png)
9. To link notice sections:
  1. Select the text and click **Add Link** (chain icon) on the toolbar, as shown below.

![Document outlining personal data collection, usage, and sharing information sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_9(1).png)
  2. From the **Add Link** popup, select **Anchor Link** card.
  3. From the **Select Section** menu, choose the section you want to link, and then click **Submit**, as shown below.

![Linking sections in a privacy notice for data collection and usage information.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_10.png)
10. To rename, duplicate, hide or delete a section, next to the section name, click **Options** (three dots) and from the menu select the preferred option, as shown below.

![Document section on data collection with options to rename, duplicate, or delete sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_11.png)
11. To rearrange sections, drag and drop them up or down to move them to the desired position in the sequence.

The notice editor lets you add [Co-owners](/latest/docs/adding-and-removing-privacy-notice-co-owners), assign [Approvers](/latest/docs/using-approval-workflows-for-privacy-notices), and collaborate through [Messages](/latest/docs/using-messages), as shown below.

![Overview of personal data collection notice with sections on information use and sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_12.png)

To modify the content of your privacy notice, manage translations in different languages, and customize its appearance, see:

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti lets you add custom metadata to filter, organize, and manage your privacy notices. To assign custom metadata to your privacy notices and search or filter them using custom metadata, see:
> 
> - [Assigning custom metadata](/latest/docs/assigning-custom-metadata)
> - [Searching and filtering privacy notices and templates using custom metadata](https://helpcenter.securiti.ai/docs/searching-and-filtering-privacy-notices-and-templates-using-custom-metadata)
  - [Understanding privacy notice formatting tools](/latest/docs/understanding-privacy-notice-formatting-tools)
  - [Managing language translation for privacy notices](/latest/docs/managing-language-translations-for-privacy-notices)
  - [Customizing privacy notices](/latest/docs/customizing-privacy-notices)
12. To preview the entire privacy notice as it would appear to a website visitor, near the upper right corner of the notice setup area, click **Preview**. From the **Preview** menu, you can preview both the draft and the published version of the privacy notice. Securiti opens the preview in a separate browser tab.

After making the required changes, you can publish your notice.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> You can publish the notice only when the notice status changes to **Ready to Publish**.
13. After making the changes, near the upper right corner of the editor, next to **Preview** click **Save & Publish**, and then **Save Changes** to save the updates to your notice, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying personal data collection notice with sections for information and usage.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_13.png)
14. To preview the notice, near upper right corner of the notice editor, next to **Save & Publish**, click **Preview**, and from the menu, select **Preview Draft**, as shown below.

![Overview of personal data collection notice with sections on information use and sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/preview_notice.png)
15. To publish the notice, near the upper right corner of the notice editor, next to **Preview**, click **Save & Publish**, and then **Publish Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying personal data collection notice with sections for information and usage.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_blank_14.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If the **Publish Notice** button is disabled, it is probably because you have not filled in some required information. In the Sections list, find the numerical badges that are not green and click them to provide the required information.

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> Securiti does not let you publish privacy notices if the **required** custom metadata fields have missing values.
> 
> To add the missing custom metadata details, near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish**, and then click **Missing Custom Metadata,** as shown below.
> 
> ![Privacy notice dashboard showing sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/missing_custom_metatdata_1(1).png)
> 
> Enter a value for each required custom metadata field.
16. To deploy the privacy notice after publishing, near the upper-right corner of the notice editor, next to **Preview**, click **More** (three dots icon), and from the menu, select either **Copy Hosted Link** or **Copy Embedded Link**, based on the deployment requirement, as shown below.

![Privacy notice document outlining information collection, usage, and data sharing policies.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/publish_embed_privacy_notice_links(1).png)
17. Work with your website team to deploy or embed that link onto your website so visitors can access your privacy notice.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If your website embeds Securiti's privacy notice using an `iframe`, you can dynamically adjust the `iframe` height on the parent page using the following script:
> 
> ```javascript
> const iframe = document.getElementById('iframe_id');
> window.addEventListener("message", function(event) {
> 	if(iframe && event && event.data && event.data.type === "IFRAME_CONTENT_SIZE" ) {
> 		iframe.style.height = event.data.payload.height + 'px';
> 	}
> });
> ```
> 
> In the parent page script, you must replace the `iframe_id` with the ID of your embedded `iframe`.
> 
> The script on the parent page sets the embedded `iframe` ‘s height using the content height that Securiti’s `iframe` sends, ensuring a scroll-free experience.

On the published privacy notice, your data subjects can enable accessibility settings to improve content visibility and readability. Near the lower right corner of your privacy notice, they can access these settings by clicking the **Web Accessibility** icon, as shown below.

![Overview of data collection, usage, and sharing practices in a structured format.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/accessibility_settings_1(1).png)

From the **Accessibility Settings** popup, they can enable **Enable high contrast for increased visibility** toggle, and click **Apply Settings** to switch the content to high contrast mode, as shown below.

![Accessibility settings for color contrast with options to enable high visibility features.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/accessibility_settings_2(2).png)

## Configuring privacy notice settings

You can use general settings to manage the notice header title, allowed domains, and the display layout of the privacy notice.

To configure the general settings:

1. Near the upper right corner to the page, click **More**, and from the menu, select **Settings**.
2. From the **General** tab, configure the following settings:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy Notice Header Title | Enter the title that appears at the top of the privacy notice. |
| Embeddable Domains | To build a list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice, must type the full URLs of domains with prefixes such as `https://`, `http://`, and `www`, etc. to add to the list, and press **Enter**, as shown below: - Acceptable domain URLs: - `https://www.example.com` - `http://www.example.com` - `www.example.com` - Unacceptable domain URLs: - `acme.com` - `example.com` - `social.com` ![Settings for privacy notice customization, including embeddable domains and display options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_1(1).png) |
| Notice View | Customize the layout and display settings of the privacy notice page through the following options: - **Enable collapsible mode​​** **for sections** displays the privacy notice in an accordion-style layout, making navigation easier. - **Hide table of content for mobile view** hides the table of contents on mobile for a cleaner, more focused experience. - **Hide table of content for desktop view** hides the table of contents on desktop. - **Enable card view for sections** to enable a tiled layout, making content easier to browse. ![Settings for privacy notice customization including collapsible sections and content visibility options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_2.png) |
3. To reset the notice settings to the default module-level settings, near the bottom left corner of the **Notice Settings**, click **Reset to Default**, as shown below.

![Settings page for data protection notice with options for collapsible sections and visibility.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_1.png)
4. From the **Reset to App Settings** popup, click **Reset Settings**, as shown below.

![Settings menu displaying options for privacy notice and app settings reset.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_2.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti applies only the **Color & Styling** changes when resetting the notice settings to the default module-level settings. Update the following settings manually if needed:
> 
> - Added languages
> - Font selections
> - Translation sources

To configure the default settings, see [Customizing privacy notices](/latest/docs/customizing-your-privacy-notices).

In the Privacy Notice Management module settings, you can include details about embeddable domains. Here, you can enter the list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice(s).
