# Customizing privacy notice templates

You can customize the privacy notice templates by updating the page color settings, language dropdown menu, and organization logo to align with your organization’s requirements.

## Customizing colors and styling of individual templates

To customize a privacy notice template:

1. Near the upper right corner to the page, click **More**, and from the menu, select **Settings,** as shown below.

![Privacy notice template showing sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_templates.png)
2. From the **Color & Styling** tab, configure the following template settings:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Page Color Settings | Customize the page color of the template using the following settings: - Primary Color - Secondary Color - Background Color The **Primary Color** changes key UI elements such as buttons, links, and toggles. The **Secondary Color** changes the text color, while the **Background Color** updates the page’s background, as shown below. ![Settings page for customizing privacy notice colors and language options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notice_template_2.png) |
| Language Dropdown Menu | Change the language dropdown appearance using the following settings: - Set the Background Color to update its color. - Adjust the Border Radius to control the roundness of its corners, as shown below. ![Settings page for customizing privacy notice appearance and language options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notice_template_3.png) |
| Organization Logo | Click **Upload New Logo** to upload your company logo to have it appear at the top of the privacy notice, as shown below. ![Settings interface showing options for logo upload and template customization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_privacy_notice_template_4.png) > [!NOTE] > Note > > - You can upload the logo in **PNG**, **JPG** and **GIF** formats. > - Use a logo size of at least **96 × 96 px** and at most **480 × 480 px** to prevent errors. |

## Customizing default colors and styling

You can also configure default colors and styling to maintain a consistent branded experience across all templates.

To configure the default color and styling settings in Securiti:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Settings > Color & Styling,** as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with settings for font and color styling options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/deafult_3.png)
2. Configure **Page Color Settings**, **Language Dropdown Settings** and **Organization Logo**, and click **Save Changes**.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti applies the default color and styling settings configured in the module **Settings** globally across all privacy notices and templates. Newly created privacy notices or templates automatically inherit these settings unless you customize them individually.
