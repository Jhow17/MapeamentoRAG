# Exporting privacy notices in PDF format

You can export the privacy notice as a PDF to share it in a clear, consistent, and compliant format across your internal teams, customers, regulators, or other stakeholders.

To export a privacy notice:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Notice Management.
2. On the Privacy Notice Management Dashboard, click the privacy notice you want to export in PDF format.
3. Near the upper right corner, next to the **Preview**, click the **More** (three dots), and then choose **Generate PDF** from the menu, as shown below.

![Privacy policy document for PNM, detailing data protection and user rights information.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/exporting_privacy_notices_in_pdf_format.png)

Securiti downloads the PDF and saves it locally on your machine.
