# Customizing privacy notices

You can configure and customize privacy notices to match your organization’s branding, language preferences, layout requirements, and other notice settings.

## Customizing colors and styling of individual notices

To configure and customize your privacy notice in Securiti:

1. Open privacy notice management.
2. From the **Dashboard**, create a new or open an existing privacy notice.
3. Near the upper right corner of the notice editor, click **More**, and from the menu, select **Settings,** as shown below.

![Overview of privacy notice sections including information collection and data sharing details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/customizing_notices.png)

In the **Settings** page, from the **Color & Styling** tab, you can configure the following settings:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Page Color Settings | Customize the page color using the following settings: - Primary Color - Secondary Color - Background Color The **Primary Color** changes key UI elements such as buttons, links, and toggles. The **Secondary Color** changes the text color, while the **Background Color** updates the page’s background. ![Settings page for customizing privacy notice colors and language dropdown options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_new_privacy_notice-4.png) Open **Manage Base Appearance** to update the default privacy notice appearance, which opens the **Color & Styling** option within the module-level **Settings**, as shown below. ![Settings page for customizing privacy notice appearance and color options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/create_new_privayc_notice_1_13.png) ![Settings menu for color and styling options in privacy notice management interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/create_new_privayc_notice_1_14.png) |
| Language Dropdown Settings | Change the language dropdown appearance using the following settings: - Set the **Background Color** to update its color. - Adjust the **Border Radius** to control the roundness of its corners. |
| Organization Logo | Click **Upload Image** to upload your company logo to have it appear at the top of the privacy notice. > [!NOTE] > Note > > - You can upload the logo in PNG, JPG and GIF formats. > - Use a logo size of at least 96 × 96 px and at most 480 × 480 px to prevent errors. |

## Customizing default colors and styling

You can also configure default colors and styling to maintain a consistent branded experience across all notices and templates.

To configure the default color and styling settings in Securiti:

1. Open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Settings > Color & Styling,** as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with settings for font and color styling options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/deafult_3.png)
2. Configure **Page Color Settings**, **Language Dropdown Settings** and **Organization Logo**, and click **Save Changes**.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti applies the default color and styling settings configured in the module **Settings** globally across all privacy notices and templates. Newly created privacy notices or templates automatically inherit these settings unless you customize them individually.
