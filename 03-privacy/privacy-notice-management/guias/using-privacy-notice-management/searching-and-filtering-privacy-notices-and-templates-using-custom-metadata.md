# Searching and filtering privacy notices and templates using custom metadata

After assigning or applying custom metadata, you can search for and filter privacy notices and templates using the associated custom metadata.

To search for privacy notices or templates using custom metadata:

1. Go to **Privacy Notices** or **Templates** dashboard.
2. Near the upper left corner of the dashboard, click **+** (plus icon) to open the filter panel, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying total notices, published, and draft counts with filter options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/searching_and_filtering_notices_templates_using_custom_metadata_1.png)
3. From the left pane of the filter panel, click **Custom Metadata**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying custom metadata options and draft details for notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/searching_and_filtering_notices_templates_using_custom_metadata_3.png)
4. From the right pane of the filter panel, search for the custom metadata you want to use and mark the corresponding checkboxes.
5. Near the bottom of the filter panel, click **Apply**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying custom metadata options and draft details for notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/searching_and_filtering_notices_templates_using_custom_metadata_4.png)

Securiti lists the privacy notices or templates associated with the selected custom metadata, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying published and draft notices with their statuses and details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/searching_and_filtering_notices_templates_using_custom_metadata_5.png)
