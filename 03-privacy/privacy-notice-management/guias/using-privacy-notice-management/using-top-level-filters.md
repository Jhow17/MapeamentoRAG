# Using top-level filters

The top-level filter lets you combine different properties of the privacy notices and apply filters accordingly. It takes precedence over other filters, working from the top down and determining the result values in the grid view.

To use the top-level filter in Privacy Notice Management:

1. Near the upper left corner of the Dashboard, click **+** (plus icon) to open the filter panel, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to filter and add new notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_1.png)
2. From the filter panel, search for privacy notices using the following properties.

| Property | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| ID | Filters privacy notices by unique ID. - Use the **Is equal to** operator to match a specific notice ID. |
| Organization | Filters privacy notices by organization, such as Default Organization or a user-defined organization. - Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more organizations. |
| Owner | Filters privacy notices by owner to show privacy notices based on the notice owner, such as `sam@democo.com` or `bob@democo.com`. - Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more owners. |
| Created Date | Filters privacy notices by **Created Date** to show privacy notices based on their creation date. - Use the **between** operator to specify a date range. |
| Wizard | Filters privacy notices by **Wizard** to show privacy notices based on wizard name, such as Global Template or AI Notice Template. - Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more wizards. |
| Template | Filters privacy notices by **Template** to show privacy notices based on template name. - Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more template names. |
| Status | Filters notices by **Status** to show privacy notices based on notice status, such as Archived or Published. - Use the **is one of** operator to select one or more statuses. Securiti shows the following statuses for privacy notices: - Published - Ready to Publish - Needs Approval - Awaiting Approval - Pending Translations - Syncing Translations - Syncing Notice - Incomplete Sections - Archived |
| Custom Metadata | Filters privacy notices by custom metadata. - Use **Is one of** operator to select one or more values to include in the results. - Use **Is not one of** operator to select one or more values to exclude from the results. |

For instance, if you want to filter privacy notices by **Template** (property), from the left pane of the filter panel, select **Template**, then from the right pane mark the checkboxes next to the template names.
3. Repeat the same step for other properties according to your search needs. Once you apply all required properties, near the bottom of the filter panel, click **Apply,** as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/top_level_filter(1).png)

Securiti builds a query based on your selected properties and displays the filtered results, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notice management with filters for owner, template, and status.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_2(1).png)
4. To clear the top-level filter, from the upper right corner, click **Clear Search**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with filters for owner, template, and status.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_2.png)

## Saving views

To save a view for your set filters and search results:

1. On the Privacy Notice Management Dashboard, near the upper right corner of the page, click **More** (three dots), and choose **Save View** from the menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to save views and manage templates.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_3.png)
2. On the **Save View** panel, enter a name and description, and select a group of views or add a new one. Then, at the bottom of the panel, click **Save View** to save your view, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with filters for owner, template, and status.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_4.png)

## Using the saved views

You can access and apply your saved views at any time through the **Saved View** panel.

1. Near the upper right corner of the Dashboard, click **More** (three dots), and choose **Saved View** from the menu, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to save views and manage notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_saved_views.png)
2. Clicking **Saved View** opens a panel for saved filters on the left side. The panel displays saved groups and views, as shown below.

![Overview of saved views for privacy notices with their statuses and modification dates.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_5.png)

Click any group to view the list of saved views for that group.
3. Click the view name, or next to the view name click **Options** (three dots), and choose **View** from the menu to apply the saved filter, as shown below.

![Overview of privacy notices with statuses and options for viewing, editing, or deleting.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_apply_view.png)

## Managing the saved views

To update a saved filters, their group, or the names and descriptions associated with them:

1. Near the upper right corner of the Dashboard, click **More** (three dots), and from the menu, select **Saved View**.
2. Clicking **Saved View** opens up a panel for saved views on the left side of the page. The panel displays custom groups of saved views.
3. Next to the group name, click **Options** (three dots), and choose **Rename** or **Delete** from the menu to perform the desired action, as shown below.

![Overview of saved views for PDPL and LGPD notices with status details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_y.png)
4. Similarly, click any group to view the list of saved views. Next to the view name, click **Options** (three dots), and choose **Edit** or **Delete** from the menu to perform the desired action, as shown below.

![List of privacy notices with options to view, edit, or delete each entry.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_top_level_filter_z.png)
