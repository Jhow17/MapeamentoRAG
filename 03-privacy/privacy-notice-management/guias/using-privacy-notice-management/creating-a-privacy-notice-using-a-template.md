# Creating a privacy notice using a template

You can create a privacy notice using a [custom template](/latest/docs/creating-and-managing-privacy-notice-templates-3) tailored to your organization’s requirements. Use your template as a starting point and modify the notice as needed before publishing.

To create a privacy notice using a template:

1. In Securiti, open Privacy Notice Management, and choose **Dashboards > Privacy Notices**.
2. Near the upper right corner of the dashboard, click **+ Add Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to filter and add new notices.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_1.png)
3. From the **Create New Privacy Notice** popup, click **Start From Template** card, and then **Start**, as shown below.

![Options to create a new privacy notice using various templates and methods.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_2.png)
4. From the **Privacy Notice Templates** page, search your template and to the right of the template row, next to **Preview**, click **Use Template**, as shown below.

![Selection screen for privacy notice templates with options to preview and use templates.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_3.png)
5. In the **Start From Template** popup, update or enter a new **Notice Name**, add **Description**, **Choose Organization**, and click **Create Notice,** as shown below.

![Form for creating a privacy notice with fields for name, description, and organization.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_4.png)

Securiti opens the template in the notice editor.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The maximum character limit for a privacy notice name is **64 characters**.
6. (Optional) You can also rename the notice within the notice editor. Near the left edge of the header, next to the privacy notice name, click **Edit** (pencil icon), edit the name to suit, then click **Save** (checkmark icon).

If you do not rename the privacy notice, Securiti assigns the notice a default name based on the date you create it.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> You can also preview your template before using it. On the **Notice Management Templates** page, search your template and to the right of the template row, next to **Use Template**, click **Preview**, as shown below.
> 
> ![List of privacy notice templates with options to preview or use each template.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_5(1).png)

In the notice editor, you can add [Co-owners](/latest/docs/adding-and-removing-privacy-notice-co-owners) and [Approvers](/latest/docs/using-approval-workflows-for-privacy-notices) to collaborate with them through [Messages](/latest/docs/using-messages), as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notice sections including information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_6.png)

To modify the content of your privacy notice, manage translations in different languages, and customize its appearance, see:

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti lets you add custom metadata to filter, organize, and manage your privacy notices. To assign custom metadata to your privacy notices and search or filter them using custom metadata, see:
> 
> - [Assigning custom metadata](/latest/docs/assigning-custom-metadata)
> - [Searching and filtering privacy notices and templates using custom metadata](https://helpcenter.securiti.ai/docs/searching-and-filtering-privacy-notices-and-templates-using-custom-metadata)
  - [Understanding privacy notice formatting tools](/latest/docs/understanding-privacy-notice-formatting-tools)
  - [Managing language translation for privacy notices](/latest/docs/managing-language-translations-for-privacy-notices)
  - [Customizing privacy notices](/latest/docs/customizing-privacy-notices)
7. After making the changes, near the upper right corner of the notice editor, next to **Preview**, click **Save & Publish**, and then **Save Changes** to save the updates to your notice, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notice sections and options to save or publish changes.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_7.png)
8. To preview the notice, near upper right corner of the notice editor, next to **Save & Publish**, click **Preview**, and from the menu, select **Preview Draft**, as shown below.

![Document preview showing sections on information collection, usage, and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/preview.png)

Publish the notice after applying the required changes to the notice.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> You can only publish your privacy notice when its status is **Ready to Publish,** as shown below.
> 
> ![Dashboard displaying privacy notice sections ready for publication and detailed information collection.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_8.png)
9. To publish the privacy notice, near the upper right corner of the page, next to **Preview**, click **Save & Publish**, and then **Publish Notice**, as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notice sections and options to publish changes.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/creating_notice_template_9.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If the **Publish Notice** button is disabled, it is probably because you have not filled in some required information. In the sections list, find the numerical badges that are not green and click them to provide the required information.

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> Securiti does not let you publish privacy notices if the **required** custom metadata fields have missing values.
> 
> To add the missing custom metadata details, near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish**, and then click **Missing Custom Metadata**, as shown below.
> 
> ![Privacy notice dashboard showing sections for information collection and data sharing.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/missing_custom_metatdata_1(2).png)
> 
> Enter a value for each required custom metadata field.
10. To deploy the privacy notice after publishing, near the upper-right corner of the notice editor, next to **Preview**, click **More** (three dots icon), and from the menu, select either **Copy Hosted Link** or **Copy Embedded Link**, based on the deployment requirement, as shown below.

![Privacy notice document outlining information collection, usage, and data sharing policies.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/publish_embed_privacy_notice_links.png)
11. Work with your website team to deploy or embed that link onto your website so visitors can access your privacy notice.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If your website embeds Securiti's privacy notice using an `iframe`, you can dynamically adjust the `iframe` height on the parent page using the following script:
> 
> ```javascript
> const iframe = document.getElementById('iframe_id');
> window.addEventListener("message", function(event) {
> 	if(iframe && event && event.data && event.data.type === "IFRAME_CONTENT_SIZE" ) {
> 		iframe.style.height = event.data.payload.height + 'px';
> 	}
> });
> ```
> 
> In the parent page script, you must replace the `iframe_id` with the ID of your embedded `iframe`.
> 
> The script on the parent page sets the embedded `iframe`‘s height using the content height that Securiti’s `iframe` sends, ensuring a scroll-free experience.

On the published privacy notice, your data subjects can enable accessibility settings to improve content visibility and readability. Near the lower right corner of your privacy notice, they can access these settings by clicking the **Web Accessibility** icon, as shown below.

![Overview of data collection, usage, and sharing practices in a structured format.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/accessibility_settings_1.png)

From the **Accessibility Settings** popup, they can enable **Enable high contrast for increased visibility** toggle, and click **Apply Settings** to switch the content to high contrast mode, as shown below.

![Accessibility settings for enabling high color contrast for improved visibility in documents.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/accessibility_settings_2(1).png)

## Configuring privacy notice settings

You can use general settings to manage the notice header title, allowed domains, and the display layout of the privacy notice.

To configure the general settings:

1. From the upper right corner to the page, click **More**, and from the menu, select **Settings**.
2. From the **General** tab, configure the following settings:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy Notice Header Title | Enter the title that appears at the top of the privacy notice. |
| Embeddable Domains | To build a list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice, must type the full URLs of domains with prefixes such as `https://`, `http://`, and `www`, etc. to add to the list, and press **Enter**, as shown below: - Acceptable domain URLs: - `https://www.example.com` - `http://www.example.com` - `www.example.com` - Unacceptable domain URLs: - `acme.com` - `example.com` - `social.com` ![Settings for privacy notice customization, including embeddable domains and display options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_1.png) |
| Notice View | Customize the layout and display settings of the privacy notice page through the following options: - **Enable collapsible mode​​** **for sections** displays the privacy notice in an accordion-style layout, making navigation easier. - **Hide table of content for mobile view** hides the table of contents on mobile for a cleaner, more focused experience. - **Hide table of content for desktop view** hides the table of contents on desktop. - **Enable thumbnail view for sections** to enable a tiled layout, making content easier to browse. ![Settings for privacy notice customization including collapsible sections and content visibility options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/configuring_pn_2(1).png) |
3. To reset the notice settings to the default module-level settings, near the bottom left corner of the **Notice Settings**, click **Reset to Default**, as shown below.

![Settings page for data protection notice with options for collapsible sections and visibility.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_1.png)
4. From the **Reset to App Settings** popup, click **Reset Settings**, as shown below.

![Settings menu displaying options for privacy notice and app settings reset.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/default_2.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti applies only the **Color & Styling** changes when resetting the notice settings to the default module-level settings. Update the following settings manually if needed:
> 
> - Added languages
> - Font selections
> - Translation sources
5. To configure the default settings, see [Customizing privacy notices](/latest/docs/customizing-your-privacy-notices).

## Unlinking a privacy notice from a template

You can unlink a privacy notice from its template when you no longer want template updates to apply to the notice.

To unlink a privacy notice from a template:

1. Go to the **Privacy Notices** dashboard.
2. In the list of privacy notices, on the far left of the privacy notice row, click **Options** (three dots).
3. From the **Options** menu, select **Unlink From Template**, as shown below.

![Privacy Notice Dashboard displaying published, draft, and archived notices with actions available.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/unlink_notice_from_template_2.png)
4. From the **Unlink Privacy Notice** popup, click **Unlink Notice** to unlink the privacy notice from its template, as shown below.

![Dialog box for unlinking a privacy notice from its template in the dashboard.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/unlink_notice_from_template_3.png)

In the Privacy Notice Management module settings, you can include details about embeddable domains. Here, you can enter the list of domains where you wish to embed your privacy notice(s).
