# Using organization-level access

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you must manage privacy notices across multiple business units while keeping records clearly separated for governance and reporting. However, when all privacy notices are managed within a single account, it becomes difficult to distinguish which notices belong to which business unit and to track performance or updates independently. For instance, if your organization operates separate retail, healthcare, and financial services divisions, each unit may require its own privacy notices aligned with different regulations and operational needs. With Securiti’s multi-organization feature, you can create distinct organizations within your Securiti tenant, each maintaining its own privacy notices and related statistics. You can also migrate privacy notices between organizational units when needed, enabling better structure, clearer oversight, and more flexible management of privacy records across your enterprise.

Securiti now has a new multi-organization feature for **Privacy Notice** **Management**. This feature lets you create and manage different accounts within your Securiti tenant. These accounts are called ‘organizations’, have no hierarchy with each other, and are independent with their own data.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> This feature is available by request; contact the Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to have it activated for your Securiti tenant.

In **Privacy Notice** **Management** module, the following data is unique for each organization:

- Privacy notices statistics

You can migrate the following from one organization to another:

- Privacy notices

### Migrating data between organizations

For **Privacy Notice** **Management**, you can migrate the privacy notices from one organization to another.

1. In Securiti, go to **Privacy Notice** **Management**.
2. From the privacy notices grid view, click **Options** (three dots) at the end of the privacy notice you want to migrate and choose **Edit Organization,** as shown below.

![Dashboard displaying privacy notices with options to edit, publish, and manage translations.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_organization_level_access(1).png)
3. On the **Edit Organization** popup, choose the organization you want to migrate the privacy notice to, and click **Save,** as shown below.

![Editing organization settings for Privacy Notice management in a dashboard interface.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/using_organization_level_access_2.png)
