# Using approval workflows for privacy notices

When creating or updating a privacy notice, you can designate one or more approvers, at least one of whom must approve the notice before you can publish it.

To use an approval workflow for a privacy notice:

1. Open **Privacy Notice** **Management** and create a new notice or open an existing one for editing.
2. To the left of the Co-owners tool, use the **Approvers** tool to add at least one approver to the notice, as shown below.

![User approval section in a privacy notice document with listed approvers' names and emails.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/approval_workflows_for_privacy_notices_1.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> - If you do not designate any approvers, you can publish the notice immediately as usual.
> - If this feature is not available in your Securiti tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan.
3. Near the upper right corner of the page, click on **Save & Publish,** from the menu click **Save Changes** and then **Submit for Review** to submit the notice for review.

![Privacy notice management dashboard showing company information and submission options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/approval_workflows_for_privacy_notices_2(1).png)

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> Securiti does not let you submit a privacy notice for review if **required** custom metadata fields have missing values.
> 
> To add the missing custom metadata details, near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save & Publish**, and then click **Missing Custom Metadata**.
> 
> ![Privacy notice dashboard showing sections for information collection and approval status.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/submit_for_review_disable.png)
> 
> Enter a value for each required custom metadata field.
4. The notice status changes to **Awaiting Approval**, and Securiti sends notifications to the approvers both in the Securiti messaging center and by email, as shown below.

![Privacy notice document showing company information and data protection details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/approval_workflows_for_privacy_notices_3.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can add existing Securiti users, or anyone with an email address within your domain. If you add someone without a Securiti user account, Securiti creates a user account for them.

Near the upper right corner, the approver clicks **Save & Publish**, then selects **Approve Notice** from the menu, as shown below.

![Privacy notice document with sections and approval options highlighted for review.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-02-04 at 3.27.28 PM.png)
5. After the approver approves the notice, the notice status changes to **Ready to Publish**. Click **Save & Publish**, and then choose **Publish Notice** from the menu to publish the approved notice.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If a notice has multiple approvers, approval from one approver is sufficient to proceed with publishing.

Approvers in the Privacy Notice Management module play a crucial role in validating and ensuring the appropriateness of the given information in the privacy notice(s).
