# Working with Assessment Templates

Securiti lets you build custom assessment templates that you can use when our standard templates don't fulfill your specific needs. You can either build a new template from scratch or build from multiple existing templates and modify it to suit your needs. However, Securiti does not populate all assessment categories by default.

Also, any risk assessment templates only apply to internal assessments where you must choose risk assessment from the regulation category.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Risk assessment is not available to all accounts; contact your Securiti representative for details and to activate this feature for your Securiti account.
