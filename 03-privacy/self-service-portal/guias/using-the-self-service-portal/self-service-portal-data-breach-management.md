# Data Breach Management

Using the Data Breach Management module you can easily report the breach incidents. Securiti publishes a breach intake form on your self-service portal that anyone with an email address in your company domain can use to report an incident See [Reporting data breach incidents](/v1/docs/reporting-data-breach-incidents). In either case, the breach intake form collects basic information about the breach, its timeline, and contacts that the incident manager can use to investigate and manage the incident.
