# Reporting data breach incidents

If you have an email address within your organization's domain, you can use your organization's Securiti self-service portal to report a data breach incident, even if you do not have a Securiti user or admin account, you can access the self-service portal as a guest user.

### Reporting a data breach

To report a data breach incident as a guest user:

1. Open your organization's Securiti self-service portal and enter your email address.
2. The system redirects you to the identity provider’s (Google or Facebook) login screen where you provide your email address and password to login to the self-service portal.
3. On the self-service portal landing page, click **Incidents** tab.

![incident_tab.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3f74d721-4fdf-02da-dd61-5865d18ef13e.png)
4. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Report New Incident**.

![report_a_new_incident.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-7fe10c94-80a4-9544-da7c-6f70381d391c.png)
5. On the Incident Details page, fill out the form with as much detail about the incident as you can provide.

![Basic_info_area.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-8bafee34-7411-ca45-77e8-f0b35087dbd3(1).png)

At the very least, you must provide an Incident Name.

Click **Upload Attachment** to add documents and files that must be sent as evidence with the breach intake form. At the bottom of the form, click **Report New Incident**.

![Upload_attachment.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-55ed944a-be1c-bbbd-457c-ce37243a00f8.png)
6. The portal redirects you to a page that shows all of the current and past incidents you have reported.

![NEW_INCIDENT_REPORTED.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f1461a44-c37b-04ee-13ce-5056303f77b4.png)

### Tracking incident progress

To track the progress of an incident you reported:

1. Login to the self-service portal using your email address.
2. On the self-service portal landing page, click **Incidents** tab return to the page showing the incidents you have reported.
3. Click the entry for the incident to view its details.
