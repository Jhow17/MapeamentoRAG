# Using the self-service portal

With Securiti's self-service portal, members of your organization who do not have their own Securiti accounts can:

- Initiate [assessments](/v1/docs/assessment-automation) from published assessment templates
- Report [data breach incidents](/v1/docs/data-breach-management) into the incident workbench
- Monitor the progress of all ongoing assessments and/or incidents
