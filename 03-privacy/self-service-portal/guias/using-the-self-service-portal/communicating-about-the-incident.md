# Communicating about the incident

Each incident has a communication side panel that lets incident owners and data breach reporters communicate with each other. As a breach reporter, the communication panel also lets you share incident attachments with the incident owner.

To communicate with others working on the incident:

1. Login to the self-service portal using your email address.
2. On the self-service portal landing page, click **Incidents** tab to return to the page showing the incidents you have reported.
3. Click the entry for an incident.
4. Near the upper right corner of the incident header, click the **Messages** icon as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-HXEFCA5O.png)
5. Use the messages panel to view or send messages regarding the incident.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-WY5XE38Q.png)
