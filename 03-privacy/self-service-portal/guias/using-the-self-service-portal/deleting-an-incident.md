# Deleting an incident

If you reported an incident, you can delete it from within the self-service portal. Doing so removes the incident from the self-service portal, but does not delete it from your organization's data breach management system. Only an administrator for your organization's Securiti account can do that.

To delete an incident from the self-service portal:

1. Login to the self-service portal using your email address.
2. On the self-service portal landing page, click **Breach Management** to return to the page showing the incidents you have reported.
3. At the right end of the incident choose **Options > Delete**, then click **Delete Incident** to confirm as shown below.
