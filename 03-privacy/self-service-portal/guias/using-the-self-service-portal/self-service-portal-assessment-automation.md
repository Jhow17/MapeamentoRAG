# Assessment Automation

Assessment Automation lets you manage *Internal assessments* that you conduct within your own organization. See [initiate SSP assessment](/latest/docs/start-an-assessment).

There are several kinds of internal assessment, including:

- Those you conduct to evaluate the readiness of your company’s internal departments and divisions--you then publish these and share them with customers and clients to show your readiness. Based on the results of an assessment, we assign a *Readiness Rating*, a percentage that shows you at a glance the level of readiness reflected by the assessment. These assessments help you evaluate how ready your organization and your vendors are to comply with privacy regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
- Those you conduct to collect information that you use to create or enrich data catalog items with information from the assessment responses. Once you publish such an assessment, the information collected is added to the data catalog entry for the asset or process.
- Those you conduct to quickly collect all available information about a reported data breach incident. You can then use the information collected to determine whether the incident qualifies as a data breach, and evaluate the need for notification.
