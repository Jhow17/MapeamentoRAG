# Reassigning domains between domain groups

You can reassign a domain group to your domains in bulk, and also create a new domain group at the same time when reassigning a domain group. Securiti lets you reassign the domain group from:

- The **Cookie Consent Domains** page that lists all your domains.
- The **Cookie Configuration** page, from the **Basic Information** tab of your particular domain using the **Edit** mode, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2024-01-09_at_3_54_29_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-fc46868b-0f82-a299-a0f6-04e4d343795c.png)

## Procedure

To reassign a domain group to your domains:

1. In Securiti, open **Cookie Consent Management**, and choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**.
2. Mark the checkboxes next to the domains you want to reassign a domain group, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/scanning_bulk_0(3).png)
3. (Optional) To select multiple domains at once, from the upper left corner of the domain list headers, mark the checkbox next to the Domain/Subdomain column header, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/scanning_bulk_2(2).png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> The Cookie Consent Domains page initially displays 10 domains, with pagination set at 10 domains per page. To change the number of displayed domains per page, from the bottom left corner of the page, click the menu next to **Showing**. You can choose to view 10, 25, or 50 domains per page.

Securiti displays the number of currently selected domains above the domain list, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/domain_list_4(3).png)
  1. Click **Select all across all pages** to select all domains across the entire pagination. For instance, if you are viewing 10 domains per page and have 100 domains spread across 10 pages, this option selects all 100 domains at once.
  2. Click **Select all on this page** to select all domains currently visible on the page. For instance, if you are viewing 10 domains per page and have 100 domains across 10 pages, this option selects only the 10 domains on the current page.
  3. Click **Select none** to not select any domain.
4. From the action toolbar above the domain list, click **Reassign Domain Group**, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/reassigne_1.png)
5. On the **Change Domain Group** confirmation popup, from the **Select Destination Domain Group** menu:

After successfully assigning a different or new domain group, Securiti displays a confirmation message near the upper right corner of the Cookie Consent Domains or Cookie Configuration page, depending on where you changed the domain group.
  1. To assign an existing domain group name, from the menu select one existing domain group, and click **Apply Change**, as shown below. ![Reassign_domains-6.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f3f46656-1fe7-156d-0926-d27d74183679.png) ![Reassign_domains-7.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b11138a9-7d23-52e5-b9cb-78ead8680545.png)
  2. To create a new domain group for the domains, type a unique new name for the domain group in the text field, click **+ Add New Domain Group** to add it, and then click **Apply Change**, as shown below.

![Reassign_domains_new_group_v2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-50746d7c-8f4b-4e15-84ba-b297600516e3.png)
