# Managing cookie policies

Each cookie policy is a combination of an inventory of the cookies dropped by a website and the categorizations you assign to those cookies.

The Securiti cookie consent tools let you display a Securiti-generated banner on your website that advises visitors that the site uses cookies. One option available for this banner lets the visitor consent to specific categories of cookies, and lists the cookies in each category. The cookies and categories come from the cookie policy you maintain using the consent management tools.

To comply with privacy regulations such as GDPR, your banner must correctly reflect the cookies being dropped. Each time the website cookies change, you must update the cookie policy so that it updates the banner to reflect the latest cookies. Any website visitor who consented to a previous version of the policy is shown the banner again and asked to consent to the latest cookies.

To update a cookie policy:

1. In Securiti, open **Cookie Consent Management** and choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**.
2. Click the domain for which you want to update the cookie policy, then click the **Website Scan & Report** tab.
3. Click **Reinitiate Website Scan**.

The scan may take up to a few minutes to complete.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If the scan is marked as "Failed," it might mean that you need to [configure cookie scanner allow-listing](/v1/docs/configuring-cookie-scanner-allow-listing).
4. Click the **Categorize Cookies** tab.
5. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Edit**.

Note that Securiti marks the cookie policy as Draft in the Website Domain panel, as shown below. This shows you that you have not yet published the new cookie policy.

![Cookie_policy_draft.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-5e67c700-b717-5d79-c29d-7910f7cc681c.png)
6. Categorize the cookies as described in [Categorizing your website cookies](/v1/docs/categorizing-your-website-cookies), then click **Save**.
7. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Publish**.

Securiti marks the policy as Published, and advances the policy version number, as shown below.

![Cookie_policy_published.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-37ff854a-1e29-a5bc-4745-18800077d0af.png)
8. To review previous versions of the cookie policy, click the version number and choose a version from the menu.

Note that Securiti marks such versions as Archived to let you know that they are not current.

![Cookie_consent_archived.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a1489f82-5260-95e5-e2dd-c17e394f4445.png)
9. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Cancel** to return to the Cookie Consent page.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an European Union (EU) regulation on information privacy. It aims to ensure the protection of personal information through a human rights-centric approach, and imposes obligations onto organizations anywhere, so long as they target or collect data related to people in the EU.

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
