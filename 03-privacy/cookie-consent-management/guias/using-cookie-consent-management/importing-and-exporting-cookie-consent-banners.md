# Importing and exporting cookie consent banners

#### Importing and exporting cookie consent banners

You can export a domain's cookie banners including all the selected countries plus all other design configurations for that banner. You can then import it to another cookie domain. Doing so overrides the cookie banners by country for the cookie domain.

### Exporting cookie consent banners

1. In Securiti open **Cookie Consent Management**, choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**, and click a domain.
2. Click the **Customize Banner and Preference Center** tab, and from the Banner Intelligence panel on the left side, click **Download** (down arrow) icon as shown below.

![Export-cookie-banner-1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d9ed6af1-85fc-5272-a42e-ab6fb89cf183.png)
3. On the **Export Banner** popup, mark the checkbox for the cookie consent banner that you want, and click **Export** to export it as shown below.

![Export-cookie-banner-popup.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-fb417cb5-d015-f471-cad1-49c87dd46b64.png)

You can select multiple checkboxes if you want to download more than one cookie banner.

Clicking **Export** downloads a JSON file containing your cookie consent banner in your local machine.

### Importing cookie consent banners

1. In Securiti open **Cookie Consent Management**, choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**, and click a domain.
2. Click the **Customze Banner and Preference Center** tab, and near the upper right corner of the page click **Edit**.
3. From the the Banner Intelligence panel on the left side, click **Add** (+) icon, and choose **Import** as shown below.

![Import-cookie-banner-1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1f858bf0-37a4-16db-cc81-9797d7671721.png)

![Import-cookie-banner-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-79cf6dd7-a56e-0075-ec75-0fc91e6ab820.png)
4. On the **Import Banner** popup, either drag and drop the JSON file containing your cookie consent banner or upload it. Once done, near the bottom of the popup, click **Import** to import your cookie consent banner as shown below.

![Import-cookie-banner-3.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d86a7e26-e995-9379-aaf8-ed7554f47982.png)

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
