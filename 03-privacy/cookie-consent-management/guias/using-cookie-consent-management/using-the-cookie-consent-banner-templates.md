# Using the cookie consent banner templates

Securiti lets you select different cookie consent banner templates to fully comply with different privacy regulations. You can choose a banner template from the list of predefined cookie consent banner templates. This banner template contains appropriate placeholder texts to help you properly configure your cookie consent preference banner, and to comply with the regional regulations.

To choose a predefined cookie consent banner template:

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose Cookie Consent > Domains.
2. On the Cookie Consent Domains page, click the domain you want the banner for.
3. Click the Customize Banner and Preference Center tab. Securiti lands you on the Configuration & Mapping subtab by default.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(94).png)

1. From the upper right corner, click Edit, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(95).png)
2. From the upper right corner of the left panel, click + (plus icon), and select Select from Gallery, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(96).png)
3. On the Cookie Banner Templates popup, mark the checkbox for the regulation you want to comply with. You can also select more than one banner depending upon your organizational requirements of complying with one or many regulations.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(97).png)
4. Near the bottom of the popup, click Import to configure this banner template to suit.

For more information, see [Customizing your cookie banner and preference center](/v1/docs/securiti-pods-and-elastic-pods)

### Using cookie banner templates from Banner Templates Gallery

You can also import cookie banner templates directly from the Banner Templates Gallery directly from the Customize Banner and Preference Center tab. This is an easy and seamless way of importing cookie consent banner templates that you have already configured in the Banner Templates Gallery. The gallery of banner templates contains system and custom cookie banner templates. For more information, see [Using the Banner Templates Gallery](/v1/docs/securiti-pods-and-elastic-pods).

- You must have Banner Templates Gallery functionality enabled for your Securiti tenant, as shown below.

- You must have one or more System or Custom cookie banner templates present in the banner templates gallery, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(99).png)

### Procedure

To import cookie consent banners templates from the Banner Templates Gallery:

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose Cookie Consent > Domains.
2. On the Cookie Consent Domains page, click the domain you want the banner for.
3. Click the Customize Banner and Preference Center tab. Securiti lands you on the Configuration & Mapping subtab by default.
4. From the upper right corner, click Edit, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(100).png)

1. From the upper right corner of the left panel, click + (plus icon), and select Select from Gallery, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(101).png)
2. On the Banner Templates Gallery popup, mark the checkbox next to the cookie banner template that you want to import in your domain. You can also select more than one banner templates depending upon your organizational requirements, as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(102).png)

1. Near the bottom of the popup, click Import to import the selected system or custom banner template(s), as shown below.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image(103).png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The Banner Templates Gallery popup initially loads and displays 50 banner templates, with Pagination set at 50 templates per page. To view the rest banner templates, you must scroll down to the bottom of the popup, and click Next (> icon), as shown below.
>

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.

A preference center helps organizations immediately and adequately honor consent preferences and consent revocations of their data subjects across multiple systems.

Pagination is a way of organizing content on a page by breaking it into smaller, manageable sections or pages. It helps in presenting information more clearly and lets the users navigate through different parts of the content, typically with the use of page numbers or navigation buttons. For instance, pagination is like turning the pages of a book to access more information instead of displaying everything at once.
