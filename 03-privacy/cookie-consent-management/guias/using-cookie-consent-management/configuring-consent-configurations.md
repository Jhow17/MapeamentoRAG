# Configuring consent configurations

Securiti lets you configure general settings for your domains in your Securiti tenant from this page. For instance, you can enable bot detection and encode the value of cookies dropped by Securiti for all your domains from these settings.

### Bot Detection

These tools let you detect and discard the consent traffic generated by bots or spiders by only letting you access a clean stream of consents only generated by your data subjects. Securiti detects the following bots currently:

- Baiduspider
- Bingbot
- Discordbot
- DuckDuckBot
- Googlebot
- LinkedIn bot
- SemrushBot
- Slackbot
- Twitter bot
- Yandex bot

To enable bot detection on your domains:

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management and choose **Advanced Settings > Module Configurations**.
2. From the **Cookie Consent Configurations** section, enable the **Bot Detection** toggle.
3. From the upper right corner of the page, click **Save** as shown below.

![CCM_bot_detection.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a6d9f7b8-f27c-8173-9ce1-686c5e207466.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti applies this setting to all of your domains in your Securiti tenant.

### Encoding Cookie values

These tools let you encode the values of cookies that are dropped by Securiti. We encode the values of these cookies in the ASCII encoding standard.

To encode the values of Securiti dropped cookies in your domains:

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management and choose **Advanced Settings > Module Configurations**.
2. From the **Cookie Consent Configurations** section, enable the **Encode Cookie values** toggle.
3. From the upper right corner of the page, click **Save** as shown below.

![CCM_encoding_cookie_values.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-bac34ba8-f55b-124b-ecfc-a85c048596b7.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You must revoke consent for your existing domains to encode the values of cookies present in them.

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
