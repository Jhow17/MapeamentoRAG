# Using Cookie Consent Management

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This module is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

Individual consent is a core privacy principle that features prominently in all privacy directives and regulations. It puts individuals in control of how their personal data is collected and used. In most data protection regulations, consent is one of the legal bases under which organizations can legally process personal data.

Collecting consent from website visitors and the data subjects of your organization or business for cookies and other tracking technologies on websites and digital environments is challenging. Use Securiti’s Cookie Consent Management to collect consent for the dropping of cookies on visitors' browsers. Securiti also has a related set of tools for collecting form consent; see [Using Universal Consent Management](/v1/docs/using-universal-consent-management).

With Securiti's Cookie Consent Management module, you can completely automate your cookie consent lifecycle from scanning to reporting. With Cookie Consent Management module, you can:

- [Scan the domains and subdomains of your organization](/latest/docs/scanning-your-website-for-cookies)
- [Auto-classify cookies found on your domain and understand their purpose](/latest/docs/categorizing-your-website-cookies)
- [Build custom cookie banner and preference center for your domain(s)](/v1/docs/customizing-your-cookie-banner-and-preference-center)
- [Enable your data subjects to make consent choices and block cookies](/latest/docs/automatically-blocking-scripts-from-dropping-cookies)
- [Deploy cookie banner and preference center on your domain(s)](/latest/docs/deploying-your-website-cookie-consent-banner)
- [Collect consent from your data subjects based on global laws and regulations](/latest/docs/configuring-iab-consent-frameworks)
- [Develop reports and ensure global compliance](/latest/docs/exporting-and-scheduling-cookie-consent-reports-and-metrics#scheduling-a-cookie-report)
- [Integrate the Cookie Consent Management module with third-party tools such as Google Tag Manager](/latest/docs/integrating-securiti-with-google-tag-manager)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Securiti currently does not support the multi-org functionality for the Cookie Consent Management module.
