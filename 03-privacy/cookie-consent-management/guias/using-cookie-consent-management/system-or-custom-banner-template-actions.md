# System or custom banner template actions

Securiti also lets you perform the following actions on your imported system templates and newly created custom banner templates:

- Clone system and custom banner template(s)
- Edit custom banner template(s)
- Delete system and custom banner template(s)
- Update system banner template(s)

### Cloning a system or custom banner template

To clone your system or custom banner template(s):

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose **Cookie Consent > Banners Template Gallery**.
2. From the right side of your system or custom banner template, click **options** (three dots), and select **Clone** as shown below.

![Clone-template.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-16a31836-9cce-18a2-a321-7008aa505860.png)
3. On the **Clone Template** popup, enter a unique name for your banner template, and click **Clone** as shown below.

![Clone-template-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d187a12c-8cfc-1489-6600-a482eda16eaf.png)

### Editing a custom banner template

To edit your custom banner template(s):

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> Securiti only lets you edit your custom created and cloned banner templates. You cannot edit the system banner templates.

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose **Cookie Consent > Banner Templates Gallery**.
2. From the right side of your custom banner template, click **options** (three dots), and select **Edit**, as shown below.

![Edit-template.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-54fae164-30b9-a27e-ecc4-7a5e3cbefbbe.png)
3. On the **Edit Template** page, make changes to banner template, and once you are satisfied with the changes, from the upper right corner of the page, click **Save** to save your edits, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2024-01-16_at_7_15_33_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-35a4761f-b2e0-4522-942e-9b8282218727.png)

For more information when editing a cloned or custom banner template, see [Customizing your website cookie consent banner](/v1/docs/customizing-your-cookie-banner-and-preference-center).

### Deleting a system or custom banner template

To delete your system or custom banner template(s):

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> After applying a banner template on domain(s), deleting it from the templates gallery does not erase the applied changes from the domain(s). Securiti only deletes the banner template from the gallery; your updates remain as they are on the domains.

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose **Cookie Consent > Banner Templates Gallery**.
2. From the right side of your system or custom banner template, click **options** (three dots), and select **Delete**, as shown below.

![Delete-template.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d72f2d3d-439e-cd4a-26a3-ab9dfdb65461.png)
3. On the confirmation popup, click **Yes, continue** to delete your system or custom banner template, as shown below.

![Delete-template-confirm.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a905c1c9-5c97-aa1b-3d60-b3b3e2d1bf13.png)

### Updating the system banner templates

You can also update the system banner templates that you have already imported in the banner templates gallery whenever Securiti updates the system cookie banner templates to comply with the latest regulation changes. You must update your existing system banner templates from the gallery to ensure they comply with the most recent regulatory changes. When you update these templates, Securiti automatically upgrades them to the newest version available at that time.

When updates are available for the system banner templates, we show:

- A red dot next to the **Update All Templates** button as a visual indicator for you that updates are available for the system banner templates.
- An "Update Available" indicator with the names of respective system cookie banner templates that you’ve imported and are already using, as shown below.

![Update-banner-templates-indicator.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f225ff85-3efa-476c-a539-077905aa3ee2.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> - Securiti only lets you update system banner templates that you have already imported in your banner templates gallery. The update action takes the system banner templates to the latest available version at the moment.
> - Securiti doesn't automatically apply the latest template changes to domains using system cookie banner templates. After updating the system cookie banner templates, ensure you deploy these changes to the domains where these system cookie banner templates are in use.

To update your system banner template(s) with the latest available version:

1. In Securiti, open Cookie Consent Management, and choose **Cookie Consent > Banner Templates Gallery**.

![Updates-available](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-72c550f1-e798-bedf-8746-06af31209f14.png)
2. From the upper right corner, click **Update All Templates**, as shown below.

![Update_banner_templates.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a8217299-ad38-4b9d-5eb0-60c6640f1206.png)
3. On the **Update System Templates with Master Gallery** popup, mark the checkboxes for the system banner templates that you want to update, and near the bottom of the popup, click **Update**, as shown below.

![Update-banner-templates-popup.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-89900489-023d-4358-097c-db794fd00ab5.png)

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
