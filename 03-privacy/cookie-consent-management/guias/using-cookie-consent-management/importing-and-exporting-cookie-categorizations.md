# Importing and exporting cookie categorizations

You can export the cookie categorization in published or draft mapping from the selected cookie domain. You can take the file and import it into another domain’s cookie categorization mapping.

Securiti can scan the current domain's cookies and apply the cookie categorization if the selected cookies match the cookie name.

### Exporting cookie categorizations

1. In Securiti open **Cookie Consent Management**, choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**, and click a domain.
2. Click the **Categorize Cookies** tab. Securiti lands you on the **Cookie & Category Details** subtab by default.
3. In the **Cookie Categories** panel along the left side of the page, click **Download** (down arrow icon), as shown below.

![Export-Cookie-Categories-1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-aca5450e-2bf3-9ac6-9ff5-bd82408b198d.png)
4. On the **Export Cookie Categories** popup, mark the checkboxes for the cookie categories you want to export, and near the bottom of the popup, click **Export**, as shown below.

![Export-Cookie-Categories-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9728ebf0-36ad-2cae-1224-224c9bd90e27.png)

> [!WARNING]
> **Warning**
> 
> If there is no match found for the cookies during the cookie categorization process, they are kept as is.

### Importing cookie categorizations

1. In Securiti open **Cookie Consent Management**, choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**, and click a domain.
2. Click the **Categorize Cookies** tab. Securiti lands you on the **Cookie & Category Details** subtab by default.
3. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Edit**.
4. In the **Cookie Categories** panel along the left side of the page, click **+**, and choose **Import**, as shown below.

![Import-Cookie-Categories-1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3e26ffbc-d7ea-c8d7-3347-299c96b98731.png)

![Import-Cookie-Categories-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a3244a09-3f78-8a86-8538-6c9da368632c.png)
5. On the **Import Cookie Categorization** popup, and upload the JSON file containing the cookie categories.
6. Once done, near the bottom of the popup, click **Import**, as shown below.

![Import-Cookie-Categories-3.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-53d5509c-d5fb-f27b-ba21-2abacc8ef8f6.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> If a cookie category does not exist in this cookie configuration, the import automatically creates it. If a cookie category with the same name already exists in the cookie configuration, the import shows a confirmation popup asking to **overwrite** the cookie category, as shown below.
> 
> ![Import-Cookie-Categories-4.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b51353af-8f24-5cd3-a239-72a5c7cbc164.png)

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
