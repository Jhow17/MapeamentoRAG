# Single cookie consent for all subdomains

When a data subject visits the base domain (primary website) of your organization and gives their consent for the very first time through the cookie preference banner, that consent is recorded against only the base domain.

For instance, if your organization or business has some other subdomains (secondary websites) and the data subject visits any other subdomain, they are asked to give their consent again through the cookie preference banner.

- In **Consent Code** tab of your scanned cookie domain, if the following toggle is enabled, upon visiting the base domain the data subject is shown the cookie preference banner and when the data subject gives consent through that banner, their consent is automatically recorded for all existing subdomains of your organization or business.

![Screenshot_2022-11-28_at_3_46_56_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6d26d05e-0913-b9b9-1380-cb5884379d32.png)
- When this toggle is disabled, the data subjects have to exclusively give consent again for the subdomains of your organization.
