# Customizing your cookie banner and preference center

You can customize the appearance of your domain's cookie banner and preference center, and also have multiple versions of the cookie banner that Securiti shows only to visitors from specific geographic locations based on their IP addresses.

1. In Securiti, open **Cookie Consent Management** and choose **Domains**.
2. On the **Domains** page, click the the domain you want the cookie banner for.
3. Click the **Customize Banner and Preference Center** tab.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If the **Customize Banner and Preference Center** tab is disabled, it may be because the current cookie policy is in a **Draft** state. Click the **Categorize Cookies** tab and check to see if the **Publish** button is enabled. If so, review and publish the policy. See [Categorizing your website cookies](/v1/docs/categorizing-your-website-cookies) and [Managing cookie policies](/v1/docs/managing-cookie-policies) for more information.
4. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Edit**.
5. Near the left edge of the page, at the top of the Banner Intelligence panel, enable the **Language** toggle to display the banner in the default language configured for the website visitor's browser.

When you enable the **Language** toggle in the Banner Intelligence panel for a cookie consent banner, Securiti uses commercially-available machine translation to automatically translate the banner.

> [!WARNING]
> **Important**
> 
> Securiti offers translation support to help localize your cookie consent experience across global audiences. However, certain terms such as **legal references**, **privacy frameworks**, and **organization** or **product names** will remain untranslated to preserve legal accuracy and brand integrity. These include, but are not limited to:
> 
> `Auti`, `AZURE`, `CCPA`, `Cookie`, `Cookies`, `CPRA`, `DIFC`, `DPL`, `DPIA`, `ePrivacy`, `GDPR`, `KYS`, `LGPD`, `PIA`, `PIPEDA`, `POPIA`, `Privacy Act`, `SCHREMS II`, and `VCDPA`.
> 
> These terms appear in their original form across all supported languages.

However, in some cases, you might find that this translation does not provide satisfactory results. In those cases, you can override the machine translation of some or all of the cookie banner's elements with your own translations as described in [Overriding banner translations with translation mapping](/v1/docs/overriding-banner-translations-with-translation-mapping).

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The **Language** feature is available by subscription for **freemium** and **lite** Securiti tenants. If the toggle does not enable, it probably means that language intelligence is not enabled for your Securiti tenant. In this case, contact your **Customer Success Manager** or **Account Representative** to confirm whether it is included in your plan.
6. Next to the **Language** toggle, use the **Trackers** toggle to enable or disable the display of cookies under each category in the cookie preferences center. When disabled, the preferences center only shows cookie categories, and the cookies themselves are hidden from view below each category.
7. To create versions of the cookie banner that Securiti displays only to your data subjects or website visitors from specific geographic location:

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> This **Location** feature is available by subscription for **freemium** and **lite** Securiti tenants. In this case, contact your **Customer Success Manager** or **Account Representative** to confirm whether it is included in your plan.
  1. Near the top of the **Banner Intelligence** panel, click **+** to create a new cookie banner for the region or location.
  2. Next to the banner name, click **Edit** (pencil icon) and change the name to suit, then click the checkmark to save the name.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> We recommend you include the applicable locations in the cookie banner name.
  3. In the Location Intelligence area of the central panel, mark the radio button for **Show only in**, and use the menu to build a list of countries and states, provinces, or regions, as shown below.

![Show-only-in.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-47dad1cd-bb9d-b22a-8f04-04ba5e465be0.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If you have a default cookie banner, it is shown in all other locations.
8. Use the remaining tools located in the **Customize Banner and Preference Center** tab to create your custom banner. Securiti lands you on the **Configuration & Mapping** sub-tab by default.

For more information, see [Best practices for cookie banners and preference centers](/v1/docs/best-practices-for-cookie-banners-and-preference-centers):
