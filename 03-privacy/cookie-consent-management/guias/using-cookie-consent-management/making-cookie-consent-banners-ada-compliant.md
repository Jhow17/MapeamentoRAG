# Making cookie consent banners ADA-compliant

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you must block non-essential cookies until data subjects’ consent preferences are recorded and ensure accessibility. For instance, when deploying a new cookie banner, manually configuring scripts for cookie blocking and adjusting contrast settings for accessibility can be time-consuming. With Securiti’s Cookie Consent Management module, you can enable auto-blocking and ADA compliance through simple toggles. This helps you deliver a lawful and inclusive experience without development delays.

You can make cookie consent banners ADA accessible. Your website visitors can use features like:

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Screen Reader Report | Banners can be accessed through screen readers. |
| Keyboard Navigation | Every element of the banners can be accessed through a keyboard, without the need for a mouse or trackpad. |

To enable ADA compliance:

1. Open **Cookie Consent Management**, choose **Cookie Consent > Domains**, and click a domain.
2. Click the **Consent Code** tab, scroll down and enable the **Enable ADA compliance** toggle as shown below.

A feature refers to one of the aspects of processing personal data within the IAB EU TCF v2.2 consent framework. These are outlined in the Policies or Specifications and are employed to achieve one or more Purposes. Unlike Purposes, the data subjects do not have a separate choice for each Feature; their choices are tied to the overarching Purposes.

A small text file that is stored on a data subject's or website visitor's device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by a domain (website). Cookies are used to collect information about the user's interactions with the website, such as preferences, login status, and browsing history. This information helps domains enhance user experience, perform analytics, and deliver personalized content and advertisements. Cookies can be categorized into various types, including Essential, Performance, Functionality, and targeting cookies, each serving different purposes in managing user consent and data privacy.
