# Vendor Ratings FAQs

This section covers frequently asked questions regarding vendor ratings.

---

### Frequestly Asked Questions

#### We would like to understand the vendor rating process better. How/what is specifically reviewed? How often it's reviewed? What weights determine the rating score.

After our team researches and review a vendor, we store their timestamp and hash, and gradually check for vendors we have not revisited for some time. This way we plan reviews, accordingly. For hash, we store hash for website's important pages and compare them with their recent version.

#### Can the vendor rating process pull information from other data sources?

Most information is extracted using vendor pages, and yes, we extract some framework information from different sources.

#### If an assessment is completed against a supplier, how does that weigh into the vendor ratings?

There is no connection between the two. Vendor ratings weigh method is separate.

#### Are all supplier assessments conducted by customers shared in the vendor ratings?

No

#### Are suppliers provided with feedback and action items regarding identified open gaps or low scope as part of supplier ratings? If yes, do they have SLA and tracking to closure?

No

#### What do other Securiti customers do for vendor assessment if a vendor rating DOES NOT exist in Securiti (fallback option)?

If a vendor rating does not exist, we receive request for their addition.

#### Can the score be updated if we have information that is missing?

We don't have a manual process for updating vendor information from the UI. If you notice any vendor information that's missing, share that with us. We can check if that is correct and push the changes from the backend.

#### Can we expand the scoring to include new AI Risks?

Vendors available in the marketplace have their scoring fixed. You can't expand it.

#### How often are new vendors added to the explorer by Securiti?

At the moment, we add new vendors based on customer requests.

#### How can we request that a new vendor be added proactively? What is the SLA? How are NDA's handled with Vendor in this scenario?

Typically, we receive requests through Zendesk or from our Product Managers, who shares the list. If only a few vendors need adding, we can do so right away.

Vendors are the companies you collaborate with and send assessments to for completion. You can also evaluate prospective vendors and track the performance of existing ones.
