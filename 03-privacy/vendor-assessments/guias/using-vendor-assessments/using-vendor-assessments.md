# Using Vendor Assessments

*Vendor assessments* are those you invite your vendors and partners to conduct and submit. Doing so helps you evaluate their readiness. You can send invitations to individual vendors, or you can bulk import multiple vendors and send multiple invitations at a time.

The Vendor Assessment tools also include our Vendor Explorer, which shows you vendors who have been specifically evaluated by the Securiti research teams. Each vendor is assigned a *Securiti rating* that you can evaluate and compare with other vendors.
