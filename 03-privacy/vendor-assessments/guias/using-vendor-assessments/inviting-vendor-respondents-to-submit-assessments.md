# Inviting Vendors Respondents to submit assessments

**Scenario**

As a Data Compliance Officer supporting procurement, you're responsible for evaluating vendors during onboarding. Manually sending assessments one by one slows down the process. By inviting multiple vendors to complete security assessments through a centralized platform, you streamline intake, compare responses side by side, and ensure each vendor meets your organization’s security standards before approval.

Use vendor assessments to evaluate the compliance readiness of external partners who handle and process personal data for you. When you invite a vendor to submit an assessment, the vendor data protection officer (DPO) is invited as a respondent. Therefore, they do not need a Securiti account to handle the assessment as an internal assessment at their company.

The DPO is instead supplied with an email invite to answer and submit assessment questions. The email contains a direct link to a page that takes the vendor respondent to their invited assessments. This allows both the vendor and vendor manager to message back and forth at the question level.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Vendor Assessment Magic Links expire 2 weeks after they are sent.

You can [invite a single vendor](/v1/docs/inviting-vendor-respondents-to-submit-assessments#inviting-a-single-vendor) or [send a bulk invitation to multiple vendors](/v1/docs/inviting-vendor-respondents-to-submit-assessments#inviting-multiple-vendors) you have imported. See the sections below.

## Inviting a single vendor

### 

1. In Securiti, open **Vendor Assessments** and choose **Vendor Assessments > Vendor Assessments**.
2. In the upper right corner of the vendor assessments list page, click **Invite**.

![Screenshot_2021-02-12_at_1_47.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6992dbf6-39ba-0e38-94a1-9097c1714ab9.png)
3. Proceed to [Launch an assessment](/v1/docs/launching-a-new-assessment-in-securiti).

## Inviting multiple vendors

### 

1. In Securiti, open **Vendor Assessments** , and choose **Vendor Assessments > Vendor Assessments**.
2. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Import**.

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_11_46.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-842fd097-1eb6-837f-12e7-0d71589b726a.png)
3. On the "Import Vendor List" popup, drag and drop a list of vendors or click **Select File to Import**, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_11_51.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c3161011-c02a-d867-b748-f5d2acd66e63.png)
4. Once you've selected a file or a list of vendors, click **Import** , as shown below.

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_12_24.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-13d868b6-973b-bd73-6389-a8dae63cc6f6.png)

The "File successfully imported" message is displayed.
5. Mark the individual checkboxes for vendors, or mark the "Ready to invite" checkbox to select all, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_11_58.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2f5b2023-df99-e84d-5e68-f657f0f96ccb.png)

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_12_27.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2ad7f3ed-d498-7707-36c2-41f232e84758.png)
6. Click **Bulk Invite**.

Make appropriate menu selections from the Assessment Category and Assessment Template, and enter a custom message in the "Message to DPO" box, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2021-02-24_at_12_02.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4d487b26-b6ee-016a-4492-b62942296974.png)
7. Click **Send Invite**.

Securiti displays a message for successful invitations sent.

Securiti hides the left-hand sidebar for guest users who you've added as collaborators and can access an assessment only using the magic link shared via email.

![VA_guest_user_view.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2ca4f0d0-55ae-d36e-30ab-fe3c25553e54.png)

When you have Advanced Invite Controls enabled in your tenant settings, Securiti sends you an email containing an OTP, as shown below.

![Email_with_the_OTP.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-62b101f3-5bc8-eeae-4cc8-65cdf088a281(1).png)

![Advanced_Magic_Link_control.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e67c9f35-b5e0-8ef4-e215-1f660eeee3e6(1).png)

After you've verified yourself as the intended vendor, Securiti takes you to the assessment you need to fulfill.

To enable Advanced Invite Controls:

1. On the Securiti Dashboard view, click **Settings**.
2. Select **Organizational > General**, and in the **Basic Information** area, click **Edit (pencil icon)** at the top right corner of the page.
3. Scroll down to the **Advanced Invite Controls** option, then toggle **Enable Advanced Invite Controls**.

![Enable_Advanced_Invite_Controls.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-63472351-c99a-0cdc-ab3b-4d3378575556(1).png)

In the upper right corner of the page, click **Save**.

Vendors are the companies you collaborate with and send assessments to for completion. You can also evaluate prospective vendors and track the performance of existing ones.
