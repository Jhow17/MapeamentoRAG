# Adding internal notes to vendor completed assessments

You can now add notes and comments to vendor completed assessments. These can only be viewed by internal users. This allows users to collaborate on vendor responses in real-time over messages. Users can become part of the assessment if:

- They are tagged in messages
- They are co-owners of that assessment
- They are the vendor manager of that vendor

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> There is no 'Save' button because internal notes are auto-saved.
> 
> Internal Notes have a limit of 4096 characters.

To add internal notes to the vendor complete assessments, do the following:

1. Log in with any user who is part of the assessment.
2. Go to Vendor Assessments.

![Vendor_assessment.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-8ed21b65-e5a6-256c-4a92-32fc9f93fb30.png)
3. Select the particular vendor or that assessment which the user is made part of.

![Select_vendor.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-aa069587-7c75-82d8-8abb-c870a5a3b087.png)
4. Against any submitted response, click the Internal Notes row (collapsed by default).
5. Click on the expand icon.
6. In the Internal Notes area, enter your comments and they will be auto-saved.

![Internal_response_row.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-ccc5cf68-bdb9-dde1-3a32-68e285007bcf.png)
7. You can delete or edit comments in the Internal Notes by removing or changing the text.

To message other users, follow these steps:

1. In the response header, a little to the right click on the message icon.

![Message_icon.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-77e86e3d-a740-1015-e006-1c49b6d941f7.png)
2. Enter messages and you can communicate with team members on a vendor response.

![Enter_messages.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-200f3ab4-c685-3838-559d-2d8a0c92c422.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Other users who are part of the assessment can also see messages from their team members.
3. In the message chat window, click the 3 dotted icon to either delete or edit the message.

![Message_chat_window.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-87949618-3626-a5e8-4f1c-2f2d9acf4751.png)
