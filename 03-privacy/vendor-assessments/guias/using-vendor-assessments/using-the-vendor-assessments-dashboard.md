# Using the Vendor Assessments dashboard

Use the Vendor Assessments dashboard to monitor and manage assessments conducted by your vendors.

On the Securiti Dashboard View, click the card for **Vendor Assessments**; the dashboard opens by default. To see the Vendor Assessments list, select **Vendor Assessments > Vendor Assessments** from the left navigation menu.

(Optional) You can also configure Multi-Org Units which you can request by emailing [support@securiti.ai](mailto:support@securiti.ai). Once you have it enabled for your tenant, in the left sidebar, click your user to select(deselect) the organizations you want to include or exclude for your tenant. Securiti displays all the assessments data for your actively selected organizations without switching between them. Now, before you can clone an assessment template or invite vendors, etc., Securiti prompts you to select the organization you want.

- Near the top of the dashboard, a row of widgets shows overall metrics for your vendor assessments.

![A00061.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-da90df03-c401-5ffb-d8e1-4216e4a2821f.png)
- The vendors list along the left edge of the page shows you a list of all of the vendors who you have sent bulk or individual invitations to submit assessments.

![A00062.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9566c91d-8395-37a3-4ae0-6e2d4c7d0b36.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> By default, Securiti filters the vendor list so it shows the vendors for whom you are a vendor manager. You can clear the filters to view all vendors as described below. See [Assigning vendor managers](/v1/docs/assigning-vendor-managers) for more information.
- Next to the vendor list is a panel that shows details for the assessments you invited from the selected vendor. For each assessment, the panel shows a tile with:

![A00063.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-5687abfa-c70c-76af-6e2a-fb01c0e0eab0.png)
  - The date on which you requested the assessment. For published assessments, the tile also shows date on which the vendor published it.
  - The progress the vendor has made towards completing the assessment, based on the percentage of questions with approved responses.
  - The readiness rating based on the approved responses.
  - The assessment type.
- At the upper right corner of the page is an icon that opens a filters panel. Use the filtering tools to refine your view of the available assessments as shown below. See [Filtering Securiti pages and dashboards](/v1/docs/filtering-securiti-pages-and-dashboards) for more information.

![A00064.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2e1f79db-593a-23b6-5ea3-3cb2600f0f85.png)

To see detailed information about a specific assessment:

- Near the right end of its tile click **Options** (three dots) **> View Details**.

To delete an assessment request for which the vendor has not yet published the assessment:

- Near the right end of its tile click **Options** (three dots) **> Delete Request**.
