# Assessing and managing risks in vendor assessments

Similar to using conditional logic for risks in internal assessments, you can use risk logic for vendor assessments as well. Once your vendor conducts the assessment, the risk logic shows you the vendor’s assessment responses flagged as risks.

![flagged_as_risk_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c5a1f6b5-17ca-e0bb-7b79-4102885a644e.png)

From the vendor's perspective, they can individually flag and track risks when conducting an assessment.

To assess and manage risks for a question:

1. Expand the **Set a Risk** area and set a risk level after the question owner submits a response.
2. Add a description and recommendation as shown.

![response_approval_1__1_.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2edc9614-aa42-0619-587a-80ba641b7fb7.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The risk settings are exclusive to each customer account. In the vendor organization, neither the assessment owner nor the question owner sees the results of your assessment template risk logic.
> 
> Likewise, the risk settings configured by the vendor's questions owner are not visible to the organization that invited them to submit the assessment.
