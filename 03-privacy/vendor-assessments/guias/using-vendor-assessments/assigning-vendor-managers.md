# Assigning vendor managers

Securiti lets you assign specific users to manage vendors. By default, Securiti filters the Vendor Assessments dashboards of these users so that they only see vendors and vendor assessments submitted by their assigned vendors. This feature helps the vendor managers maintain focus on their assigned vendors. If they want, they can clear the filters and see all vendors and vendor assessments.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You must be a Securiti admin to assign vendor managers.

To assign users as vendor managers:

1. If you have not already done so, log in with your admin credentials.
2. In Securiti, open **Vendor Assessments** and choose **Vendor Assessments > Vendor Assessments**.
3. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Filter**, then click **Clear All Filters**.
4. On the list along the left edge of the page, click the vendor you want to assign managers.
5. In the Vendor Managers area near the upper right corner of the vendor pane, click **Edit** (the pencil icon) to edit the list of managers for that vendor.
6. Use the tools on the vendor managers pop-up to add and remove managers for the vendor as shown below.

![Vendor_manager_tools.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-64741533-138a-e719-4687-7b2e71a0f693.png)

To add as a vendor manager a new user who does not yet have a Securiti account, click **+ Add new users**, fill out the form, and click **Save**.

Securiti puts your changes into effect as soon as you dismiss the pop-up.
