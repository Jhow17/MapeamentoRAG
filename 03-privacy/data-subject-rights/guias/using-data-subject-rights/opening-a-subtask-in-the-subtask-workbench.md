# Opening a subtask in the subtask workbench

The Subtask Workbench is a place where privacy team members or technical resources of your organization can process subtasks. Unlike [DSR workbench](/v1/docs/dsr-workbench-overview), the Subtask Workbench provides a focused view of only the subtasks assigned to you making it easier to track, manage, and complete your assigned tasks.

- On the Subtask Management dashboard, click the card for the subtask you want to process.

You can also click the DSR ID on the dashboard to open the subtask in the DSR workbench.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2025-10-21 at 2.22.08 PM.png)
- By default, the list shows all subtasks from the dashboard. You can also carry forward filter results from the **Subtask Management** dashboard to the Subtask Workbench and navigate through subtasks in the workbench using the navigation arrows. Applying a filter on the dashboard and opening subtask in the workbench displays the **Search Results** based on the applied filter.

![Subtask Workbench showing carry forward filters.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2025-10-21 at 2.23.28 PM.png)
- At the upper right corner of the page, click **Subject Details** to view all information provided by the data subject in their request, including any file attachments they submitted as part of the request. For more information, see Viewing data subject details.

![Showing data subject details from subtask workbench](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2025-10-21 at 2.24.27 PM.png)
- Once you are in the subtask workbench, the top header displays the following for the currently selected subtask:

![Showing subtask metadata details from the workbench.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2025-10-21 at 2.25.03 PM.png)
  - Subtask ID
  - Subtask deadline

The deadline field displays a dash (–) in the following cases:
    - The subtask deadline is set to **None** in the subtask template.
    - The form that raised the DSR request does not have any rule configured.

- In the subtask panel you can use the following tools:

Rest, you can process subtasks the same way as in the DSR workbench, depending on the DSR request type. For more information, see [Processing DSR subtasks](/v1/docs/processing-dsr-subtasks).
  - **Regulatory Details**: Provides regulatory details that help you understand regulations applicable to the various portions of that subtask. This information helps you properly complete the subtask. The information shown is for the regulatory jurisdiction configured for the DSR request form response deadline (CCPA, GDPR, etc.).
  - **Subtask Messages**: Use this messaging panel to communicate with your colleagues who are working on parts of the same DSR request.
  - **Audit Trail**: The audit log records each time an owner or co-owner is removed from the subtask.
