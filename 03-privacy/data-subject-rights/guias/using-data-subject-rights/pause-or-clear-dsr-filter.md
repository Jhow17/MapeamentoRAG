# Pause or clear DSR Filter

You can also pause the filter condition to temporarily disable or exclude it from the search query. You can always re-enable it as needed.

To pause the filter:

1. Once you apply the filter, click the **option** (3 dots) next to the filter applied and toggle **Show Filter.**

![Screenshot_2023-09-25_at_9_52_27_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-51afb548-8b28-b096-728e-dddc7ed075dc.png)

This will temporarily deactivate the filter condition, and you can easily reactivate it later if needed.

You can clear filter condition which is no longer relevant or needed.

1. To clear the filter, click the **option** (3 dots) next to the filter applied and click **Clear Filter**.
