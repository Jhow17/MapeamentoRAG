# Using the Regulations Support dashboard

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you manage DSR requests across regions with unique and evolving privacy laws. Securiti’s DSR module supports global regulations out of the box. In cases where your interpretation of these regulations differs, your organization follows stricter requirements, or you need to include a regional regulation not yet covered by the module, you can use the Regulations Support page to create custom regulations or edit existing ones. This lets you maintain precise compliance without waiting for standard module updates.

## Introduction

The Regulations Support dashboard provides a centralised view of all standard regulations supported by the DSR module and lets you define custom regulations to match your organization’s needs. You can search, filter, and review regulation details, including supported request types, deadlines, and applicable locations. The dashboard helps you adapt existing regulations or create new ones for regions or requirements not yet covered, ensuring complete and accurate compliance management.

To open the dashboard, choose **Settings > Regulations Support**.

- Use the top-level filters to narrow the results from the regulations list:

| Filter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Regulation Name | Filter regulations by name. |
| Region | Filter regulations by regions or continents |
| Country | Filter regulations by countries where they are applicable. |
| Applicable Location | Filter regulations by applicable location, such as a province, state, or country. A regulation may apply to an entire country or to a specific state or province, depending on its scope. |
| Source | Filter regulations by their source type. The source can have one of the following values: - **Standard:** An out-of-the-box regulation supported by Securiti’s DSR module. - **Customized**: A standard regulation modified by updating its details, such as deadlines or request types. - **User-defined:** A regulation created by you when your organization’s interpretation of an existing regulation differs, when stricter requirements apply, or when a regulation is not yet covered by the DSR module. |
| Extension Supported | Filter regulations that support extension periods |
| Appeal Support | Filter regulations that support appeal handling |
- Use the search bar to search for a particular regulation by its name.
- The dashboard shows the total number of regulations and the total number of applicable locations. **Applicable locations** represent the specific countries, states, or provinces within a region where a regulation applies. A regulation may apply to an entire country or to a specific state or province, depending on its scope.
- The regulations list displays the following columns to help you view and compare regulation details at a glance.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Display name | Shows the formatted name of the regulation, including the country or region where it applies. |
| Regulation Name | Displays the official name of the regulation. |
| Source | Identifies the origin of the regulation. - **Standard**: An out-of-the-box regulation supported by Securiti’s DSR module. - **Customized**: A standard regulation is marked as customized if its deadlines are updated or if any request type is added, deleted, or updated. - **User-defined**: A regulation created by you to meet stricter or region-specific compliance needs not covered by the default regulations. |
| Region | Shows the continent such as North America or Asia, where the regulation applies. |
| Industry | Indicates the industry category to which the regulation applies, such as Commercial, Education, or Financial. |
| Applicable Location | Shows where the regulation applies within a region. If it applies to an entire country, this column shows the country name. If it applies to a specific state or province, that location name appears instead. |
| Deadline | Shows the number of days allowed to complete a DSR request under the regulation. |
| Extension Supported | Indicates whether the regulation supports deadline extensions. |
| Extension Period | Displays the number of days available as an extension, if extension supported. |
| Appeal supported | Indicates whether the regulation supports appeals. |

### Viewing regulation details

You can view regulation details such as DSR forms associated with a particular regulation.

To view details:

1. On the Regulations Support dashboard, near the right corner of the regulation row click **View Metadata**.

From the metadata card, you can:

- Click the number shown next to DSR forms to view all forms associated with the selected regulation.

![Fetch DSR forms associated with the regulation](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2025-12-04 at 4.59.21 PM.png)
- Review regulation information, including the date of application and date of enforcement for standard or customized regulations. These dates are not shown for user-defined regulations.
  - **Date of Application** refers to the day on which a law is formally approved or enacted by the governing authority, such as when a governor signs a state privacy law.
  - **Date of Enforcement** refers to the day on which the law becomes effective and enforcement begins, meaning regulatory authorities can apply penalties for non-compliance.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> These definitions may vary slightly by jurisdiction. For instance, in some regions, the date of application may differ from the legislative approval date, while the date of enforcement consistently represents when the law takes effect.

### Viewing regulatory guidance

You can now view regulatory guidance for each supported regulation, helping you interpret legal requirements, understand obligations, and manage DSR requests with greater accuracy.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Regulatory guidance is available only for standard or customized regulations. It is not supported for user defined regulations.

To view regulator guidance:

1. On the Regulations Support dashboard, at the end of the regulation row, Click **More Actions (3 dots) > Regulatory Guidance**.

![Regulations support table displaying various data protection regulations and their details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-01-02 at 1.46.38 PM(1).png)

You can switch between sections from the left panel to view different parts of the regulation.

![Overview of Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree and its application details.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-01-02 at 1.48.24 PM.png)

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> - Selecting an item from the left panel highlights the related section and scrolls the page to that content.
> - Scrolling through the content on the right does not update or highlight items in the left panel.
> - Only the most specific section is highlighted. Parent sections do not highlight automatically.
