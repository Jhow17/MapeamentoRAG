# Supported request types

The DSR portal supports the following request types:

| Request type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Data subjects may obtain confirmation as to whether their data is being processed and request access to it. Additionally, data subjects may request information on how their data is being processed. |
| Port | Data subjects may request a portable copy of their data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. |
| Erasure | Data subjects may request erasure of their data, subject to any legitimate reasons to the contrary. |
| Rectification | Data subjects may request rectification of inaccuracies in their data. |
| Objection | Data subjects may request discontinuation of processing of their data, subject to any legitimate reasons to the contrary. |
| Restrict Processing | Data subjects may request restriction of processing of their data for a specific period of time. |
| Restrict Automated Individual Decision-making | Data subjects may request discontinuation of processing of their data for profiling/ automated individual decision-making. |
| Do Not Sell or Share | Data subjects may request that their data not be sold to or shared with third parties. |
| Confirmation | Data subjects may request confirmation of whether their data is being processed. |
| Disclosure of Sub-processors | Data subjects may request disclosure of public and private entities with whom their data is being shared. |
