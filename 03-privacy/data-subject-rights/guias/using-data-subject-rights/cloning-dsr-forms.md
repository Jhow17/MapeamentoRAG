# Cloning DSR forms

You can clone a DSR form within the same organization that you are currently managing or working in or across all your active organizations within the tenant. This eliminates the need to recreate or manually copy forms from one organization to another within your tenant.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> To migrate DSR forms across organizations, you must have the Manage Org Units premium feature enabled for your Securiti tenant.
> 
> Securiti lets the following roles in your Securiti tenant clone DSR forms:
> 
> - Super Admin
> - Module Owner
> - DPO
> - Org Admin
> - Operator

1. In Data Subject Rights, choose **Settings > Form Builder**, at the extreme right side of the form card click **Options** (3 dots) and choose **Clone**.

![Screenshot_2025-08-04_at_8_02_59_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-405f7d83-d626-f4ce-bcdf-af793d28e08d.png)
2. On the “Select Organization” popup choose the organization into which you want to clone the form and click **Proceed**.

![organization_drop_down.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-65da31a3-3412-c193-17d4-55df56a5e659.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> - You can only clone DSR forms into active organizations within your tenant. Once you clone the DSR form to the destination organization, it remains in an unpublished state. You must publish the form to use it.
> - If rules are configured for the DSR form and the form is cloned across organizations, all rules are deleted.
> - If the form is cloned within the same organization, configured rules and associated templates are retained.
