# Configuring identity verification with ID.me

You can configure Securiti to use ID.me as an identification verification feature for DSR requests. Securiti verifies data subject identities as described for ID.me in [How Securiti verifies data subject identity](/v1/docs/how-securiti-verifies-data-subject-identity).

You must contact your Securiti representative to activate this feature for your Securiti account. Once we have activated this feature for your Securiti account, use the following procedure to configure your DSR forms to use ID.me for identity verification:

1. In Securiti, choose **Data Subject Rights > Settings > Form Builder**.
2. Choose the form you want to use, or create a new one.
3. On the Form Settings tab, mark the checkbox for **External ID verification** and choose **ID.me** as the ID verification service as shown below.

![identity_verify_ID_me.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3aa0c5d6-9c8e-3870-6dd7-2cf1a0a0bb63.png)
4. Click **Review & Publish** and then click **Publish** to publish the DSR request form.
