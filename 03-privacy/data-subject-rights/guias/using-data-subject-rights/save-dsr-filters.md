# Save DSR filters

You can also save filters for efficient data retrievals in the future. Saving filters helps you save time and effort, and quickly access specific subsets of DSR records without repeatedly entering the same criteria.

To save the filter:

1. Once you apply the filter, near the upper right corner of the DSR Dashboard, click **Save View.**

![Screenshot_2023-09-25_at_10_13_32_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-697f78ec-252e-8d1f-95d9-38b0f3e0c4b4.png)
2. On the Save New Filter popup, enter the name, and description of the filter and click **Save**.

![Screenshot_2023-09-25_at_10_18_10_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d721def5-b903-fb86-b7df-3d659df790ed.png)

You can view saved filters by clicking on **Saved Views**. Further, Click **option** (3 dots) next to the saved filter that you want to apply and click **Apply**

![saved_view_apply.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-cfbda1d7-5340-5854-78cd-b2ce6414f833.png)

### Edit Saved Filter

To edit the saved filter:

1. On the DSR Dashboard, near the upper right corner, click **Saved Views**.
2. Click **option** (3 dots) next to the saved filter that you want to edit and click **Edit**.

![Screenshot_2023-09-25_at_10_49_02_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-456571a5-b5e0-3560-72b4-982197dee620.png)
