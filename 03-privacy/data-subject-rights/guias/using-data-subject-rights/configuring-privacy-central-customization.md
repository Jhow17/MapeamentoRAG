# Configuring Privacy Central customization

Privacy Central customization lets you hide the "Powered by Securiti" logo on all screens that data subjects interact with. It gives you more control over how your branding appears to the data subjects.

You can hide the “Powered by Securiti” branding that appears at the footer of:

- Published DSR forms
- Published DSR reports
- Most screens/pages of Privacy Central
- All email notifications that data subject receives

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Only Securiti super admins can enable this setting.

To access Privacy Central customization:

1. In the Data Subject Rights module, choose **Settings > Privacy Central Settings**.
2. Under Privacy Central Customization section, click **Edit**(pencil) icon on the right side to open the branding settings in edit mode as shown below.

![Privacy Central settings for data subject rights and email communication options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-12 at 11.08.32 AM.png)
3. Mark the checkbox and click **Save** to enable customization.

![Settings for Privacy Central, including branding removal and communication options for data subjects.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-12 at 11.11.16 AM.png)

Also, you can hide “Powered by Securiti” logo branding from published universal forms. For more information, see [Creating and publishing universal forms](/v1/docs/creating-and-publishing-universal-forms)

### Configuring data subject email communication settings

You can configure data subject communication settings to allow data subjects to initiate conversations on active and completed DSR requests. This lets them ask questions, clarify their requests, or provide additional context after submission.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Only Securiti super admins can enable this setting.

To access data subject communication settings:

1. In the Data Subject Rights module, choose **Settings > Privacy Central Settings**.
2. In the **Data Subject Email Communication Settings** section, click Edit (pencil icon) on the right to open the section in edit mode.

![Settings for Data Subject Email Communication in Privacy Central, including options for requests.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-12 at 11.14.49 AM.png)
3. Enable or disable the following settings as per your requirement:

| Allow Data Subjects to initiate conversation on Open Requests | Enable this toggle to let data subjects start new conversations with DSR owners while the request is in progress. |
| --- | --- |
| Allow Data Subjects to initiate conversation on Closed Requests | Enable this toggle to let data subjects start new conversations on DSR requests that are already completed. > [!NOTE] > **Note** > > You can enable this option only if data subjects are also allowed to respond to conversations on completed requests. |
| Allow Data Subjects to respond to existing conversations on Closed Requests | Enable this toggle to let data subjects reply to existing messages from DSR owners after the request is completed. |
