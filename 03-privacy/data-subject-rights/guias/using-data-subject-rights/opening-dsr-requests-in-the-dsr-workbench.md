# Opening DSR requests in the DSR workbench

You use the DSR workbench to monitor, track, and process DSR requests and DSR tasks and subtasks.

1. In Securiti, open **Data Subject** **Rights** and choose **Data Subject Rights > Workbench**.
2. If necessary, expand the All DSRs list at the left edge of the page.
3. On the All DSRs list, click the card for the DSR request.

Securiti opens the DSR request as shown below.
