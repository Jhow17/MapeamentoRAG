# Processing DSR subtasks

Securiti simplifies the process of processing DSR requests by breaking them down into a series of subtasks that you assign to the team members with the specialized knowledge to accomplish them quickly and efficiently.

The procedures in this section describe at a high level how Subtask owners process their subtasks. This content assumes that a DPO, DSR Module Owner, or Super Admin has assigned you one or more subtasks. It also assumes that you have access to the data systems containing the personal data associated with the subtasks.
