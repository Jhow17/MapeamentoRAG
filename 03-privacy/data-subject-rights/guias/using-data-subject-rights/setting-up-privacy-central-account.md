# Setting up Privacy Central account

Privacy Central Account Setup gives you the access and flexibility to set up a Privacy Central account to interact with your data subject requests. To access the Privacy Central account:

1. In the Data Subject Rights module, choose **Settings > Privacy Central Settings**.
2. Click **Edit** (pencil icon) on the right side to open the Account Setup in edit mode as shown below.

![Privacy Central settings showing account setup and data subject communication options.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-12 at 11.52.20 AM.png)
3. Mark the checkbox, select an expiry date to suit and click **Save** to enable the Privacy Central Account Setup via a login link.

![Privacy Central settings for data subject rights and email communication options displayed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-12 at 11.53.29 AM.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> By default the magic link expiry date is set to **3 days**.

### Accessing Privacy Central

After setting up the Privacy Central Account, data subjects can access the Privacy Central to view and download DSR reports.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The Privacy Central module aligns with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA with enhanced accessibility.

- Once the data subject submits a DSR request via DSR form, they receive a verification email. Clicking the link in the verification email verifies the authenticity of the data subject.

![Screenshot_2022-08-23_at_12_30_11_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4012a23d-467a-7250-6b61-ae1e4a908e6e.png)
- Once the data subject clicks **Confirm Email**, they receive another email containing the link to the **Privacy Central**.

![Screenshot_2022-08-23_at_3_19_05_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-7ac4b5cd-7c1b-beb8-be35-30bab43dbaae.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If the encryption is not enabled, the "Key for zip unlock" option does not appear, and the Data Subject can download and view the report directly without downloading the passkey. For more information on encryption standards, see [Configuring DSR encryption standards](/v1/docs/configuring-dsr-encryption-standards)
- Clicking **DSR Request Center** reroutes the data subject to the **Privacy Central** portal. Data subjects can view their existing and new DSR requests in this portal and collaborate with the DSR owner via messages.

![Screenshot_2022-08-23_at_3_20_09_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-71f8ec43-970c-8b36-2b1f-0746a3dd9fc5.png)
- Once the request has been processed, the DSR report appears in the Completed Request section. Data Subjects can click **View Details** to view report details and further click **Download all data** and **Key for Zip unlock** respectively to download the DSR report and key to access the report.

![Screenshot_2023-08-21_at_9_51_02_AM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-78d33ffb-bef4-b64b-539b-047703915bdd.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> In the case of a rectification request, the DSR report sent to the data subject contains only the details of attributes that data system owners find and rectify. If you, as a data subject, do not find all attributes in the DSR report you submitted for rectification, it means the subtask owners did not locate those attributes in the data systems.

### Initiating an appeal request

Once a DSR request is processed, the data subject can initiate an appeal request if they are dissatisfied with a DSR report shared as a result of a submitted DSR request.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If this feature is not available in your Securiti tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan.

To initiate an appeal request:

1. In the Completed Request section, Data Subjects can click **Appeal**.

When data subjects hover over the **Appeal** button, it displays them the last date to file an appeal, which corresponds to the appeal time limit set during the configuration of the DSR form.

![appeal_hover.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-749c5467-02e4-6518-0874-4d423ba09219.png)
2. Enter the reason to file an appeal request and attach files as supporting evidence to request reconsideration of the submitted DSR. Once done, click **Submit Appeal** and further click **Proceed** to confirm submission of appeal.

![Submit_an_appeal.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-03373c02-8e8a-4dea-0c07-6c44e1e44f1b.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Data subjects can attach a maximum of 10 files with their appeal request. Additionally, each file size shouldn’t exceed 10 MBs.

Data subjects can see all submitted appeals in the Active Appeal Requests section. After the data subject submits their appeal, Securiti sends a confirmation email to the data subject, stating that their request has been received.

#### Downloading the appeal report

Once the request has been processed, the appeal report appears in the Completed Appeals Request section. Data Subjects can click **View Details** to view report details and further click Download all data and Key for Zip unlock respectively to download the appeal report and key to access the report.

![Download_appeal_report.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-89a3b745-b4b3-350f-acaf-e0fa43bd5e65.png)

Following the appeal report, data subjects can also download the previously sent DSR report.

### Submitting more details for request clarification

When the request clarification process starts, Securiti emails the data subject, asking for additional details to process their DSR request. The email includes a link to the DSR request center, where they can provide the required information.

![Screenshot_2025-02-14_at_1_24_06_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2f4eb54e-4da8-b733-daca-8f6b0636b653.png)

To submit additional details:

1. Login to the Privacy Central and choose the request for which additional information is needed.

When data subjects log into their portal, they see a summary of their DSR requests. If additional details are needed, the "Additional Requirements" section highlights it, prompting them to take action. The data subject can click Details to proceed.

![Screenshot_2025-02-14_at_2_32_52_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-47fe5557-7ce1-77be-9c88-711c368a16d4.png)

Securiti shows a popup when additional details are needed for a pending request, directing the data subject to provide the required information.
2. In the Active Requests section, the data subject can click **Additional Details Required.**

![Screenshot_2025-02-14_at_2_38_37_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1a39855a-d15e-d623-e0cd-112c8829a322.png)
3. Fill out the form with all required details and attach files if requested for clarification. Once done, click Submit additional details. Data subjects can attach a maximum of 3 files and each file size shouldn’t exceed 10 MBs.

> [!WARNING]
> **Important**
> 
> Data subjects should consider these factors when handling submitting additional details:
> 
> - If a data subject attaches a file larger than 10 MB, the request might be submitted, but the attachment does not appear in the **Subject Details** section of the DSR Workbench. This is happening due to an issue in the third-party library being used. We are actively investigating the matter.
> - If the supplemental form has first name and last name fields with the same label and unique key as the DSR form, Securiti applies the same validations as the original DSR form. Since these fields are mandatory on the DSR form, submitting them empty on the supplemental form throws an error, even if they are not required there.

### Communicating with DSR owners

Data subjects can message DSR owners on both open and closed DSR requests depending on the [communication settings](/v1/docs/configuring-privacy-central-customization).

To initiate a conversation:

1. Select the request you want to message about and click **Message.**
2. The page shows a list of all past conversations with DSR owners. Click **Send Message**.

![Screenshot_2025-05-15_at_1_41_39_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-bf05e0cc-f9d3-1417-5903-b3952b0db877.png)
3. Compose your message, add relevant attachments, and click Send Message.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If a DSR owner deletes the request while a draft message is in progress, Securiti prevents sending the draft and hides the reply actions in Privacy Central. Data subjects can attach an unlimited number of files. Each file must not exceed 100 MiB in size.

### Changing language in Privacy Central

Data Subjects can change the language of the portal to view content in their preferred language. Securiti currently supports [41 languages](/v1/docs/supported-languages-in-data-subject-rights) in the Privacy Central.

1. Log in to your Privacy Central account.
2. At the upper right corner, click **Language**.
3. Select desired language from the menu.

![Screenshot_2025-03-06_at_12_57_16_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-9a1df7ef-40ba-f58f-06f7-77d860ee8c76.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Data subjects must consider how language settings behave in these two cases:
> 
> - If a data subject accesses the report without an account on Privacy Central, the portal defaults to the language in which the DSR was raised. If the data subject changes the language from the navigation bar, the data reloads in the selected language. However, if the page is refreshed, the portal reverts to the DSR-raised language.
> - If a data subject logs in with a password, the Privacy Central portal prioritizes their saved language. Changing the language from the navigation bar reloads the data in the selected language and updates their language preference. If the page is refreshed, the portal remains in their saved language. Even if the same data subject raises another DSR request in a different language, the portal continues to prioritize their saved language.

### Deleting Privacy Central account

Data subjects can delete the accounts that they created as part of their DSR request process. The option to delete the account is only visible once all the DSR requests belonging to a data subject are fully completed and the data subject accesses the DSR report.

![Screen_Shot_2022-10-06_at_2_52_42_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-68a0cbfa-eb2c-5fa6-ce91-1fb405349a91.png)

To delete your Privacy Central account:

1. Log in to your Privacy Central account.
2. On the top right side of the page, click **Welcome**.
3. Select **Delete Account** from the menu.

![Screen_Shot_2022-10-06_at_2_52_42_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-68a0cbfa-eb2c-5fa6-ce91-1fb405349a91.png)
4. Click **Yes, delete** on the confirmation popup to delete your Privacy Central account.

![Screen_Shot_2022-10-06_at_2_53_18_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-59badab3-86fb-915e-32a1-3c003fe9e7fd.png)

### Enabling accessibility settings in privacy central

Securiti lets data subjects enhance accessibility in the Privacy Central portal, making it usable for individuals of all abilities. They can enable high color contrast to improve visibility, meet WCAG compliance, and support a wide range of users.

To enable the color contrast settings:

1. At the bottom right corner of the published form, data subjects can click **Accessibility Settings**.
2. On the Accessibility Settings popup, enable **Enable high contrast for increased visibility** toggle and click Apply **Settings**.

![Screenshot_2025-03-06_at_1_18_20_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c70e0f91-9069-905e-a3b5-89c4a7a81a5b.png)

### Storing DSR Reports in Private Cloud Storage

You can store all the data in your environment and on your own data bucket by enabling customer-owned storage and setting up your private cloud storage account details. Securiti uses that to store the DSR reports. In this case, it is expected that customers ensure the availability, security, and redundancy of the data in their environment. For more information, see [Configuring private cloud storage](/v1/docs/system-settings-configuring-private-cloud-storage)

If the data subject removes the DSR reports from Privacy Central, Securiti deletes attachments/reports from Privacy Central and the customer's private storage. Further, if you delete the files from the DSR workbench, they also get deleted from custom storage. Alternatively, the DSR report is automatically deleted from the Privacy Central once the retention period reaches expiration. To configure the DSR report retention period, see [Configuring DSR retention policy](/v1/docs/configuring-dsr-retention-policy)

Similarly, if you don’t configure the private storage, DSR reports are stored on Securiti's configured S3 bucket. They are automatically deleted as soon as the reports are deleted from within the DSR workbench or when the DSR retention period elapses.

Securiti removes copies of data subject reports, and the evidence of this action can only be demonstrated to customers by accessing the storage location and showcasing the content before and after the process. Securiti does not store this data and only uses metadata for classification.

### FAQ

#### 1. Where are DSR reports stored?

You can store all the data in your environment and on your own data bucket by enabling customer-owned storage and setting up your private cloud storage account details. Securiti uses that to store the DSR reports. In this case, it is expected that customers ensure the availability, security, and redundancy of the data in their environment. For more information, see [Configuring Private Cloud storage](/v1/docs/system-settings-configuring-private-cloud-storage)

#### 2. What happens if a data subject tries to access their expired DSR report link at a later point (e.g., on the 5th day), after the initially set expiry period (e.g., 3 days) has passed?

Upon clicking an expired DSR report link, Securiti redirects the data subject to a new page. The page offers the option to regenerate an authorization token. If accepted, a new link to Privacy Central is generated and emailed to the data subject's email address.
