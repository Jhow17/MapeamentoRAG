# Configuring DSR reopening

The Reopen DSRs setting controls whether you can reopen a closed DSR request instead of creating a new one when additional work is needed.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Once you enable this setting, the following roles can reopen an eligible closed request from the DSR workbench: Super Admin, Organization Admin, Operator, DPO, and Module Owner. For more information, see [Reopening closed DSR requests](/latest/docs/reopening-closed-dsrs).

1. In the Data Subject Rights module, choose **Settings > DSR Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Reopen DSRs** section, then click **Edit** (pencil icon).

![Settings for Data Subject Rights, including options to pause and reopen requests.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-25 at 1.19.20 PM(1).png)
3. Mark the **Allow Reopen of DSRs** checkbox. On the Enable DSR reopen popup, review the notice, then click **Yes, Proceed**. Once done, click **Save**.

![Settings page showing options for pausing and reopening Data Subject Requests.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-25 at 4.14.51 PM.png)

![Settings interface for managing DSR deadlines and report configurations in a software application.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Screenshot 2026-08-25 at 4.15.09 PM.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Caution
> 
> Most global privacy laws and regulations do not generally permit retroactive revisions or amendments to a closed DSR request. You are responsible for determining whether reopening a request, and any resulting deadline recalculation, is permitted under the laws that apply to it. Using this feature might require a documented reason for reopening, consideration of the impact on regulatory deadlines, and notification to the data subject.

Once you enable this setting, anyone with access to a closed DSR request in the DSR workbench can reopen it. For more information, see [Reopening closed DSRs](/latest/docs/reopening-closed-dsrs).
