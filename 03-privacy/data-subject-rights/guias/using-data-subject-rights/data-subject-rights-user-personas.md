# User Personas

Use the following table to understand the roles of different types of users and what they are responsible for in the Data Subject Rights module. From the Responsibilities column, use the links of the relative help topics to learn more about the responsibilities of the following roles.

| Roles | Title in DSR Module | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy Professional, Chief Privacy Officer, Data Protection Officer | DSR Owner, DSR Form Owner | - Confirm the [identity of the data subject](/v1/docs/how-securiti-verifies-data-subject-identity) making the DSR request to ensure that the requester is indeed the individual to whom the personal data relates. - Act promptly to respond [within the timeframe set by data protection laws](/v1/docs/creating-and-publishing-dsr-request-forms). If additional time is needed, CPOs/DPOs provide a clear explanation for the extension and inform the Data Subject of the expected completion date. - [Collaborate with other team members](/v1/docs/using-the-dsr-request-and-subtask-messaging-side-panels) involved in processing the DSR. - Provide a [clear and concise response to the data subject](/v1/docs/approving-and-publishing-reports) in the form of DSR reports, addressing each specific request made in the submitted DSR. - Communicate any relevant details about the [processing activities](/v1/docs/understanding-process-records), including the purposes and recipients of the data. - Keep an [audit trail](/v1/docs/auditing-dsr-requests) of all the steps taken to comply with the request, to prove compliance in case of legal issues. |
| Database administrator,Data System Owner | Task owner,Subtask Owner | - Verifies whether the personal data retrieved as a result of DSR Automation belongs to the Data Subject. - Evaluate any [sensitive or confidential information](/v1/docs/processing-dsr-subtasks) that may need special handling and apply appropriate measures to protect such sensitive data during disclosure. - In case of manual processing of DSR, Data System Owner/administrators access the Data System and search it for any personal data of the Data Subject. - [Collaborate with other team members](/v1/docs/using-the-dsr-request-and-subtask-messaging-side-panels) involved in processing the DSR Subtask assigned to them. |
| Requester, Agent | Data Subject | - Submit the DSR request accurately and clearly, providing sufficient details to facilitate the identification of the requested information. - [Communicate with privacy professionals](/v1/docs/communicating-with-data-subjects) for DSR-related matters and provide any additional information or documentation required to validate the DSR. - [Review the DSR reports](/v1/docs/setting-up-privacy-central-account) provided in response to the DSR request in the DSR request center and seek clarification or raise concerns with the organization if necessary |
| Collaborator,Guest (External users) | Subtask Owner | - Verifies whether the personal data retrieved as a result of DSR Automation belongs to the Data Subject. - Evaluate any [sensitive or confidential information](/v1/docs/processing-dsr-subtasks) that may need special handling and apply appropriate measures to protect such sensitive data during disclosure. - In case of manual processing of DSR, Data System Owner/administrators access the Data System and search it for any personal data of the Data Subject. - [Collaborate with other team members](/v1/docs/using-the-dsr-request-and-subtask-messaging-side-panels) involved in processing the DSR Subtask assigned to them. |

By default, the Securiti Admin who creates a DSR form is the owner of all DSR requests that are submitted through the form. DSR form owners can add additional owners to a form. Also, Securiti admins and DPOs can change the ownership of a form.
