# About repetitive requests

DSR regulations such as GDPR and CCPA specify that you must respond promptly (Within one month for GDPR, 45 days for CCPA) to DSR requests from data subjects. However, they do not demand that you respond to repeated or persistent requests.

In both GDPR and CCPA, when requests are “manifestly unfounded or excessive, in particular, because of their repetitive character” (GDPR Article 12(5), CCPA 1798.145g3), you can charge a reasonable fee for complying with it (GDPR), or refuse it (CCPA).

The Securiti DSR portal filters out excessive requests primarily by discarding requests for which the sender does not respond to the verification email. We also prevent data subjects from opening multiple requests of the same type until the active request is serviced or closed, and also within 30 days from the last fulfilled request of the same type. Also, once DSRs are opened within the DSR portal, DSR owners can decline requests manually by providing a legitimate reason for doing so.

Per CCPA regulations, opt-out and deletion requests are not subject to repetition restrictions, and are always allowed.
