# Using the PrivacyOps Center

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you need a clear view of your organization’s privacy posture. Manual reports slow you down and hide regional risks. In the PrivacyOps Center, you open the dashboard to see global and country level metrics, review DSR trends, and check internal and vendor assessment readiness. You quickly spot gaps, act on them, and maintain stronger privacy oversight.

## Overview

The PrivacyOps Center shows your overall privacy posture on a single pane of glass. To open it, click **PrivacyOps Center** near the top of the left sidebar.

- The tiles along the top row show metrics for the country or data center currently selected on the world map below them. If no country or data center is selected, they show your total worldwide metrics.

![Using_privacy_ops_center.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-ca77ee4d-f304-5c29-3dff-21da233b1536.png)

Each tile shows metrics both numerically and with a trend line. You can choose the past month, quarter, or year as the scope for the trend line

You can also collapse the trend lines for all tiles to save space on your screen. Click the up or down arrow at the bottom of any tile to toggle the trend lines.
- The world map, and the panel to its right, show you metrics for specific countries and data systems. Use the menu near the upper right corner of the map to toggle between personal data records per residency and identity records per residency.
  - Hover over a shaded country to open a popup with information about what personal data you hold for its residents, and where. Click the country to show detailed information in the tiles at the top of the page.

![detailed_info.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-81e8321c-38f8-130c-1d5b-d1effcc7f265.png)
  - Hover over a data system dot to open a popup with information about what personal data it holds. Click the data system to show detailed information in the tiles at the top of the page.

![tiled_detail_info.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2e656d29-f198-acf0-2575-1d43f697e6a2.png)
- The Open DSR Requests panel shows metrics and trends for your DSR requests currently in progress. Hover over any of the graphics for numerical values.

![dsr_panel_request.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-43bc2c14-c6d9-a491-654c-b1fa353e3b7e.png)
- For PrivacyOps dashboard to show internal assessments, ensure that:

The Internal Assessments panel shows metrics and trends for your privacy compliance readiness as revealed by the assessments conducted within your organization.

![internal_assessment_panels.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4bd0f759-2a8b-01f7-6394-2b13870ac676.png)
  - Readiness is greater than zero.
  - Templates belong to internal assessments and not include Data Maps.
  - Assessments include internal assessments and not vendor assessments.
  - System templates are used as the source for assessment creation and not custom templates.
- The Vendor Assessments panel near the bottom of the page shows metrics and trends for your vendors' privacy compliance readiness as revealed by the assessments they have conducted and submitted to you.

![vendor_assessment.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-af300d92-0c43-fcd9-a7e8-ebea4cf6b2ca.png)
