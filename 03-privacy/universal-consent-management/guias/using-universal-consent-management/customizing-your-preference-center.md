# Customizing your universal preference center

You can customize your universal preference center by dragging and dropping custom fields with their custom parameters that fit the need of your organization's preference center.

After building your preference center as described in the [Building a universal preference center](/v1/docs/building-a-universal-preference-center), you can customize the preference center form by clicking on the **Customize Verification** tab.

Scroll down to the **Define Form Fields and Requirements** section as shown below.

![1st_ss.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c972b0ee-b4e1-12a5-3180-6da79fb0788b.png)

1. Click on the **Custom Fields** button.
2. Hover over to the required custom field. Click on it and drag it over to the **Drag and Drop a from component** area and drop it here.

![step_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b8d2c90e-27cf-e6d6-b065-be14509e9f31.png)
3. Click the **Save** button in the **Preview** section on the opened popup to save the field in your preference center.

![step_3.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f70b7299-e439-25de-8087-a567a5ababd6.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You must **avoid using special characters** when labeling new **Custom Fields**, as they may be truncated during API key storage, potentially causing mismatches or identification issues, as shown below.
> 
> ![pc_note_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1c5450c3-a621-0e33-4e0d-f0e95c341db0.png)
> 
> ![pc_note_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a602dd24-759d-afe2-2e5e-d191685e350f.png)
4. This field now becomes a part of your preference center. You can still remove it by clicking on the **X** icon.
5. Hover over the added field and click the **X** icon on the top right side of the field to remove it from the preference center as shown below. ![step_5.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-13bc39d6-e716-d417-7af4-e5168e8d2f53.png)

See [Custom universal preference center form tabs](/v1/docs/custom-preference-center-form-tabs) for more information on how to fully customize the preference center custom fields.
