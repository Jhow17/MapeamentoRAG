# Location and Language Intelligence

Location intelligence lets you create multiple preference centers via the preference center builder for the same region. For instance, you can create a default preference center for the European region and can create specific preference centers for different countries in the European region.

With language intelligence, you can provide dynamic translations for the preference centers depending on the language selected by the data subjects in their web browsers.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The **Location** and **Language Intelligence** features are available by subscription for **freemium** and **lite** Securiti tenants. Contact the Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to enable these features for your freemium or lite Securiti tenant.

### Location Intelligence

When you enable the **Location Intelligence** toggle in the preference center builder, it automatically detects the geographic location of the data subjects and shows them the preference center built specifically for their region. You can also build multiple preference centers for different countries that belong to the same region as shown below.

![location.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-dfc0b932-af68-82e5-ce62-f01b86bbe56e.png)

### Language Intelligence

When the **Language Intelligence** toggle in the preference center builder is enabled, the preference center is dynamically translated in the language that is selected by you for your web browser. Language intelligence applies to both the verification page and the preference center page.

![language_1st_ss.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d32b8e25-00d6-4489-fc7d-b588ea0027b3.png)

You can switch on the **Provide Translation Mapping** toggle to configure the translations for specific language(s). The translation you provide here for the selected language is then applies to the preference center when that same language is selected in the web browser.

![language_2nd_ss.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e7e2acd3-04b8-f86b-9f27-eef4afa8e360.png)
