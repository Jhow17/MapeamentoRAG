# Using Universal Consent Management

Universal consent management refers to a comprehensive system or approach for managing the consents of individuals such as consumers, customers, or data subjects etc. across various platforms, services, or applications. It involves:

- Obtaining permission from individuals such as consumers, end users, website visitors or data subjects that apply broadly across different aspects of data processing within a given system or service.
- Appropriately informing these individuals clearly and unambiguously about how their data is used in data processing and marketing activities.

Universal consent management is crucial for organizations operating in diverse digital landscapes, helping them adhere to privacy regulations and build trust with data subjects by giving them control over their data. In the context of privacy and data protection, particularly in compliance with regulations such as the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** in the European Union, universal consent management involves providing data subjects with clear information about data collection practices and obtaining their consent in a transparent manner.

Universal Consent Management lets you:

- Collect user consent on any platform such as mobile application, web application etc.
- Aggregate consent into a single location or platform.
- Allow users to update their preferences at any time.
- Orchestrate user consent across all systems, apps, databases, etc.
