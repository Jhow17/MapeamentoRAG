# Downloading universal consent records from the Legacy Dashboard

You can download the universal consent records present in your **Consent Records** table on the Universal Consent Dashboard in a **CSV** file. Securiti sends you an email containing the download link to the CSV file.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The contents of the file reflects the filters applied at the time of the download.

To download the universal consent records:

1. In Securiti, open **Universal Consent Management** and choose **Dashboards > Dashboard**.
2. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Filter**, and apply filters to suit. For more information, see [Filtering universal consent](/v1/docs/filtering-universal-consent).
3. From the upper right corner of the **Consent Records** table, click **Download** icon, as shown below.

![Downloading_Universal_Consent_Records_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-703698ee-fd54-a423-efa7-55c53438c67f.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> To prevent multiple report requests and reduce system strain, the **Download Records** button on the Universal Consent Dashboard disables after a single click and only re-enables when you change the filters. Additionally, Securiti enforces a limit of **5 report requests per hour** for each Securiti user role.
4. Securiti schedules your download, and you receive an email containing the download link to your universal consent records, as shown below.

![Downloading_Universal_Consent_Records_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-5b4e1b5a-55c3-4d9a-f2da-dd39eea9296d.png)
5. Open your email associated with your Securiti tenant, and click **Download File** to download the CSV file containing universal consent records in your local machine, as shown below.

![Screenshot_2023-10-02_at_1_25_38_PM_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f1f34401-1518-ec48-e732-6993e09c821a.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> - The download link remains valid for **7 days**.
> - Securiti only lets you download a maximum of **31 days** worth of universal consent records. If your selected date range from the **Custom Range** filter in the module exceeds 31 days, Securiti auto-adjusts your start and end dates.
