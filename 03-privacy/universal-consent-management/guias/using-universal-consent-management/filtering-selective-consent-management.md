# Filtering selective consent management

You can filter out specific consent records by activating the **Consent Narrow Path** filter from the Universal Consent dashboard. When this filter is activated, the consent records table in the dashboard only shows consent records given with the selective consent management functionality.

To filter out selective consent records:

1. In Securiti open Universal Consent Management.
2. Click the **Filter** icon near the upper right corner of the dashboard to open the filters panel.
3. Go to **Source** and mark the **Consent Narrow Path** checkbox.
4. Click **Apply** to activate this filter.
