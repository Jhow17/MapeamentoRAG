# Universal preference center translations

Securiti translates your preference centers and the content of access emails that go to your data subjects into the following languages based on the language selected in the visitor's web browser:

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> To apply language translations, you must enable **Language Intelligence** for your preference center.
