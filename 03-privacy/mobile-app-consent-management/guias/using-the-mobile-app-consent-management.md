# Using the Mobile App Consent Management

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This module is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

Securiti lets you tailor user experiences while upholding privacy standards with Securiti's Mobile App Consent Management (MACM) module. Our core offerings empower you to manage user consent effectively and ensure compliance with global privacy regulations.

| Core offering | Description |
| --- | --- |
| In-App Consent Tracking | Lets you seamlessly track and manage user consent within your mobile app, ensuring adherence to global privacy laws. |
| Complete Coverage | Lets you expand your reach and build user trust by integrating key consent frameworks, including IAB, GCM, and Apple’s and Google’s Advertising Frameworks. |
| SDK Integration | Lets you effortlessly integrate Securiti’s lightweight SDK for efficient consent management without compromising your app's performance. |
| User Experience Customization | Lets you personalize consent banners to align with your mobile app's design, enhancing user interaction while maintaining data protection compliance. |
| Analytics and Reporting | Lets you access detailed analytics on the mobile app user’s consent preferences to make informed decisions about app features and compliance strategies. |
| Dynamic Consent Options | Lets you provide your mobile app users with granular, region-specific consent options based on app usage, automatically adapting to local regulations. |

The Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) is a trade association representing advertising businesses. It develops industry standards, conducts research, and provides legal support for the online advertising industry. Cookie Consent Management module lets you configure your cookie banners and preference centers with two IAB privacy consent frameworks: the **EU TCF v2.2** and the **MSPA U.S. National**.
