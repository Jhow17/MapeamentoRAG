# Mobile App Configurations

To configure your mobile application, adhere to the steps in the sequence below:

1. [Scan your mobile application for SDKs and Permissions](/latest/docs/scanning-your-mobile-application-for-sdks-and-permissions)
2. [Customize consent banner and preference center for your mobile application](/latest/docs/customizing-the-consent-banner-and-preference-center-for-mobile-application)
3. [Assign SDK Purposes](/latest/docs/assigning-sdk-purposes)
4. [Manage Permissions](/latest/docs/managing-permissions-for-mobile-applications)
5. [Deploy and implement SDK](/latest/docs/deploying-a-consent-banner-and-preference-center-to-the-mobile-application)
6. (Optional) [Configure operatings system features for your mobile application](/latest/docs/using-the-operating-system-features):
  1. [Configure the Google Advertising ID Framework](/latest/docs/using-the-google-advertising-id-framework)
  2. [Configure the Apple's App Tracking Transparency Framework](/latest/docs/using-the-apples-app-tracking-transparency-framework)
7. (Optional) Configure add-on feature(s):
  1. [Configure the Settings Prompt](/latest/docs/configuring-the-settings-prompt)
