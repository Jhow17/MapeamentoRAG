# Monitoring process and asset risks

**Scenario**

As a Data Privacy Officer, you're responsible for reducing exposure from high-risk data assets. Without a clear view, it’s hard to know where to focus first. By monitoring risk scores tied to specific assets, you quickly spot the most vulnerable areas. This helps you prioritize mitigation efforts, allocate resources effectively, and strengthen your overall data protection posture.

You can view risks for data mapping entities (vendors, assets, etc.) and processes revealed by assessments you initiate as described in [Using data maps assessments](/latest/docs/creating-new-data-mapping-items-with-assessments).

To view risks for a specific entity or process:

1. In Securiti, open the Details page for the data mapping element.

For example, for an asset you would choose **Data Mapping > Catalog > Asset**, then click the table entry for the asset.
2. On the Details page, click the **Risks** tab.
3. Use the Risks tab to view details for all the risks associated with the catalog element:
  - The Risk Resolution & Global Risk Score Impact Timeline near the top of the page shows you a timeline for the risks associated with the entity.
  - Below the timeline, a row of widgets gives you at-a-glance information about risks for the entity.
  - At the bottom of the page is a table listing all the risks for the entity. Click a risk to open a details panel with detailed information.
  - At the right end end of a risks table entry, click **Options** (three dots) **> Go to risk** to open the assessment that revealed the risk.

To view risks for all entities and processes in the Securiti [Risk Management](/latest/docs/using-the-risk-register-list-view):

- In Securiti open **Data Mapping Automation**, then choose **Data Mapping > Risks**.

Opens the Risk Register and filters it so that it shows only risks for elements of the following catalog types:
  - Asset
  - Vendor
  - Institution
  - Process

Risks help assess the criticality of an asset, and measure breaches and repercussions in the event of a breach.

Risk is the likelihood of effects or implications that would negatively impact what you value. Risk in Securiti refers to risks that may arise when conducting internal or vendor assessments. You can track them using the risk dashboard.

An asset records data for any application or store. You can use It to record an asset's name, description, purpose, etc. Also, you can use an asset in processes.

Vendors are the companies you collaborate with and send assessments to for completion. You can also evaluate prospective vendors and track the performance of existing ones.

Institution is the same as an asset - an entity that serves a different purpose.

A process defines the flow of data between entities and processes.
