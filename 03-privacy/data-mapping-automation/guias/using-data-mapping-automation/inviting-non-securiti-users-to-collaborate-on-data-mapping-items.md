# Inviting non-Securiti users to collaborate on data mapping items

You can invite members of your organization who are not Securiti users to collaborate on data mapping items (assets, processes, etc), without having to create Securiti user accounts for them. They receive invitations with links that give them access only to the functions necessary to collaborate on the item.

Each collaborator receives an email as shown below with a link that opens the catalog item.

![RN_catalog_collaboration_2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-498e5905-76e4-25f9-c494-f9534c71b0aa.png)

After clicking the link, they can fill in information for the catalog item but have limited or no access to other Securiti functions, as shown below.

![image_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-7119e28b-40b3-1832-b7ef-8c522b1d90ff.png)

Also, when you have Advanced Invite Controls enabled on your Securiti tenant as a guest user, Securiti sends you an email containing an OTP, as shown below.

![Email_with_the_OTP.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-62b101f3-5bc8-eeae-4cc8-65cdf088a281(2).png)![Advanced_Magic_Link_control.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e67c9f35-b5e0-8ef4-e215-1f660eeee3e6(2).png)

After you've verified your identity as the intended guest user, Securiti opens up the catalog item you need to fill in.

To enable Advanced Invite Controls:

1. On the Securiti Dashboard view, click **Settings**.
2. Select **Organizational > General**, and in the **Basic Information** area, click **Edit (pencil icon)** at the top right corner of the page.
3. Scroll down to the **Advanced Invite Controls** option, then toggle **Enable Advanced Invite Controls**.

![Enable_Advanced_Invite_Controls.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-63472351-c99a-0cdc-ab3b-4d3378575556(2).png)

In the upper right corner of the page, click **Save**.

### Adding collaborators to a single catalog item

1. In Securiti open the Details page for the item and near the upper right corner of the page click **Edit**.
2. Next to the ownership avatars, click **Edit** (pencil icon).
3. On the View & Access popup, click the **Collaborators** tab and use the Add [item type] Collaborators box to build a list of collaborators who receive invitations to collaborate on the item as shown below.

![RN_catalog_collaboration_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-ff50734c-b457-6616-bbd9-3378738e9d45.png)
4. At the right end of the Add Collaborators box, choose as the collaborator type either Editor or Viewer.
5. If you want, mark the Send Notification checkbox and compose a message for the notification. When you are done, click **Send Invite**.

### Adding collaborators to multiple catalog items

1. In Securiti, open **Data Mapping Automation** and choose either **Catalog > Assets** or **Catalog > Processes**.
2. Filter the view as necessary to focus on the assets or processes of interest.
3. Mark the checkboxes at the left edge of the assets or processes to which you want to assign collaborators.
4. Next to the Search box, click the new **Add Collaborators** button.
5. Use the Add Collaborators popup to identify the new collaborators by their email addresses as shown below, pressing **Enter** after each address.

![RN_add_multi_collaborators.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-ac55c4b2-667a-87f0-d162-32fa5b2efa79.png)
6. When you are done, click **Send Invite**.
