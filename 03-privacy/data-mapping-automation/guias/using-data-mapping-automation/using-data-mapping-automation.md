# Using Data Mapping Automation

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> This module is compliant with WCAG-aligned accessibility enhancements.

The Securiti data mapping catalog captures your entire application or process data lifecycle and lets you monitor, manage and maintain all its properties including data elements in use, vendor interactions, Privacy Impact Assessments, and Risks.

You populate your data mapping and create data flow processes in Securiti in order to document the internal processes at your organization that involve the Collection, Processing, Storage, and archiving of data subjects' personal data. Use these data processes to:

- Understand and communicate about your internal processes.
- Perform privacy assessments such as PIA and DPIA.
- Demonstrate compliance with privacy regulations such as GDPR Article 30.

In general, you start by creating a data mapping that contains all of the entities that are to appear in the data flow process. Then you create the process and add to it components that represent your data assets. After that, you can initiate assessments to evaluate elements and processes, and also create Article 30 reports for your processes. See the topics listed below for details.

Assessments answer different questions related to an entity, such as purpose, etc. Also, with each assessment, you can associate risks.

Risks help assess the criticality of an asset, and measure breaches and repercussions in the event of a breach.
