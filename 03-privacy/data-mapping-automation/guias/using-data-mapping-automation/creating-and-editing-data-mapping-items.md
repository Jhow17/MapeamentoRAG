# Creating and editing data mapping items

You populate your data mapping with four different types of items:

- *Data Assets* are SaaS applications and data systems that you use to collect, store, and process personal information.
- Vendor represent relationships with suppliers and vendors who offer assets and services or act as sub-processors for your organization.
- *Institutions* represent controllers, processors, joint controllers, and data recipients when you sell data. You might also use them to identify entities that act as sources, storage locations, and destinations of data.
- *Processes* are combinations of the elements described above that show your data process flow in both graphic and tabular forms.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> For some subscription plans, Securiti limits the number of data mapping items you can create and maintain. Where limits apply, Securiti shows you the number of items versus the maximum number allowed near the upper left corner of the page.

Vendors are the companies you collaborate with and send assessments to for completion. You can also evaluate prospective vendors and track the performance of existing ones.
