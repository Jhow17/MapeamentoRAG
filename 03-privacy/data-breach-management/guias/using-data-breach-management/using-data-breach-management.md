# Using Data Breach Management

Due to an increased focus on data collection and analytics, breach risks have never been more prevalent for businesses. A global influx of breach regulations have also put pressure on organizations to prevent breaches or suffer heavy fines and paperwork in the event one occurs. Companies require clear insight when a breach happens and guidance on their obligations. Manually handling breach notification based on the right rules in each area makes it more expensive and complicated.

Securiti’s Data Breach Management is an automated incident response management system that helps you comply with applicable breach notification regulations and reduces cost and time to remediation. It helps you avoid costly regulatory penalties and mitigates the potential reputation damage from a Data breach. The platform also maintains centralized audit trails of how a breach incident was managed and remediated

#### Data Breach Management Key Elements

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Impact Radius of Data Breach | The Data Breach Management module supports 200+ structured and unstructured data systems. The platform automatically discovers compromised individuals’ data in all these systems including their residencies and contact information. Along with that, it also detects personal Data Elements that were compromised. |
| Identify worldwide regulatory obligations | The Breach analysis feature of Data Breach Management automatically discovers regulatory obligations that apply to organizations based on the residencies of impacted individuals. These obligations let organizations assess the impact of a breach and determine specific mitigation or required impact analysis |
| View regulatory research data | From the Data Breach workbench, you can also view regulatory summaries against impacted regulations. |
| Financial penalties for breaches | The Data Breach Management module also provides insights into financial penalties that the organization could face if appropriate actions are not taken within the prescribed timelines. |
| Breach Notification | The platform gives accurate notification recommendations based on regulations and risk analysis. - For every jurisdiction, track and inform all parties that need to be notified about the incident such as data owners, regulatory authorities, commissioners, and data controllers. - Built-in notification templates can be used to send accurate breach notifications promptly. - The platform guides the selection of communication channels (phone, email, etc.) for notifications, aiming to streamline the process, reduce manual effort, and minimize errors. This ensures timely and accurate communication, emphasizing the use of technology to enhance the notification process. |
| Impacted identities report | The platform also lets you download a report that includes compromised individuals’ data along with their residency information. |
| Document Remediation Actions | The platform lets you document remediation actions you have taken in response to the incident and upload the relevant attachments. |
| Generate Breach Report | Once you have processed the incident in the Data Breach Workbench, you can export incident details in PDF or JSON format to send to regulatory bodies or law enforcement agencies as required by data protection laws and regulations. |
| Audit Logs | The system allows you to record logs for each action performed in response to an incident, including tasks like adding incident details and specifying jurisdictions. These logs can be exported from the breach workspace for prolonged storage and reporting purposes. |

To learn more about the Data Breach lifecycle, see [Understanding the reported incident process.](/v1/docs/understanding-the-reported-incident-process)

### Example Case

Consider an imaginary organization named SecureTech Corp, a leading multinational cybersecurity solutions provider specializing in cutting-edge technologies for data protection, threat detection, and secure communications. With a global presence, the company serves a diverse range of clients, including financial institutions, healthcare organizations, and government agencies.

#### Scenario

SecureTech Corp, with operations in multiple countries, has experienced a potential data breach that compromised customers’ information. An unauthorized third party accessed a database containing personal details, such as names, addresses, and contact numbers. The organization identifies the breach through its monitoring systems and immediately activates the Data Breach Management module

#### Securiti's Data Breach Management in Action

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Impact Radius of Data Breach | As soon as an incident is reported, privacy professionals at SecureTech can perform a preliminary investigation on the incident. They can also initiate a breach assessment from within the Data Breach module to collect all available information about the incident and determine whether the incident qualifies as a data breach. Privacy professionals can either choose to [process the incident manually](/v1/docs/processing-a-data-breach-report) or use the [enhanced breach analysis](/v1/docs/using-the-enhanced-breach-impact-analysis-dashboard) feature to automate processing. The enhanced breach analysis refers to advanced analysis of the reported incident through the integration of real-time data intelligence and People Data Graph(PDG) technology. Assume that SecureTech decides to use enhanced breach to automate processing. In this case, privacy professionals further connect the compromised data systems(both structured and unstructured) to scan and identify compromised individuals' data. |
| Identify Worldwide Regulatory Obligation | The platform automatically determines regulatory obligations in various jurisdictions impacted as result of the breach. For instance, if impacted individuals are located in the United States, European Union, or Asia, this triggers compliance obligations under GDPR for European individuals, CCPA for U.S. residents, and various data protection laws in Asian countries. Compliance officers at SecureTech can also check the severity and impact of the data breach within different regions and determine if a jurisdiction falls into the **high impact** or **low impact** bucket. |
| Financial Penalties for Breaches | The Breach analysis dashboard in the Data Breach module provides insights into potential financial penalties, allowing SecureTech Corp to evaluate the financial implications and prioritize necessary actions for mitigation. The platform also provides a breakdown of the penalties involved to identify specific areas that need attention or improvement to prevent future penalties. |
| Breach Notification | SecureTech Corp’s privacy professionals can use the notification recommendations to promptly inform customers via internal communication channels, regulatory authorities following the specific notification requirements of each jurisdiction, and data controllers, ensuring compliance with notification regulations. They can also export a comprehensive report that includes compromised individuals' personal data and residency details for client notifications. |
| Document Remediation Actions | The company can document and upload remediation actions taken in response to the incident, demonstrating a proactive approach to rectify the breach and prevent future occurrences. |

SecureTech Corp has effectively used its Data Breach Management module, leveraging advanced features such as enhanced breach analysis and People Data Graph technology to automate the processing of the incident.

A data breach involves the unauthorized access, disclosure, or acquisition of sensitive information, including instances of both data loss and the loss of devices containing such data. It compromises privacy and security by allowing unauthorized access to sensitive information or exposing confidential data when devices are lost or stolen.

Refers to specific pieces of data, which can be categorized as either accessed by users or potentially accessible by them. Data elements are basic units of data that have a unique meaning and are of distinct value to an organization.
