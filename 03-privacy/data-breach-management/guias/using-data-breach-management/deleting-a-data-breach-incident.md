# Deleting a data breach incident

If you are an incident owner or co-owner, you can delete incidents from within the Data Breach Dashboard. Doing so deletes the incident from the dashboard, and also from the self-service portal of the original reporter.

To delete an incident from both the dashboard and the self-service portal:

1. Choose **Data Breach Management > Incidents** and locate the incident you want to delete.
2. At the right end of the incident choose **Options > Delete**, then click **Delete Incident** to confirm as shown below.
