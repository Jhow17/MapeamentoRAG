# Reporting a data breach

If you have an email address within your organization's domain, you can report a data breach as follows, even if you do not have a Securiti user or admin account:

1. Browse to the URL of your organization's Securiti self-service portal.

If necessary, ask your manager or IT contact for that URL.
2. On the portal page, enter your email address, then click **Login**.

![DBM_portal_login_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-eae59d8a-30f9-7e46-1110-c0c61cb93e13.png)

Securiti emails you a link.
3. Check your inbox for an email from Securiti, and open it when it arrives.

If you do not see the email within a few minutes, check your spam and junk mail folders in case the email was mistakenly routed there.
4. On the email, click the "click here" link for the self-service portal.

![DBM_email_link.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4b0fd8d4-60cd-2b5c-3cf1-ba2563b532f3.png)
5. On the top nav bar, click **Incidents**.

![Screenshot_2024-07-06_at_4_27_28_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3189e4ee-3464-8126-a375-1de73745aefa.png)
6. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Report Incidents**.

![Screenshot_2024-07-06_at_4_27_59_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-ff1cff2f-1e6d-8adf-4dc6-9e3198de2ffb.png)
7. On the Incident Details page, fill out the form with as much detail about the incident as possible or at least provide the incident name. Click **Upload Attachment** to add documents and files that must be sent as evidence with the breach intake form. At the bottom of the form, click **Report New Incident**.

![Upload_button.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-6ad24d8c-379f-ad23-2391-d4172dd83b94.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can upload up to 10 files per attachment; the attachment size should be at most 10 MBs.
8. The portal redirects you to a page that shows all of the current and past incidents you have reported.

![Your_Incidents_table.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1a4a2e45-cfcd-bd53-cf97-187bc0024593.png)
9. To track the progress of the incident report, click the link in the original email, then click Incidents to return to the page showing the incidents you have reported.

If you are a Securiti admin, you can initiate a breach report from within Data Breach Management, as follows:

1. In Securiti, open Data Breach Management.
2. On the Data Breach Workbench, near the upper right corner of the page click Report New Incident.
3. On the Incident Details page, fill out the form with as much detail about the incident as you can provide. Also, add documents and files that must be sent as evidence with the breach intake form.

![Basic_info_area.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-8bafee34-7411-ca45-77e8-f0b35087dbd3.png)

At the very least, you must provide an Incident Name.
4. At the bottom of the form, click **Report New Incident**.

![Click_Report_New_Incident__to_submit_.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f7225b41-9ba4-6551-6522-2944b9729d52.png)

It is recommended that you provide incident details such as the date and time of occurrence, description, type, and contact details for the reporting party, etc. in the break intake form for post-incident analysis. Also, attach all the evidence attachments and relevant supporting documentation, such as logs or screenshots, to aid in the investigation.

> [!NOTE]
> **Important**
> 
> Securiti lets the following roles in your Securiti tenant report an incident:
> 
> - Super Admin
> - Module Owner
> - Operator
> - DPO
> - Org Admin
> 
> To submit an incident through the self-service portal as a guest user, ensure that your email ID belongs to one of the supported domains within the organization.
