# Export an incidents summary report

You can export an incidents summary report in PDF format in the language of your choice.

1. In Securiti, open **Data Breach Management** , and at the middle right side of the page, click **Export Incident Summary Report**.

![export_incident_summary.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-fe47ae28-acc1-6f33-99a0-4cad0fb48934.png)
2. On the Export Incident Summary popup, select the PDF format and language to suit and click **Export**. This sends you an email containing the link to the incidents summary report. Click the Download file link to download a pdf report on your device.

![Export_button.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1b8c3f09-abe5-2fa8-dcc0-958ee37db4f8.png)

### Export summary report of filtered incidents

You can apply filters on reported incidents and export a summary report of selective incidents.

To export filtered incidents:

1. In Securiti, open **Data Breach Management** and at the upper right corner of the page, click the **Filter** icon to open filter panel .

![Filter_option.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-47db12f2-25ba-a785-e73e-91131d960690.png)
2. Expand one or more areas on the filter panel and choose or enter criteria on which to filter the reported incidents.
3. Near the bottom of the filter panel, click **Apply** to apply your filters to reported incidents.

![Apply_button.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-62f05a7f-009a-caa3-e0cb-fce885bd75ca.png)
4. To generate a summary report of incidents, near the right side of the page click **Export Incident Summary Report**.
5. On Export Incident Summary popup, choose the Format and Language and click **Export**. This sends you an email containing a Download File link to the incident summary report.
