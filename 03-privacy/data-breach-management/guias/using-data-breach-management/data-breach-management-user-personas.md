# User Personas

Use the following table to understand the roles of different types of users and what they are responsible for in the Data Breach Management module. From the Responsibilities column, use the links of the relative help topics to learn more about the responsibilities of the following roles.

| Persona | Skills/responsibilities | Title | Actions that can be performed in Data Breach Management |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy Officer, Data Protection Officer, Privacy Professionals | **Skills:** - In-depth knowledge and experience of data protection laws and privacy regulations. - Expertise in forensic analysis and incident response. - Organizational, facilitation, written, and oral communication skills. - Legal, operational, and financial skills. **Responsibilities:** - Establish privacy programs, ensuring confidentiality of protected information. - Conduct investigations and manage breach notifications. - Ensure compliance with privacy laws.Maintain knowledge of privacy laws and represent the organization’s privacy interests externally. | Incident Reporter or Owners | - Assess the nature of the data breach and conduct a thorough forensic analysis of the breach to understand its origin and impact. - Possess expertise in executing incident response procedures with a specific focus on effectively [addressing Privacy Incidents and Breaches](/v1/docs/using-the-enhanced-breach-impact-analysis-dashboard). - Determine whether the [breach requires notification](/v1/docs/processing-a-data-breach-report) to relevant data protection authorities as per legal requirements. - [Communication with the incident reporter](/v1/docs/using-data-breach-management-messaging) to get insights and additional information that can help in the investigation. - Understand data protection laws and regulations and ensure legal compliance with data protection regulations. |
| Compliance Officers | **Skills**: - Comprehensive understanding of legal requirements and controls. - Proficiency in compliance management and audit procedures. - Excellent communication skills and business acumen. **Responsibilities:** - Ensure adherence to regulations and internal rules. - Develop and implement legal compliance programs. - Audit processes, practices, and documents. - Educate and train employees on regulations and industry practices. | Incident Owner | Compliance officer ensures the organization's adherence to data protection and privacy regulations, including [breach notification requirements](/v1/docs/using-the-enhanced-breach-impact-analysis-dashboard) relevant to the industry and region. |
| Risk Managers | **Skills:** - Proficiency in risk assessment frameworks and methodologies. - Analytical mind with problem-solving aptitude. - Strong communication and presentation skills. **Responsibilities:** - Identify and analyze various risks (business, financial, legal, security). - Conduct risk assessments and manage risks. - Develop risk management controls and contingency plans. - Prepare reports on risk management. - Audit processes and procedures. | Breach Assessment Owner or collaborators | - Risk manager is familiarized with risk assessment frameworks and methodologies to identify and assess potential risks. - Risk managers [initiate and handle the assessment](/v1/docs/using-data-breach-assessments) and mitigation of risks, including those associated with data breaches. |
| External users or reporters | **Skills:** - Basic understanding of incident reporting mechanisms. - Familiarity with regulatory applications. **Responsibilities:** - Report incidents and provide detailed information. - Assist in preparing financial statements and disclosures. - Coordinate efforts in compliance with GAAP and SEC regulations. | Incident Reporter or Guest User | Access the [Self-Service Portal](/v1/docs/configuring-the-securiti-self-service-portal) to report an incident, track the progress of reported incidents and communicate with designated privacy professionals processing the incident. |
