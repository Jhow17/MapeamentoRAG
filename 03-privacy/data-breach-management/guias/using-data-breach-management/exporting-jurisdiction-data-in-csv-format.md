# Exporting jurisdiction data in CSV format

You can export jurisdiction data in CSV format from the **Data Breach Workbench**. To export jurisdiction report:

1. In Securiti, open **Data Breach Management** and click **Data Breach Management > Incidents.**
2. On **Data Breach Workbench**, locate an incident and near the upper right corner of the **Breach Impact Analysis** tab, click **Export Jurisdiction Report**.

![export_jurisdiction_data.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c3cad8eb-12db-1f9a-9581-95bd9f54e217.png)
3. This sends you an email containing the link to the jurisdiction data report and the password to access that report. Click the **Download File** link in the email to download the zip file on your device. The email includes the 32-character password to access the zip file.

![jurisdction_data_download_email.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-587d4ef3-264e-5227-bf0d-cbb5171c81ba.png)
