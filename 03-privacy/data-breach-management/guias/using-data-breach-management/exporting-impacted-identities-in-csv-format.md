# Exporting impacted identities in CSV format

You can export impacted identities report from the Data Breach Workbench. To export an identities report:

1. Choose **Data Breach Management > Incidents** and locate the incident of interest.
2. Near the upper right corner of the **Breach Impact Analysis** tab, click **Export Impacted Identities Report**.

![Impacted_identities.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-21b71c67-27c7-546a-3a6b-448400dbaf32.png)

Securiti sends you an email containing the link to the impacted identities report. Click the Download File link in the email to download the zip file on your device.

![impacted_Identities_download_mail.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-7dfb308a-106e-5c80-b53a-bdf08d415024.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> It takes some time to receive the exported CSV report for impacted identities, depending on the amount of data.

You can also export specific impacted identity records to gather detailed information about the affected individuals, such as their names, contact details, and the nature of the data impacted.

1. On the Data Breach Workbench, scroll down the page to **Impacted Identities** tab.
2. On the **Impacted Identities** tab, choose the record you want to export, click **Option (3 dots)** on the right side of the record and click Download Report.

![Screenshot_2024-08-28_at_12_18_40_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-fe32a27a-bc4d-fe9f-f4bf-94bc01a288ba.png)

Securiti sends you an email with a link to a report that contains details about a specific impacted identity record. Click the "Download File" link in the email to download the zip file to your device.
