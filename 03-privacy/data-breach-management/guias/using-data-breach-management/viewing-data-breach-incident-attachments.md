# Viewing data breach incident attachments

You can easily view Incident attachments from a centralized location within the Breach Workspace.

To view all the files attached to a data breach incident:

1. In Securiti, open **Data Breach Management**, then click on an incident to open it.
2. In the **Data Breach Workbench**, click the Attachments tab as shown below.

![DBM_attachments_tab.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2fe8ca4b-8fd2-0c93-9589-d22909e4276e.png)

Securiti shows you a list of the files attached to the incident.

To view a specific file, at its right edge click **View** (eye icon) as shown below.

![DBM_attachments_view.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-23758f52-67b9-24fb-09f1-cf0d5695c1d5.png)

In the context of a data breach, incident attachments refer to digital files, documents, or artifacts that incident reporters attach when reporting an incident to substantiate and provide tangible proof of the data breach.
