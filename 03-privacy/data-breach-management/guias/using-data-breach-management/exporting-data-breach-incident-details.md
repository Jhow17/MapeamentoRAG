# Exporting data breach incident details

You can export reported data breach incident details in the language of your choice, from the **Data Breach Dashboard**

To export data breach incidents details:

1. In Securiti, open **Data Breach Management**, then locate the **Reported Incidents** section.

You can also export incident details from the Data Breach Workbench. Near the upper right corner, click Export incident report.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can export incident details if you are the breach reporter or a co-owner of the incident.
2. Click **Options** (three dots) on the right side of the page, and select **Export**.
3. On the **Export Incident** popup, select the format and the language to suit.

![Export_incident_popup.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a2ccfd46-a311-5afc-bb0b-9e7f44441e15.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If you are a Windows user, you must use third-party tools like 7-Zip, WinRAR, and WinZip to access the exported DSR requests
4. Enable the **Include messages** and **Include attachments** toggles to include messages and attachments, and click **Export**.

This sends you an email containing the link to the incident details report and the password to access that report. Clicking the Download File link in the email downloads the file on your device. The email includes the 32-character password to access the file.

![Export_incident_email.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d22ccf4d-1df8-2940-655a-9f4258c91fd4.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The feature to export incident details in PDF or JSON format is not available for enhanced breach impact analysis.

### FAQ

#### 1. Due to the confidentiality of breach requests, are the PDF exports emailed? If they are emailed, are they password protected?

The exported incident report is encrypted and requires an encryption key to access it. Once you export an incident, Securiti sends you an email containing the link to the incident report and the password to access that report.
