# Implementing Control

Once a control is created, Securiti lets you track its progress, link it to risks, attach supporting evidence, and manage comments.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/implement_control.png)

## Changing control status

A control moves through five statuses: Not Implemented, In Progress, In Review, Implemented, and Expired. To change a control's status manually, on the Control Detail page, click the status dropdown in the top right and choose a new status.

![Control detail interface showing status, linked risks, and basic information sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/status_dropdown.png)

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti also sets a control's status to Expired automatically once its expiry date passes, whether or not anyone selects that status manually. Changing a control's status also affects the residual risk score of any risk it's linked to, once the control reaches Implemented status.

## Linking risks to a control

1. On the Control Detail page, click **Link Risks**.
2. In the Linked Object window, under Risks, use the search bar to find a risk by description.
3. Mark the checkbox next to each risk you want to link.
4. Click **Add**, or click **Clear Selections** to start over.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/link_risks.png)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/linked_control.png)

## Managing evidence

1. On the Control Detail page, go to the Associated Evidences tab.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/associated_evidences.png)
2. Click **+ Add New**.
3. In the Add New Evidence window, choose either:
  - **Add a Link** - paste a URL to reference an external source, document, or webpage as evidence.
  - **Add Attachment** - upload a file from your device as supporting evidence.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/new_evidence.png)
4. Click **Save**.

To download or delete evidence, mark the checkbox next to it in the Attachments, Links, or Comment Attachments list, then click **Download** or **Delete**.

## Adding comments

Click **Comments** in the top right of the Control Detail page to open the comments panel and add a comment. For more information, see [Add and manage comments in assessments](/latest/docs/understanding-workflow-stages-and-assessment-use-cases#add-and-mange-assessment-comments).

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Securiti supports up to 10 attachments in control comments.
