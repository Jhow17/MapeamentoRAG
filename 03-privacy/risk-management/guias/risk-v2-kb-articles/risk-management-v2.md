This is the space for help center articles on Risk Management (Risk V2).

(Optional) Risk Management supports Multi-Org Units. In the bottom left, click your user avatar and navigate to **Organizations**. Mark the checkbox for the organizations you want to include. Securiti’s Risk Register shows all the risks for your actively selected organizations without switching between them.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If Multi-Org units feature is not available in your tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan and whether your tenant meets the eligibility requirements for its activation.
