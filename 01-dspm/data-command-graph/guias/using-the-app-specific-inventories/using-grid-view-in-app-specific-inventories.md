# Using grid view in app-specific inventories

You can update your existing filters and view them using the grid panel at the bottom of the page. The grid panel contains two main components:

- **Entities**: On the left side of the page, all entities in the data model are listed.
- **Grid**: On the right side of the page, a grid displays all the instances for a selected entity and the counts of specific attributes for an entity.

To use the grid panel for populating data in the SDI or DAG Inventory:

1. On the left **Entities** navigation bar, select any entity and then apply the increment (+ icon) or decrement (- icon) operator to add the entity as an include or exclude rule.
2. On the grid, select any attribute for an instance and then apply the increment (+ icon) or decrement (- icon) operator and add the attribute as an include or exclude rule.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> The increment (+ icon) or decrement (- icon) operator in gray on the left side is for selecting or deselecting entities, while the one on the right side is for selecting or deselecting attributes.

Near the bottom of the page, a pop-up appears that shows the number of entities selected.
3. Click **Apply Filters** on the popup to update the filter with selected entities and their attributes. Once applied, these filters show up in the top filter component.

### Customize grid view

You can customize the grid layout according to your preferences. You can select the columns you want to see and rearrange them by dragging and dropping.

> **Note**
> 
> Securiti automatically saves your layout changes in your profile and eliminates the need for you to readjust the layout every time you visit the page.

To modify the grid layout:

1. On the grid view, near the upper right corner of the grid panel, click **reorder** (window icon).
2. Select columns from the list that you want to display in the table and click **Apply**.
3. You can also resize the columns according to your preference. To resize the columns.
  1. Hover your cursor on the column boundary till it transforms into a resize icon.
  2. Click and drag the boundary to the left or right to adjust the column width according to your preference.
  3. Release the mouse once you have reached your desired width, and the columns will resize accordingly.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Click **Restore Default** option to revert the grid layout to its default view whenever needed.
