# Troubleshooting discovery scan errors

Whenever a discovery scan for structured, unstructured, or streaming data has an error, view its details on the **Scan Definitions** page to identify the reason.

Additionally, you can access the following resources:

- Refer to the [discovery scan FAQs](/latest/docs/discovery-scan-faqs) section to find answers to common questions.
- If your discovery scan fails at any stage, view the different stages of your scan in the discovery scan job logs.

![Audit logs showing errors for DiscoveryScanJob activities on September 25, 2026.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/auditlogs_discoveryscanjobs.png)
- Update or customize your discovery scan settings. To do so, access the particular discovery scan page and identify the issues under **Scope** and **Advanced Settings**. For more information, see [Structured data](/latest/docs/classification-validation-and-fine-tuning-for-structured-data), [Unstructured data](/latest/docs/classification-validation-and-fine-tuning-for-unstructured-data), or [Streaming data](/latest/docs/classification-validation-and-fine-tuning-for-streaming-data).
- Initiate a rescan from the **Scan Definitions** page.

![Scan definitions list showing completed and error statuses for various data scans.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/rescanadiscoveryscan.png)
