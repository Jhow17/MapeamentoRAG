# Classification validation and fine tuning

Data classification identifies and categorizes data based on its sensitivity and the risks associated with it. Validating classification results and fine-tuning them when they're wrong ensures sensitive information such as personally identifiable information (PII), financial data, and intellectual property is protected according to regulatory and organizational requirements.

## Why validate classification

Validate classification to:

- Confirm data containing PII is accurately classified according to regulations such as GDPR and CCPA
- Confirm sensitive financial data is classified correctly, so appropriate security measures, such as encryption and access controls are applied
- Analyze how effectively classification identifies high-risk data
- Confirm classification aligns with organizational policy across departments and teams
- Ensure classification results correctly prioritize sensitive data during incident response

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an European Union (EU) regulation on information privacy. It aims to ensure the protection of personal information through a human rights-centric approach, and imposes obligations onto organizations anywhere, so long as they target or collect data related to people in the EU.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) was the first state-level comprehensive privacy law in the United States. It applies broadly to for-profit businesses that collect personal information from California consumers, creating certain rights for consumers and obligations for businesses in relation to personal data processing. It was amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) in 2020.
