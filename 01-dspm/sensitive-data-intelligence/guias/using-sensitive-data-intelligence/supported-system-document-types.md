# System document categories and types

Securiti supports a set of system document types grouped under predefined document categories. See the table below for all the supported system document types and their descriptions to better understand how Securiti classifies your documents during scanning.

| Document Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Academia - Other | Documents that are clearly academic in nature but don't fit any of our more specific academic document types, such as transcripts, diplomas, etc. |
| Action Plan for Condition | Medical action plan for managing a condition, such as asthma, allergy, or other emergency response. |
| Advance Directive | Advance directive expressing a patient's preferences for future healthcare decisions. For instance living wills, healthcare proxies. |
| Bank Statement | Bank account statements |
| Biometric | Biometric data files, such as fingerprint, iris, voice, or other biometric formats. |
| Birth Certificate | Official birth certificates |
| Compliance Report | Compliance report, form, or certificate, excluding general contracts and agreements. |
| Consent for Information Release | Patient authorization or consent to release medical or health information. |
| Consent for Procedure | Patient informed consent for a surgery, anesthesia, or other medical procedure. |
| Contractor Agreement | Independent contractor or consulting agreement |
| Credit Report | Consumer credit report from bureaus, such as Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion, including credit scores. |
| Death Certificate | Official death certificate |
| Dissertation | Graduate-level thesis or dissertation submitted for an academic degree. |
| Distribution Agreement | Distribution or distributor agreement granting rights to market or resell products or services. |
| Employment Agreement | Employment agreement or employment contract between an employer and employee. |
| Financial - Other | Documents that are clearly financial in nature but don't fit any of our more specific financial document types. For instance bills, invoices, etc. |
| Health Insurance Form | Health insurance claim, enrollment, or reimbursement form. For instance dental, vision, prescription drug, disability, and HSA/FSA forms. |
| License Agreement | Licensing agreement granting rights to use software, intellectual property, or other licensed assets. |
| Loan Application | Loan application form, including subsidized loans, promissory notes, and student loan request forms. |
| Marriage Certificate | Marriage certificate or marriage license |
| Master Services Agreement | Master Services Agreement (MSA) or master subscription agreement establishing the overarching terms for an ongoing services relationship. |
| Medical - Other | Documents that are clearly medical in nature but don't fit any of our more specific medical document types. For instance prescriptions, health insurance forms, etc. |
| Memorandum of Understanding | Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) describing the terms of a non-binding agreement between two or more parties. |
| Mortgage Application | Mortgage application or mortgage assistance form |
| Pay Stub | Employee pay stub or pay slip showing earnings, deductions, taxes, and pay period. |
| Payment Authorization Form | Payment authorization form authorizing recurring or one-time charges to a credit card or bank account. |
| Pending Patent | United States patent application publication for a patent that is pending grant. |
| Prescription | Medical prescription or medication form issued by a prescriber to a patient. |
| Published Article | Published research article or journal paper |
| Published Book | Published book or other long-form publication |
| Resume | Job applicant resume or curriculum vitae (CV) |
| Sales Agreement | Sales agreement or sale-of-goods contract between a seller and buyer. |
| Service Agreement | Service agreement defining the terms under which one party provides services to another. |
| Statement of Work | Statement of Work (SOW) describing the scope, deliverables, and timeline for a specific engagement. |
