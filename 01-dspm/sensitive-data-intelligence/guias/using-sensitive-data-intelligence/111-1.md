# Viewing policies in the reporting dashboard

For a deeper analysis of policy performance and trends, you can jump directly to the reporting dashboard from the Data Remediation dashboard. This gives you a comprehensive view of your selected policy's activity and metrics in one place.

To view your selected policy in reporting dashboard, near the upper right corner, click **View in Dashboard** to directly go to your selected policy reporting dashboard. For more information, see [Data Remediation system dashboards](/latest/docs/data-remediation-system-dashboards).
