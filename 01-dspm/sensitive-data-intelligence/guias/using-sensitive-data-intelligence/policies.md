# Policies

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This feature is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

Policies in Sensitive Data Intelligence let you act on the classification results generated by scans. You can configure policies to automate how identified sensitive data is handled; whether that means applying labels, triggering quarantine actions, flagging redundant or obsolete files, or registering metadata for governance purposes. Policies help you maintain control over your sensitive data, ensuring it is managed securely and in line with your organization's compliance standards.

Securiti supports the following policies in Sensitive Data Intelligence:

**Structured**

- [Structured Data Insights policy](/latest/docs/policies-configuring-structured-data-insights-policies)

**Unstructured**

- [Data Labeling policy](/latest/docs/using-data-labeling-policies)
- [File Insights policy](/latest/docs/using-file-insights-policies)
- [File Quarantine policy](/latest/docs/using-file-quarantine-policies)
- [File ROT policy](/latest/docs/using-file-rot-policies)

**General**

- [Automation policy](/latest/docs/configuring-automation-policies)
- [Data Tagging policy](/latest/docs/configuring-data-tagging-policy)
- [Metadata Registration policy](/latest/docs/using-metadata-registration-policies)
