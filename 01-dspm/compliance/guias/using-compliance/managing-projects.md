# Managing projects

You can manage projects as follows:

- Edit a project
- View project details
- Duplicate a project
- View Compliance score for a project
- Delete a project

### Editing projects

To edit a project:

1. On the Projects list, locate the card for the project you want to edit.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) > **Edit.**

![Managing_projects_options_EDIT.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b2485dd2-0328-e6eb-ea60-f2947901da28.png)
3. Update the required details and click Update **Project**.

### Viewing project details

To view details of a project:

1. On the **Projects** list, locate the card for the project you want to view details for.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) > **View.**

The details of the project display.

![Managing_projects_options_VIEW.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a4d4e258-b6a3-b367-56b0-e7e86af4ada9.png)

### Duplicating projects

To duplicate a project:

1. On the **Projects** list, locate the card for the project you want to duplicate.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) > **Duplicate**.

The duplicated project displays in the list of projects.

![Managing_projects_Duplicate.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2bc6eb04-23c8-b073-0a00-d9a77ae47c39.png)

### Disabling projects

You can enable or disable a project. To disable a project:

1. On the Projects list, locate the card for the project you want to disable.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) >**Disable**.

![Managing_projects_options_DISABLE.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1b6e2581-41f1-1601-ea64-7f393f34a4a6.png)

### Viewing Compliance score for projects

You can view the total compliance scores for a project. The compliance scores for a project are the metrics that reflects how well the project adheres to specific compliance requirements or standards. The compliance scores are project specific and can differ across projects even for the same frameworks.

To view the compliance score:

1. On the Projects list, locate the card for the project you want to view the compliance score for.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) **> View Compliance**.

![Managing_projects_options_VIEW_COMPLIANCE.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-0772cdb7-ba1c-d265-f875-42b17d5feff5.png)

### Deleting projects

To delete a project that you no longer require:

1. On the Projects list, locate the card for the project you want to delete.
2. At the right end of the card, choose **Options** (three dots) > **Delete**.

Securiti opens a confirmation box.

![Managing_projects_options_DELETE.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-60197f08-9a46-fd63-afd9-2dfce6923a0f.png)
3. Click **Yes Project** to confirm and delete the project.
