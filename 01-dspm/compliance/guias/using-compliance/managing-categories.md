# Managing categories

A compliance framework category is a broad grouping of related compliance requirements or objectives. For instance, 'Data Cataloging and Classification' is a category in the CDMC framework. You can add a custom category or use system-defined categories in frameworks.

To add a new category:

1. At the upper right corner of the Framework dashboard, click **Create > New Category**. You can also select the framework from the list and at the middle right side, click **Create Category**.

![Frameworks_-_Create_new_category.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4e6793cf-497e-0623-fa3f-6b2abe063dc4.png)
2. Choose **Framework** from the menu against which you are adding a category. Enter the **Category Name**, and **Category Description**, choose **Sub-categories** from the menu, and click **Create Category**.

### Edit a category

You can edit custom category details such as its name and description.

1. On the **Frameworks** list, select the framework and at the middle right side of the page click **Option**(3 dots) next to the category card and select **Edit**.
2. Update the **Category Name**, **Description**, and click **Update Category**.

![Frameworks_Category_Edit.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e87faf27-eb0b-7060-27ba-82ef2f6dd85f.png)

Similarly, to delete a category, next to the category card choose **Options** (3 dots) **> Delete**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can only edit or delete custom categories, but you cannot edit or delete the system categories within the module.

### Duplicate a category

You can duplicate a custom category.

1. On the **Frameworks** list, select the framework.
2. To the middle-right side of the page click **Option**(3 dots) next to the **category** card and select **Duplicate**.
3. To duplicate the category to the same framework, click **to the same Framework**. The category is duplicated.
4. To duplicate the category to a different framework:
  1. Click **to a different Framework**. A list of custom frameworks displays.
  2. Click on the required framework. The category is duplicated in the selected framework.
