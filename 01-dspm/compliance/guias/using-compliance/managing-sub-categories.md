# Managing sub-categories

A Subcategory has a control library populated against it. You can have a list of system sub-categories or add a custom subcategory as per your requirements.

1. On the **Framework** Dashboard, in the upper right corner, Click **Create > New Sub-Category**.

![Create_new_subcategory.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-690c178a-e686-f97a-c8ba-685ad294b9c8.png)

You can also click on the specific category from the list of categories created against a framework and further click **Option** (3 dots) **> Create Sub-Category**.
2. Choose Framework and Category from the respective menus. Enter the Sub-category Name and Description. Further, select Controls from the list.
3. Once done, click **Create Sub-Category**.

### Edit a sub-category

You can edit a sub-category's details such as its name and description.

1. On the **Frameworks** list, select the framework and expand the respective category against it.
2. At the middle right side of the page click **Option(**3 dots) next to the sub-category card and select **Edit**.

![Frameworks_subcategory_Edit.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c3ba83f2-d503-a080-2f2f-4fd909626c85.png)
3. Update the **Sub-Category Name, Description** and select controls that need to be included in the sub-category, and click **Update Sub-Category**.

Similarly, to delete a sub-category, next to the sub-category card choose **Options** (3 dots) **> Delete**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can only edit or delete custom sub-categories, but you cannot edit or delete the system sub-categories within the module.

To add controls associated with the sub-categories, see [Controls Overview](/v1/docs/controls-overview)

### Duplicate a sub-category

You can duplicate a custom subcategory.

1. On the Frameworks list, select the framework.
2. To the middle-right side of the page, click **Option** (3 dots) next to the subcategory card and select **Duplicate**.
3. To duplicate the subcategory to the same framework:
  1. Click **to the same Framework**.
  2. Choose **category**. The subcategory is duplicated in the selected category.
4. To duplicate the subcategory to a different framework:
  1. Click **to a different Framework**. A list of custom frameworks displays.
  2. Click on the required framework.
  3. Choose **category**. The subcategory is duplicated in the selected framework.

A subcategory in a compliance framework is a subset of a category consisting of controls. For instance, 5.1-Leadership and commitment is a subcategory of the category Leadership in the ISO/IEC 27001:2013 framework.
