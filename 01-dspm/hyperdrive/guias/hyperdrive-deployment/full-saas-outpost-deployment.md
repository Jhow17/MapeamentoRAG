# Full SaaS Hyperdrive

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If Full SaaS Hyperdrive deployment is not available in your Securiti tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan. For this feature, you require a separate subscription plan.

Securiti Full SaaS Hyperdrive deployment provides a fully hosted and managed infrastructure solution for customers who require seamless scanning of large unstructured datasets without managing cloud resources. Hosted in Securiti’s cloud environment, it eliminates the need to deploy and maintain infrastructure, making it an ideal choice for businesses with restricted cloud services or limited computing resources. This deployment model scans unstructured data at high scale and adapts to your data size.

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> Outpost hosted in your AWS cloud is an alternative deployment model to Full SaaS Hyperdrive deployment. You can purchase only one deployment.
> 
> For more information on deploying Securiti Outpost in your AWS infrastructure, see [Securiti Outpost - AWS](/latest/docs/securiti-outpost).

See the following table to understand the key features Securiti provides in full SaaS Hyperdrive.

| Key feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Fully managed infrastructure | It deploys and manages the complete infrastructure in Securiti’s AWS cloud account, including updates and resource management. |
| High-scale data scanning | It scans unstructured data at high scale within a short time. |
| Infrastructure overhead | It reduces infrastructure management by 80% to 90%. |
| Rapid tenant onboarding | It reduces the time required to onboard a tenant and start scanning. |
| Tenant isolation | It ensures strict separation of data, credentials, and workloads within a shared environment. |

## Supporting use case

Outpost is designed for small and medium businesses that require a fully managed, SaaS-like data scanning solution without managing infrastructure.

### Supporting connectors

It supports the following Microsoft 365 applications:

- SharePoint online
- OneDrive
- Outlook
- Teams

## Deploying Full SaaS Hyperdrive

Outpost does not require your cloud infrastructure to deploy. It uses Securiti’s infrastructure to isolately deploy for your tenant.

To get Outpost deployed for your data systems, proceed with the following steps:

1. Consult your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm plan and enable this.

It deploys the Outpost inside Securiti’s cloud for your tenant.

### Full SaaS Hyperdrive deployment management

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> Securiti does not let you make the infrastructure level changes for SaaS Outpost deployment.

Full SaaS Hyperdrive deployment is fully managed by Securiti team within its infrastructure. It also does not provide a user interface for managing its details or infrastructure updates. However, you can configure your connectors to connect your data sources. It only displays the list of configured Outposts in the table.
