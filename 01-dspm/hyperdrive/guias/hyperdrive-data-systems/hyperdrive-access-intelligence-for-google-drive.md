# Hyperdrive Access Intelligence for Google Drive

## Overview

Hyperdrive Access Intelligence for Google Drive gives you visibility into how files are used and who can access them in Google Drive. It brings together two capabilities, Activity Analysis and Entitlement Scan, so you can monitor real user activity and understand access and ownership structures across your Google Drive environment.

## Access Intelligence capabilities

- **Activity Analysis** - Analyzes Drive activity logs to show how users, OAuth applications, service account agents, and Google first-party systems interact with files and folders, including views, downloads, edits, renames, and permission changes. For more information, see [Hyperdrive Activity Analysis for Google Drive](/latest/docs/hyperdrive-activity-analysis-for-google-drive-google-workspace).
- **Entitlement Scan** - Maps access for Google Drive files by capturing which users and AI agents can access discovered files, and what sensitive data those files contain. For more information, see [Hyperdrive Entitlement Scan for Google Drive](/latest/docs/hyperdrive-entitlement-scan-for-google-drive).

## Prerequisites

Before configuring Access Intelligence for Google Drive, ensure the [Google Drive (Google Workspace)](/latest/docs/google-drive-google-workspace) connector is configured and synced in Hyperdrive.
