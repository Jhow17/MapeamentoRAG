# Security posture rules library and severity

The Security Posture Rules identify any cloud service misconfigurations and monitor changes in policy or metadata. Any detected change triggers an alert in the system and generates findings with the following severity levels:

| Severity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Very High | A very high severity indicates that the data identified by this rule poses a critical threat to the system or data. It can lead to breaches, severe financial losses, or major regulatory penalties. Immediate attention is required to assess the identified data and take action, either manually or through automatic remediation. |
| High | A high severity indicates that the data identified by this rule is at serious risk and could cause substantial harm to the system or data, potentially leading to significant data loss, financial damage, or legal consequences. While immediate attention is required, it may not be as urgent as a very high severity issue. You should assess the identified data and plan to address the issue either manually or through automatic remediation. |
| Medium | Medium severity indicates a moderate risk with a noticeable impact on your data. While it may cause some disruption or financial loss, it is generally manageable. Remediation actions for medium risks should be prioritized after addressing very high and high severity risks. |
| Low | A low severity indicates a minor risk with limited impact on your system or data. While it is unlikely to cause significant harm, it may still affect operations or result in minor losses. Regular monitoring is recommended, and issues should be addressed as part of best security practices. |
| Very Low | A very low severity indicates a minimal risk with negligible impact on your system or data. These issues are typically monitored and documented but may not require immediate action. You can defer resolution or address them as part of a long-term security pl |
