# Messaging center

**Scenario**

As a data steward, you may have to manage dataset definitions and glossary consistency. Instead of juggling emails to resolve mismatches or clarify term ownership, you use messaging center to tag owners directly on glossary pages, ask questions about schema changes, or suggest metadata updates. Discussions stay linked to the asset, helping you capture tribal knowledge and enforce governance with less back-and-forth.

With messaging center you can collaborate in EDC by commenting, asking questions and receiving feedback on EDC assets. It is a significant step forward in capturing and leveraging tribal knowledge, ensuring that all users gain a comprehensive understanding of the assets available in the Data Catalog.

### Scope

Currently, the messaging center supports conversations around the following EDC entities:

- Dataset
- Glossary term
- Glossary term group
- Data products

The messaging channel of a data product accurately reflects the latest ownership information for the related glossary terms, assets, and domains each time the channel is opened.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> If this feature is not available in your tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to determine if this feature is included in your subscription and if your tenant meets the requirements to use this functionality.

### Working with the messaging center

1. Open the dataset details/glossary term/glossary term group page upon which you want to collaborate with others.
2. At the middle right of the page, near the **Share** button, click **Options** (three dots) > **Start Conversation**.
3. On the **Messages & Notifications** page, start the conversation for the selected asset by composing and sending messages to the other team members.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> - The system automatically includes the entity owner when someone initiates a conversation about an entity. This ensures that owners can monitor and participate in discussions regarding their entities.
> - You can add an owner to an existing channel by assigning the same owner to the entity linked to that channel.
> - If you remove an owner from the entity, that owner also gets removed from the messaging center associated with that entity.
> - When you add or remove tag owners on a data product, these changes are automatically reflected in the data product’s messaging channel.
> - An owner will only be removed from the messaging channel if they have not made any comments. If they have contributed comments, they will remain in the channel to preserve the historical context.

#### Messaging & Notifications operations

1. On the **Messages & Notifications** page, the left bar displays a list of all conversations in which you are tagged or have initiated.
2. Click on any conversation to open its detail view.
3. The detail view section lets you perform the following operations:

| Operation | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Comments on assets | - You can comment on assets to collaborate and interact with peers. - You can tag other EDC users in the comments to notify them about the message. > [!NOTE] > **Note** > > These operations let you provide your knowledge on assets as well as lean about assets from other’s knowledge. All the comments made on an asset are grouped in a conversation for ease. - You can add a link to your comments by simply pasting the URL into the comment box and pressing the **Enter** key. - You can add multiple lines in a comment. |

#### Editing comments

You can edit your comment anytime.

1. Click **Options** (three dots) located at the left side of the message and select **Edit Message**.
2. Edit the comment and click **Save Changes**.

#### Deleting comments

You can delete your comment anytime.

1. Click **Options** (three dots) located at the left side of the message and select **Delete Message**.
2. Click **Delete**.

#### Receiving notifications

Whenever a user tags you in a comment, you get notified through a number badge on the Messages & Notifications icon. Inside the **Messages & Notifications** page, you can see a visual indication of conversations with unread comments.
