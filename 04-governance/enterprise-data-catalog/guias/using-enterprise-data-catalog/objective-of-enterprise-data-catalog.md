# Objective of Enterprise Data Catalog

Firstly, you have to identify the primary goals of the data catalog, such as improving data accessibility, enhancing data governance, or supporting analytics.

The following are the steps to determine the objective of the Enterprise Data Catalog. Understanding the objectives of the data catalog shapes its design and functionality. Common goals include:

- Improving data accessibility: Easily find, understand, and use the data across the enterprise.
- Enhancing Data Governance: Better control over data usage, improving compliance with regulations, and ensuring data quality.
- Supporting analytics and decision-making: Data-driven insights by making reliable, comprehensive data easily accessible for analytics, reporting, and business intelligence.

### How can you identify goals?

- **Consult key stakeholders**: Engage with data owners, business users, and executives to understand their data needs.
- **Evaluate current challenges**: Identify gaps in existing data processes, such as difficulties in finding data, poor data quality, or compliance risks.
- **Set measurable outcomes**: Define success metrics, such as improved data discoverability or reduced time for data access.
