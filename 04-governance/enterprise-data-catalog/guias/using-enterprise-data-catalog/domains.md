# Domains

**Scenario**

As a data steward, you need to organize datasets for better governance and ownership alignment. Using tags alone makes it hard to enforce structure or assign responsibility. With domains, you can group related datasets under a single category, such as Finance or HR. This lets you standardize organization, simplify access, and align datasets to the right teams and business units.

Domain is a set of categories used to group and organize similar datasets. You can create a domain and manually add datasets that fall under the same domain. Unlike tags, you can associate only one domain to a dataset.

Domains organize datasets and assets into business areas (for example, Finance, HR, or Sales), while [Glossary terms](/latest/docs/glossary-terms) provide standardized business definitions for data concepts. Together, they link business context to data assets. Domains help you discover and organize assets, and glossary terms help them understand, search, and govern those assets consistently.

You must have **Manage Domains** platform privilege to add domains at the entity level. For more information, see [EDC RBAC](/latest/docs/edc-rbac-permissions-and-roles).

**Best practice**: If you are registering data assets via discovery scan, then make sure you have created domains ahead of configuring the discovery scan. By doing this, you can have assets in the Enterprise Data Catalog already assigned to the correct owner.

To create domains:

1. Open **Enterprise Data Catalog**, from the top right corner of the page, and click **Govern > Domains**.
2. On the domains page, click **+ New Domain**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can create a new domain by clicking the + icon located at the top of the left sidebar.
3. On the **Create New Domain** popup:

Once a domain is created, it gets displayed on both the left sidebar and the main area of the domains page. In the main area, you can also view the number of entities contained within each domain.
  1. Select the parent domain (optional).
  2. Enter the name and description of your new domain.
  3. Expand the **Advanced** section to enter a custom ID. A random ID is generated by default for each domain.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Description and custom ID are optional fields.
4. Click **Create**.

### Creating subdomains

You can create domains inside the domains to make a clear hierarchical structure that mirrors your organizational or data taxonomy. This makes it easier to navigate and locate specific datasets.

Each subdomain can represent a specific context or business area, making it easier to manage and understand data in relation to its use case.

To create a subdomain:

1. On the domains page, click **+ New Domain**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You can create a new subdomain by clicking the + icon located at the top of the left sidebar.
2. On the **Create New Domain** popup:
  1. Choose the **parent domain** where you want to create a subdomain.
  2. Enter the name and description of your new subdomain.
  3. Expand the **Advanced** section to enter a custom ID.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> Description and custom ID are optional fields.
3. Click **Create**.

Once a domain is created, it is displayed on the left sidebar. To view a subdomain, click the down arrow next to the parent domain under which the subdomain is created.

### Configuring domains

1. Click on a domain to configure the following details:

| Tab | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Entities | Entities are the datasets that fall under that domain. To add entities: 1. Click **Add assets** located at the top right. 2. On the **Add assets to Domain**, link assets. |
| Documentation | The description of that specific domain. You can add documentation or help links directly from the **Documentation** tab or the right sidebar under the **About** section. |
| Data products | Data products associated with this domain. Read the data products section for details. |
| Properties | The extracted properties of the domains. |
2. Use the right sidebar to add owners or update documentation

#### Adding properties to domains

You can create properties for domains to provide additional context and enhance their usability. Once a property is added, you can easily view, edit, or remove it as needed.

**To add a new property to the domain:**

1. Open a domain, and click the **Properties** tab.
2. Click **+ Add New Property**.
3. On the **Add a new property** popup, enter a property name and value.
4. Click **Add**.

**To edit the property of the domain:**

1. Open **Properties** tab for any domain.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. On the **Edit Property** popup, enter a property value.
4. Click **Save**.

**To delete a property of the domain:**

1. Click the delete icon.
2. On the **Delete Property** popup, click **Delete**.

### Editing domains

Once the domain is created you can edit its:

- Name
- Entities
- Documentation
- Data products
- Owners

You can even move the domain to another domain as well.

- Click **Options** (three dots) located adjacent to the Share button.
- Click **Move** and select the domain where you want to move this domain.

### Deleting domains

1. Click **Options** (three dots) located adjacent to the Share button.
2. Click **Delete**.

### Sharing domains

1. Click the Share button located at the top right side and choose one of the following:
  1. Click **Copy link**.
  2. Click **Copy URN**.

### Assigning domains to assets

1. Navigate to the entity's detail page.
2. At the bottom left sidebar, navigate to the **Domains** section, and click **Set Domain**.
3. On the **Set Domain** popup, search and link the domain to the asset.

### Domains URN

You can uniquely identify any registered domain in the Enterprise Data Catalog with a URN. URN stands for Uniform Resource Name.

Example: urn:li:domain:Product

### Audit trail for domains

You can view comprehensive insights about how a domain has changed over time. This can help you make informed decisions and better manage your assets.

To view the audit trail:

1. Open Enterprise Data Catalog, then land on the **Domains** page.
2. Open any domain for which you want to see an audit trail.
3. On the upper right corner of the page, click **Change Log** to view the following audit trail details:

| Value | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Time | The time when each change occurred. |
| User | Name of the user responsible for the change. |
| Activity | Nature of the modification (for example, add, edit, delete). |
| Attribute | Specific aspects of the asset that changed. |
| New value | Updated information. |
| Old value | Previous state for a complete historical perspective. |
| Message | Additional context through accompanying messages. |

### Data products

Data products are structured and managed sets of data designed to meet specific business needs or use cases within an organization. Curated and maintained by domain experts, these products ensure accuracy, reliability, and relevance.

Each data product is an independent unit managed by a specific domain team. This team is responsible for defining, publishing, and maintaining their data assets, ensuring high-quality data that meets customer needs.

To perform actions on Data Products, enable the corresponding permission(s) for the required operation. Only the permission(s) associated with the action you intend to perform are required. For more information, see [EDC RBAC](/latest/docs/edc-rbac-permissions-and-roles).

**Creating data products**

To create a data product, you must first navigate to the domain that will contain this data product.

1. Open Enterprise Data Catalog, then **Govern > Domains**.
2. On the domains page, click any domain containing this data product.
3. Navigate to the **Data Products** tab and click **+ New Data Product**.
4. On the **Create new Data Product** popup, add name and description.
5. Click **Create**.

**Viewing data products**

You can search for the created data products in any of the following two ways:

- Navigate to the Enterprise Data Catalog dashboard and click the **Data Products** widget.

OR

- Navigate to the entity details page.
- Scroll down the page and click Data Products located at the bottom of the right sidebar.

OR

- Click any domain that has data products linked.
- Click **Data Products** tab to view the list of data products linked to the domain.

**Editing data products**

1. On the top right side of the data product, click the **Edit icon**.
2. On the **Edit Data Product** popup, update the name and/or description.
3. Click **Update**.

**Deleting data products**

- On the top right side of the data product, click **Options (three dots) > Delete**.

**Linking data products to data entities**

1. On an entity's detail page, navigate to the right panel to locate the Data Product section.
2. Click **Set Data Product**.
3. On the **Set Data Product** popup, search for the data product you'd like to add this asset to.
4. When you are done, click **Add**.

## FAQs

#### What is the difference between domains, tags, and glossary terms?

Enterprise Data Catalog supports tags, glossary terms, and domains as distinct types of metadata that are suited for specific purposes.

**Tags**: Informal, loosely controlled labels that serve as a tool to group assets to assist in search and discovery. Assets may have multiple tags. Although there is no formal, central management, admins may perform tasks to consolidate tags.

**Glossary terms**: Glossaries contain a list of terms typically organized in taxonomies. They capture business metadata and provide a controlled or governed vocabulary. Terms are typically used to standardize common business terminology and to provide an understanding of data assets.

**Domains**: A set of top-level logical groupings of assets (that is business and technical). They are usually aligned to organizational units to which the assets are most relevant. Central or distributed management. You can assign a single domain per data asset.

Domain is a logical or thematic grouping of related data assets. Domains are used to categorize and organize data assets based on their subject matter or the business context they belong to. For example, you might have a Finance domain that includes data related to financial transactions, reports, and analysis, or a Human Resources domain that encompasses data related to employee information, payroll, and HR processes.
