# Getting Started with Assessment Automation

### What is Assessment Automation

Securiti Assessment Automation is designed to help you operationalize privacy by design and comply with global privacy framework requirements.

Our solution includes a wide array of predefined content ready for you to launch from day 1 including:

- Simple privacy impact assessments for everyday use
- Readiness assessments for privacy regulations across the globe
- Vendor onboarding assessments to discover third party risk
- Control questionnaires to audit internal compliance against popular frameworks such as NIST, ISO, and more

You can fully control how assessments are configured according to your organization’s needs.

### Who uses Assessment Automation

When using the assessment solution, an organization's users typically fall into one or more categories.

#### Admins

- Users typically responsible for setting up and maintaining the assessment module
- Create, Edit, and Publish templates
- Monitor and report assessment status

#### Respondents

#### 

- Typically users from the business or vendors
- Answer questions
- Submit Assessments
- Provide additional details and attachments

#### Reviewer

- Typically users from the compliance team including privacy, security, or procurement
- Review responses
- Set Risk
- Flag questions for additional information
- Approve assessments

### Dig into specific features

#### Reviewer Respondent Assignment

Exercise fine-grain control on reviewer Securiti lets you assign reviewers on assessments for approval and also, you can mark participants as mandatory approvers. Then you have respondents who you've invited to collaborate on the assessment. As Assessment Owner, once you have specified roles, get ready to perform assessment.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/understanding-assignment-in-reviewer-respondent)

#### Defined Lifecycle Stages

Track assessment lifecycle stages easily. Workflow Stages guides you through the assessment's journey in real-time. You're able to tell clearly by looking if a particular assessment is "Not Started," "In Progress," "In Review" or "Complete." These visual indicators help you to answer any pending/mandatory questions, review assessments promptly, and provide supplementary information (if required).

[Learn more](/v1/docs/tracking-assessment-stages)

#### Submission and review flows

Submit and review assessments in a streamlined manner. One click Submit lets you submit all your answers and send them to the relevant team for review. Similarly, one click Approve button lets you approve all questions (if the information is 100% accurate). In the case, where it isn't, you have the ability to send back the assessment.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/answering-assessment-questions-in-securiti)

#### Filtering experience

Filter assessments and manage Saved Views. On the assessments list, you can sort view to suit. You have the option to sort by Assessment ID & Title, Stage, Associated Template, Risk Rating, Assessment Version, Submission and Review percentages, Due and Published dates, and so on.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/assessments-list-filter-panel-and-saved-views)

Workflow Stages relate to the assessment lifecycle in V2. A typical Securiti assessment has four stages: Not Started, In Progress, In Review, and Complete. The Not Started stage is when you haven't begun answering. As soon as you interact with at least one question, the stage changes to In Progress. After the assessment is submitted, the reviewer is called In Review. Once the concerned parties approve, the assessment transitions to the Complete stage.

Risk is the likelihood of effects or implications that would negatively impact what you value. Risk in Securiti refers to risks that may arise when conducting internal or vendor assessments. You can track them using the risk dashboard.

The term Respondent is specific to V2 assessments who can answer and submit assessments. It refers to the user who was previously a Collaborator in the V1 world. Multiple respondents can be added to the assessment, similar to how more than one collaborator can be added. Also, a Guest User can be added as a Respondent.

Reassessing an assessment lets you identify changes in regulatory compliance and organizational behaviors. As a Question Owner, you can modify the reassessment by filling in new responses or resubmitting previous answers. Where response option(s) are modified, the question is treated as new with no answers.
