# Getting Started with Privacy Center

Privacy Center is a centralized location that lets you access and try out several features of the Securiti's privacy modules that ensure global data privacy and compliance for your organization. With this module, you can create a customized Privacy Center for your organization by configuring Privacy Notices, Cookie Banners and Preference Centers, Universal Preference Centers, and DSR Forms.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Privacy%20Center-PC-GS-Workflow.drawio(1).svg)

### Fill in the Registration Form

Securiti's Privacy Center module is available for subscription through multiple sources. You can subscribe directly via the [**AWS Marketplace**](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-g3keyjg2vmlhm?sr=0-1&amp;ref_=beagle&amp;applicationId=AWSMPContessa), the official Securiti [**website**](https://securiti.ai/), or through the **Securiti platform** itself. Simply complete the registration form shown below, and Securiti will create a Privacy Center tailored to your domain, enabling data privacy management.

![PC-1_SS2024-09-02_at_12_42_47_PM.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-4e755228-e5e9-b421-1310-73da4fe80024.png)

#### Step 1 - Create Your Privacy Center

Securiti's Privacy Center module lets you create and fully customize a Privacy Center for your domain, with options to preview before publishing it. You can personalize various elements to suit your organizational preferences.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/build-your-privacy-center-in-a-day)

![Getting_Started_-1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-c02022fd-fd39-141a-787a-fe963c1916bc.png)

#### Step 2 - Add Co-Owner(s) to Your Privacy Center

Securiti lets you add co-owner(s) into your Privacy Center(s) for better collaboration. When you add co-owners, Securiti also grants them ownership in the associated Privacy Notice and DSR form.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/adding-co-owners-to-your-privacy-center)

![Getting_Started_-2.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e8e33f33-4360-de03-0111-b5997c635f8f.png)

#### Step 3 - Customize Home Page and Universal Styling

After creating your Privacy Center, you can customize its homepage elements according to your domain's branding requirements, including the **Header**, **Jump Cards**, **Feature list**, etc. You can also adjust the universal styling such as button colors, font styles, and button shapes, to align with your branding.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/customizing-your-privacy-center)

#### Step 5 - Embed Domain(s) in Your Privacy Center

Once you have configured your Privacy Center, you can embed domain(s) in it. The domain(s) that you wish to embed in the Privacy Center could be the subdomain(s) or external domains associated with your organization.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/embedding-domains-in-a-privacy-center)

![Getting_Started-5.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e1ccc240-65f0-f0ad-91d5-331f23b4b124.png)

#### Step 6 - Preview Your Privacy Center

You can preview the entire Privacy Center in the different modes such as **Desktop View**, **Tablet View**, and **Mobile View** at the same tab as it would appear to a website visitor at any time while creating it. Securiti also lets you open the preview in a separate browser tab.

To learn how to preview your privacy center before publishing it, refer to the following video:

[Embedded content](https://player.vimeo.com/video/785523096)

#### Step 7 - Publish Your Privacy Center

Once you have created and preview your privacy center, you can publish it to your website. This ensures that your Privacy Center is accessible to your data subjects and fully compliant with local data protection regulations.

To learn how to publish your privacy center, refer to the following video:

Cookies preferences are user settings that dictate how websites use and store data through cookies, providing control over tracking, personalized content, and site functionality. In the Privacy Center module, you can use the Cookies Preferences section to build and customize a cookie banner and preference center for your domain(s). Cookie banner and preference center enhance the data subject’s control over data, ensuring personalized online experiences while respecting privacy.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) mandates organizations to prominently feature a Do Not Sell My Personal Information link. This link provides guidance on opting out of the sale of data of individuals such as data subjects or website visitors etc. Using Privacy Center, you can enable or disable this option for your data subjects.
