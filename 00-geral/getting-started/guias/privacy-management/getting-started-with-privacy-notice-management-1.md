# Getting Started with Privacy Notice Management

Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management module lets you quickly create privacy notices for your domains. These privacy notices are customized to comply with applicable regulations for your region to ensure that your business meets legal requirements.

This guide lists the required steps for creating a privacy notice, from setting up the privacy notice template to customizing the content based on specific legal standards. With the Privacy Notice Management module, you can ensure that your privacy notices are clear, compliant, and up-to-date, helping to protect both your data subjects and your business.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Privacy%20Notice%20Management.drawio.svg)

### Step 1 - Select a Privacy Notice Template

Securiti’s Privacy Notice Management lets you create privacy notices in four ways: using a template, starting with a blank notice, using guided wizards, or importing existing PDF, DOCX, and TXT files. The guided wizards include ready-to-use Global, Brazil, UAE, and AI Notice templates that help meet the privacy legal requirements of different jurisdictions.

[Learn more](/v1/docs/creating-a-new-privacy-notice)

![Options to create a new privacy notice with various starting points displayed on the dashboard.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/privacy_notice_management_methods.png)

### Step 2 - Set Up Privacy Notice Sections

You can set up privacy notice sections by adding the required information in the privacy notice section panels. The Privacy Notice Management supports four creation methods. In each method, the privacy notice sections can be set up by configuring the necessary information in the section panels.

### Step 3 - Add Co-Owners and Approvers to collaborate

You can add co-owner(s) and approver(s) to your privacy notice to collaborate with them easily as well as approving privacy notice before publishing. You can only converse back and forth with the co-owner(s) of your privacy notices on which you have already initiated conversations.

[Learn more](/latest/docs/adding-and-removing-privacy-notice-co-owners)

Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) or simply called General Data Protection Law is Brazil’s first comprehensive legislation aimed at protecting the fundamental right of privacy. It applies to every natural person or legal entity, either public or private, that handles personal data of Brazilian citizens. The LGPD can also apply extraterritorially similar to the GDPR. You can choose between Global or LGPD template in Privacy Notice Management module.

The Personal Data Protection Law, 2021 (PDPL) is United Arab Emirates’ (UAE) federal data protection law that became effective on 02 January 2022. It applies to individuals who reside in UAE or have a place of business in UAE, organizations established in the UAE, and organizations established outside UAE if they process personal data of individuals in UAE.
