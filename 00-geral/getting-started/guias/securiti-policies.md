# Policies

## Overview

Securiti's Policies gives you a centralized place to create, manage, and enforce rules across your data systems. It covers twelve policy types spanning four modules, also referred to as apps in the main navigation sidebar.

All policy types use scan results and metadata that other Securiti modules generate, letting you act on findings at scale without switching between tools.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/policies.drawio.svg)

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> - You must have the relevant module subscription, for example, SDI for File Insights or Automation Policies, and SPM for Security Posture Policies. If a policy is not available in your tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan and whether your tenant meets the eligibility requirements for its activation.
> - Your role must also have the required permissions to view, create, and manage policies. For more information, see [Policy Engine RBAC Roles and Permissions](/latest/docs/policy-engine-rbac-roles-and-permissions).

> [!WARNING]
> Important
> 
> This feature is available in Securiti SaaS deployments but is not currently available in Securiti Sovereign deployments. Contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative for updates on its availability in your tenant.

### Supported policies in Securiti

Securiti offers the following policy types. Each policy type targets a specific area of data risk, from detecting cloud misconfigurations to governing AI workloads. Depending on your use case, you can apply one or more of the following across your data environment.
