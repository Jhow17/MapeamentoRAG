# What's new in 1.135

### Tracking Usage Across All Consent Modules

Ever wondered how your data subjects actually interact with your consent experience?

Consent Usage Metrics brings domain, mobile, and universal consent data into one view and shows you what’s working, and where engagement drops off. You can now track consent activity across your domains, mobile apps, and privacy platform integrations. Spot trends across key data points such as consent reads, writes, top sources, and more. Whether you’re comparing usage across channels or just looking at a single consent module, this helps you focus where it matters. You can also export the metric widgets as PDF or PPTX, email them to others, or do both at once.

Explore the [Cookie](/v1/docs/using-the-cookie-consent-usage-metrics), [Universal](/v1/docs/using-the-universal-consent-usage-metrics), and [Mobile App Consent Usage Metric](/v1/docs/using-the-mobile-consent-usage-metrics) to watch patterns unfold

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-8Q1AV84E.png)

### Editing Domain Configurations Used to Be a Chore. Not Anymore

Updating domain settings one by one can eat up your time. Now you don’t have to.

You can select multiple domains and update their cookie configurations in one go. Change configurations such as the deployment model (manual or automatic SDK), turn on ADA compliance, enable strict CSP, or adjust auto-blocking, all from one place. No back and forth, no repeated edits. Just select domain, bulk edit configs, save changes and deploy.

[Learn the art of bulk editing domain configurations](/v1/docs/editing-the-domain-configurations-in-bulk)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-QBKUZ27R.png)

### Revamped Domains Dashboard: Smarter Filters, Quicker Edits, More Visibility

Managing dozens of domains can get overwhelming fast.

The redesigned Domains dashboard gives you a clear view of each domain’s setup, including scan status, deployment model, cookie banners, and consent frameworks. You'll see everything upfront. A new metadata card shows key config details without jumping between pages. You can label domains, filter them using the expression builder, and apply changes in bulk. It's all designed to help you manage hundreds or even thousands of domains more easily and efficiently.

[Explore the revamped dashboard](/v1/docs/using-the-cookie-consent-domains-dashboard)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-MPD3992I.png)

### Rules-based DSR Assignment

Looping in the relevant team members for DSR requests just got easier. You can now set up rules to assign DSR request owners based on request type, jurisdiction, data subject type, and custom fields—like checkboxes or radio buttons. You decide the criteria, and Securiti takes care of the handoff. For example, if the form indicates the person is an employee in the EU, both HR and the EU privacy team can be added automatically. This helps avoid manual misrouting and keeps the right people engaged.

[Learn more about it](/v1/docs/configuring-rules-in-the-dsr-form)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-QRTLSFTU.png)

### DSR Form and Template Portability Across Tenants

Keeping templates and forms consistent across environments doesn’t have to be tedious. You can now export and import DSR forms in JSON format and notification templates in CSV. Export one or all templates in the languages you need. Use them to migrate setups, create backups, or sync things between tenants. We’ll also send an email summary after each import or export to show what went through and what didn’t. Simple steps to keep your setup clean and consistent.

Learn more about importing and exporting [forms](/v1/docs/exporting-and-importing-dsr-forms) and [templates](/v1/docs/configuring-dsr-email-notification-templates)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-EI6XOD74.png)

### Enhance Assessments with Dedicated Reviewer Questions

Need a way to separate what reviewers need to answer from what respondents should handle? You can now mark questions as "Reviewer Only." Respondents can submit the assessment without them.

Once it moves into a review stage, reviewers can fill them in or leave comments. These questions are easy to spot when assigning or previewing templates. And if you have multiple reviewers, they can all jump in on the same question.

[Learn more about answering assessments with Review Teams](/v1/docs/answering-assessment-questions-in-securiti#answer-regular-assessment-questions)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-9Q3BTA5N.png)

### Personalize Privacy Center with Custom Fonts

If the default fonts in your Privacy Center don’t match your brand, you can now change that.

Upload your custom fonts, preview them right in the editor, and publish when it’s ready. Whether you’re aligning with your brand’s design or adding a personal touch, this gives you more control over the look and feel of your privacy pages.

[Learn how to customize your Privacy Center](/v1/docs/build-your-privacy-center-in-a-day)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-VN92AY6X.png)

### Scan Oracle for Transparent Data Encryption Posture

It’s easy to miss when encryption isn’t set up correctly in Oracle. With posture scan, you can now catch gaps in Transparent Data Encryption (TDE) across your self-hosted Oracle databases. The platform checks encryption status and shows you what’s protected and what’s not—so you can fix issues before they become a risk.

[Show me how to do it](/v1/docs/activate-the-oracle-connector-in-securiti)

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-PQT6EZ70.png)

### Asset Discovery Now Covers More Cloud Services

If you’re storing data in Azure Data Lake or GCP AlloyDB, Securiti can now discover these assets. This covers both AlloyDB Clusters and Instances in GCP. These services often support large-scale data workloads, so having them in discovery helps you identify and manage critical resources. It’s especially useful for teams working across environments where key services might otherwise get overlooked.

[Read more about it](/v1/docs/data-security-posture-management-solution#list-of-supported-cloud-native-services)
