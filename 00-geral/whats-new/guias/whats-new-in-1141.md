Cookie consent is entering the AI era. Build with AI lets you configure fully compliant, geolocation-based cookie banners using simple prompts instead of manual setup. ### Your Cookie Scans, Your Schedule

When website updates happen on their own schedule, cookie scans shouldn't lag behind. With custom scan scheduling, you can now control exactly when cookie domain scans run. Choose monthly or twice-monthly scans and set the specific dates that work best for your website. Once enabled, scans run automatically on the saved schedule, with no duplicate setups. You can review or update schedules anytime, check the latest scan status, and rely on audit logs to track all schedule changes for better visibility and control.

### Google Consent Mode Meets Mobile Apps

Teams often struggle when consent behaves differently across mobile apps. Google still requires clear consent signals for analytics and advertising to respect user choices. With Google Consent Mode v2 support now available in Securiti, you can standardize consent signaling across all your mobile apps. The consent preferences you collect in Securiti are shared with Google services based on your configuration. This works across iOS, Android, React Native, and Flutter apps, ensuring consistent behavior everywhere. You can enable this support in Mobile App Consent Management and reuse the same configuration across platforms.

[Learn how to enable Google Consent Mode v2 for mobile apps](/latest/docs/configuring-google-consent-mode-for-apps)

### Instantly Turn Off Non-Essential Cookies After a Do Not Sell or Share Request

When someone submits a Do Not Sell or Share request, the website often does not react right away. Cookies and third party tools can still stay active in that same session. This update helps close that gap. After the request is submitted, a signal is sent to the same browser and device. Your website can listen for that signal and turn off non essential cookies automatically. This limits third party access immediately. You can use the default listener script for cookie controls or adjust it to trigger other browser level actions.

[Show me how to do it](/latest/docs/configuring-signal-to-cookie-consent-in-dsr-form)

### Stop the DSR Clock Until Missing Details Are Available

Managing DSRs gets complicated when requests come in without enough context. You cannot proceed, yet the deadline keeps counting down. This update lets you pause a DSR deadline when more action or clarification is needed. You can pause for verification, scope clarification, or any reason your process requires. You can also notify the data subject and explain the delay. When the request resumes, the system recalculates the deadline for you. This reduces manual tracking and helps you maintain accountability across every request.

### Keep Datasets Locked with Read-Only Protection

Need a way to make sure important datasets aren’t accidentally updated? You can now mark datasets as read-only to help prevent unwanted edits. Once a dataset is read-only, its contents stay unchanged unless an authorized user approves an update. Only users with the right permission can remove the read-only status when changes are needed, so control stays with the right people. Every lock and unlock action is automatically recorded in the change log, which makes it easy to track what happened and who did it. And because read-only datasets are searchable, you can quickly find and manage the ones you want to keep stable.

[Explore dataset locking and permissions documentation](/latest/docs/datasets)

### Introducing RBAC for Gencore AI

Need tighter control over who can perform specific actions in Gencore AI? The new RBAC framework lets you define that. It introduces OOTB roles with default permissions, plus the flexibility to create custom ones that match real responsibilities. Whether it’s modifying firewall profiles or accessing storage metadata, you decide what’s allowed. Linked permissions handle dependencies automatically, keeping access consistent and secure. It’s a straightforward way to enforce least-privilege access while letting teams work confidently within their defined boundaries.
