# Gencore AI RBAC permissions and roles

The new Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) framework provides fine-grained permissions for Gencore AI. It lets you create custom roles with specific permissions.

Custom roles can include permissions that are linked to other related permissions, creating dependencies and ensuring consistent access control. In contrast, system-generated roles automatically inherit the specific permissions defined for them.

## Accessing Gencore AI permissions

To access the Gencore AI RBAC default permissions in the Securiti platform, follow the steps provided below:

1. From the upper left corner of the left navigation bar, click the ​**Jewel**​ icon, and choose ​**Settings**​​ from the left navigation bar.

![Settings page displaying active organizations with descriptions and owners listed.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Settings for roles.png)
2. Under the ​**Organizational**​​, choose **​Access Management > Roles​​**.

![System settings overview highlighting access management roles and organization information sections.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Settings-Roles(1).png)
3. On the top right of the ​Roles​ page, click ​**+ Create Role**​​.

![Create role](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/Create role(1).png)
4. On the **​Create Role**​ page under the ​**Permissions​​** section, click the **Gencore AI** tab to access the permissions, as shown below.

![Creating a role with permissions for Generative AI Module Owner and User.](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/RBAC Gencore AI Permissions(1).png)

## Gencore AI permissions with custom roles​​

Securiti lets you select fine-grained permission for each custom role you create in the Platform.

**​Definitions:**

- ​​**Permission**​​: It is a parent permission that grants specific rights to perform certain actions within the system. For example, `Read AI Model Card`​​.
- ​**Explanation**:​​ Explains why the permission is needed and in which context it is used.
- **​​Dependent permissions**:​​ These are a set of permissions that are automatically applied in the same or different modules when a parent permission is selected.
