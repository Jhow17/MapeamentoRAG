# Managing firewall policies

You can perform the following actions on a firewall policy:

- [View](/v1/docs/managing-firewall-policies#viewing-firewall-policy-details)
- [Edit](/v1/docs/managing-firewall-policies#editing-a-firewall-policy)
- [Delete](/v1/docs/managing-firewall-policies#deleting-a-firewall-policy)
- [Clone](/v1/docs/managing-firewall-policies#cloning-a-firewall-policy)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You cannot edit or delete system-defined policies.

### Viewing firewall policy details

1. On the list of policies, locate the card for the policy you want to view.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **View**.

### Editing a firewall policy

1. On the list of policies, locate the card for the policy you want to edit.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Edit**.
3. Update the policy as required.
4. To save changes, click **Update Policy**.

### Cloning a firewall policy

1. On the list of policies, locate the card for the policy you want to clone.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Clone**. A dialog box displays with the name of the policy to be cloned as the name of the original policy with the word “duplicate” added to it. You can edit the name as required.
3. To clone the policy, click **Clone**.

### Deleting a firewall policy

1. On the list of policies, locate the card for the policy you want to delete.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Delete**.
3. To confirm deletion, on the confirmation popup box, click **Delete Policy**.

Securiti policies translate data intelligence into actions. Each policy has rules that, when matched by a file, document, or resource, trigger a response.
