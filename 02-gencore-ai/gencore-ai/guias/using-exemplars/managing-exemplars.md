# Managing exemplars

You can perform the following actions on an exemplar:

- [View](/v1/docs/managing-exemplars#viewing-an-exemplar)
- [Edit](/v1/docs/managing-exemplars#editing-an-exemplar)
- [Clone](/v1/docs/managing-exemplars#cloning-an-exemplar)
- [Delete](/v1/docs/managing-exemplars#deleting-an-exemplar)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> You cannot edit or delete system-defined exemplars.

### Viewing an exemplar

1. On the list of exemplars, locate the card for the exemplar you want to view.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **View**.

### Editing an exemplar

1. On the list of exemplars, locate the card for the exemplar you want to edit.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Edit**.
3. Update the exemplar as required.
4. To save changes, click **Update Topic**.

### Cloning an exemplar

1. On the list of exemplars, locate the card for the exemplar you want to clone.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Clone**. A dialog box displays with the name of the exemplar to be cloned as the name of the original exemplars with the word “duplicate” added to it. You can edit the name as required.
3. To clone the exemplar, click **Clone**.

### Deleting an exemplar

1. On the list of exemplars, locate the card for the exemplar you want to delete.
2. To the right side of the card, choose **Options** (three dots), and select **Delete**.
3. To confirm deletion, on the confirmation popup box, click **Delete Topic**.
