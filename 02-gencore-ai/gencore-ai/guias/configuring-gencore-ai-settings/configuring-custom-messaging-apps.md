# Configuring custom messaging apps

**Scenario**

As a Data Security Officer, you need to ensure that messaging-based GenAI interactions comply with internal security and infrastructure policies. Relying solely on external hosting creates control and compliance concerns. With **Custom Messaging Apps** dashboard, you deploy and manage messaging integrations on your own infrastructure. This gives you full control over data flow and security while enabling seamless AI access for users.

In the **User Portal > Messaging Apps > Custom Apps** section, you can use custom apps on the Securiti infrastructure or you can carry out custom deployments for your messaging applications. In this case, you can configure your messaging applications on your own infrastructure.

You can create a custom messaging app for:

- [Slack](/latest/docs/creating-custom-slack-app)
- [Microsoft Teams](/latest/docs/creating-custom-microsoft-teams-apps)

### Prerequisites

To configure custom messaging apps, you must have an active account on the relevant messaging app portal.
