# Configuring messaging app integrations

In the **Messaging Apps** tab, you can integrate your messaging apps with your user portals.

- [Standard messaging apps](/latest/docs/configuring-standard-messaging-app): You can integrate standard messaging apps in both Securiti-hosted and self-hosted user portals.
- [Custom messaging apps](/latest/docs/configuring-custom-messaging-apps): You can only integrate custom messaging apps in self-hosted user portals.
