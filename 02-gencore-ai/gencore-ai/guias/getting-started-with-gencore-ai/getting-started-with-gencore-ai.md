# Getting Started with Gencore AI

Enterprise’s data ecosystems have evolved over the years with rigorous controls governing sensitive data, access controls, regulatory compliance and more. As the generative AI continues to make an impact on optimizing and boosting productivity in our work flows, the challenges that come with its main contributing component - data - also continue to grow. In the context of generative AI, data primarily refers to unstructured data. Historically, unstructured data has been set aside in governance solutions due to its complexity and perceived lower value as a data asset. However, generative AI is transforming this paradigm, elevating unstructured data into a highly valuable asset.

The Gencore AI module supports both GenAI pipeline firewalls and standalone profiles.

- GenAI pipelines let you create pipelines that ingest semi-structured and unstructured data safely transforming them to be used by GenAI models. [Get started with GenAI pipelines here](/v1/docs/getting-started-with-genai-pipelines).
- Gencore AI firewalls let you work with GenAI pipelines or independently to safeguard external AI applications by enforcing rules that prevent sensitive data exposure and regulate inputs and outputs. [Get started with Gencore AI firewalls here](/v1/docs/getting-started-with-gencore-ai-standalone-firewalls).
