# Scheduling IaaS connectors asset and posture scans

Securiti lets you choose the interval at which asset and posture scans are run for all IaaS connectors integrations.

1. In Securiti, open **Integrations** and choose **IaaS Connectors**.
2. On the **IaaS Connectors** dashboard, find the instance you want to configure scheduling for and then navigate to its **Instance Setup** tab.
3. Scroll down to the bottom of the page and mark the checkbox for **Schedule Automatic scans to discover data systems and their security posture**, then use the tools in that area to schedule the scans.

You can configure both the interval (period) for the scans and also define exclusion windows during which the scans are not performed.
4. When you are done, near the bottom of the page click **Save**.
