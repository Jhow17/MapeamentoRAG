# Acquire authentication credentials in BrainTree Payments

1. Log in to your BrainTree sandbox account.
2. On the Home page, click **Settings** near the upper right corner and choose **API** as shown below.

![select_API.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-d6fb52c6-ec48-910a-b9d2-19bfff3e0124.png)

On the next page, BrainTree shows you a **Public Key** and **Private Key**.
3. Click **View** to reveal the Private Key as shown below.

![click_View.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e882d730-d182-9ab0-cf07-0bc6e186a821.png)

![keys_braintree_1.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-de13a4e3-00d7-9f07-fdf0-7486b1194c15.png)
4. Record both the Public Key and Private Key to use later.
5. Proceed to [Connecting BrainTree Payments with Securiti](/latest/docs/connecting-braintree-with-securiti).
