# Configuring data system ownership

You can change the ownership of Securiti data systems, and also add co-owners to them. When you re-assign ownership, all open subtasks for the Data system are assigned to the new owner.

To change ownership for a Data system:

1. Log in to Securiti with your admin credentials.
2. To open the main menu, from the upper left corner of the left navigation bar, click the **Jewel** icon.
3. From the Platform Services section in the new navigation, select **Settings**.
4. From the **System Settings** menu, choose **Integrations** > **Data Systems** > **Monitored**.

1. Select the data system instance and go to Basic Information tab, under **Owners** section you will see the details of ownership

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-Y35XNCIF.png)
2. Click the **Primary Owner** box, and choose a new owner from the menu.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-2YM6BA2Z.png)
3. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Save**.

To add co-owners to a Data system:

1. Log in to Securiti with your admin credentials.
2. To open the main menu, from the upper left corner of the left navigation bar, click the **Jewel** icon.
3. From the Platform Services section in the new navigation, select **Settings**.
4. From the **System Settings** menu, choose **Integrations** > **Data Systems** > **Monitored**.

1. Click the card for the Data system and scroll down to the Owners area
2. Click **+ Add co-owner**.
3. Click the new co-owner box, and choose the new co-owner from the menu.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-XGMCX85O.png)

1. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Create and Continue**.
