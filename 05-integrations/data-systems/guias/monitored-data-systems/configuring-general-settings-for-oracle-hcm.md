# Configuring general scan settings for Oracle HCM

This article covers the post-authentication scan settings for the Oracle HCM connector.

## Breach Analysis

In the **Configuration** tab, under the **Breach** section, mark the **Breach Analysis** checkbox to initiate enhanced analysis for any reported incident for your data system and get an automated report for remediation.

When enabled and saved, this will scans and links identities to evaluate the unique identities present.
