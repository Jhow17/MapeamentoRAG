# Connector scanning limits

Connector scanning limits define the constraints on scanning data through Securiti cloud connectors. Each scanning connector stops scanning and triggers an alert after reaching its data limit. If this happens, contact Securiti sales at [support@securiti.ai](/docs/support@securiti.ai) to increase the data limits for your account.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**
> 
> - These limits do not apply to discovery scans or self-hosted connectors.
> - Contact [support@securiti.ai](mailto:support@securiti.ai) to configure scanning limits for your monitored data systems whether you are on premium or trial account.

For trial tenants, specific limits are enforced to manage system load, while premium tenants are typically set to unlimited.

- **Trial tenant limits**: By default, trial tenants have limits on the number of cloud connectors, data size, rows, and files that can be scanned. The values for trial tenants can be adjusted by contacting sales team.
- **Premium tenant limits**: For premium tenants, the limits are generally set to unlimited by default.
