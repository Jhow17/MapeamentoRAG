# Obtain the account domain for your Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM Suite (Self-hosted) account

To integrate Securiti with Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM Suite for DSR Automation, you must obtain the account domain name for your Dynamics CRM account:

1. Log in to your [Microsoft Dynamics CRM account](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365).

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-MRY5YIM2.png)
2. From the top navigation bar, click **Products.**

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/image-R1J0J38Z.png)
3. From the menu, select **Customer Service** (Or any other product you want to use).

![dynamicsCRM_customerservice.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-0be9ba5c-e22e-ea16-b688-a9a28a698690.png)
4. Enter your email address and click **Start Free Trial**. You can purchase the product from the account's setting.

![dynamicsCRM_freetrial.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-1e75a5a6-58ec-e9e5-b3d2-d754b9eac21c.png)
5. Click **Launch Trial.**

![dynamicsCRM_launchtrial.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-428af71d-b440-2098-dd69-a003dc6de784.png)
6. As you are redirected to the homepage, copy the initial part of the URL as highlighted in the screenshot below. This is the **Account Domain** required for Connector Activation. Record the **Account domain** to use later.

![dynamicsCRM_accountdomain.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-e4c05496-135b-e7d3-b771-1dc40caefa16.png)
7. Proceed to [Assigning security roles in Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM Suite (Self-hosted)](/latest/docs/assigning-security-roles-in-microsoft-dynamics-365-crm-suite-self-hosted).
