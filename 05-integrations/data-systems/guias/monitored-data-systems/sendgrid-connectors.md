# SendGrid

SendGrid is an email-based communications service provider which focuses on enabling marketing and transactional emails to be sent to customers on behalf of enterprise clients. Metric tracking functionality is also available to a certain extent to clients who wish to gauge their mailing lists' performance in terms of recipients' visibility and conversions. In an optional synchronization with Twilio, SendGrid is also able to provide the same suite of services for SMS and voice messages as well.

SendGrid falls under the category of [Monitored, Scanless](/latest/docs/managing-data-system-connectors) data systems.

### Connect SendGrid connectors with Securiti
