# Register an application in HelpScout

1. Log in to your HelpScout account.
2. Click your avatar near the upper right corner of the page and choose **Your Profile** as shown below.

![C00156.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a3b13429-27b0-ca42-83ad-1a7ddb2c36bb.png)
3. On the left sidebar, click **My Apps** as shown below.

![C00157.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-b8932f3e-b1e7-b028-f067-7520b586f199.png)
4. Click **Create My App** as shown below.

![C00158.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-a40e1fe4-d229-c446-7ef8-fffe2f7e23d9.png)
5. On the Create Custom App box, enter the following information:
  - For App Name enter any convenient name.
  - For Redirection URL, enter one of the following URLs:
    - US Cloud: `https://app.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - US1 Cloud: `https://app1.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Canada Cloud: `https://app3.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - EU Cloud: `https://app.eu.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Indonesia Cloud: `https://app2.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Australia Cloud: `https://app4.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - UAE Cloud: `https://app5.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Mumbai Cloud: `https://app6.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - KSA Cloud: `https://app7.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Qatar Cloud: `https://app8.securiti.ai/static/activateConnector.html`
    - Sovereign deployment: `https://&lt;sovereign_deployment_portal_host&gt;/static/activateConnector.html`
6. Click **Create**.

HelpScout creates the custom app and shows you its information.
7. Record the App ID and the App Secret as shown below to use later.

![C00159.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-77522942-c4a4-210f-765d-a3b3b970f18d.png)
8. Proceed to [Connecting HelpScout with Securiti](/latest/docs/connecting-helpscout-with-securiti).
