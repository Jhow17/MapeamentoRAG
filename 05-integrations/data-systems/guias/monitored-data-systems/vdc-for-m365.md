# VDC for M365

The Veeam Data Cloud connector enables Securiti to discover and govern data protected within your Veeam Data Cloud (VDC) environment for Microsoft 365 workloads.

## Supported authentication types

VDC for 365 connector supports Service Account-based authentication.

## Prerequisites

- > [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> - If Databricks Migration is not complete for your tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan and whether your tenant meets the eligibility requirements for its activation.
> - If Veeam Data Cloud integration is for your tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan and whether your tenant meets the eligibility requirements for its activation.
- You must integrate your VDC instance using Service Account credentials before connecting VDC For M365 Cloud connector. For more information, see [Integrate VDC instance in Securiti Platform](/latest/docs/veeam-data-cloud-integration).
- You must have RBAC permissions or role to create and activate connector in Securiti. For more information, see [Data Systems RBAC roles and permissions](/latest/docs/data-systems-rbac-permissions-and-roles#data-system-permissions-with-custom-roles).
