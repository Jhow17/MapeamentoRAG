# Register an application in PayPal

1. Browse to the PayPal developer console at [Paypal portal](https://developer.paypal.com).
2. Near the upper right corner of the page, click **Log into Dashboard**.

![C00020.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-3eb4b296-af2f-09d1-ca9a-04bd38e83979.png)
3. In the Dashboard area of the left sidebar, click **My Apps & Credentials**.

![C00021.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-f8e78687-7c74-ccb3-a1e6-2cbd09c8b370.png)
4. On the My Apps & Credentials page, in the REST APIs section, click **Create App**.

![C00022.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-99206a08-d914-71a6-641c-29249abf5841.png)
5. On the Create New App page, enter a custom App Name and your Sandbox Business Account, then click **Create App**.

![C00023.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-2249acff-f9b4-90ae-41b7-2d6c77fe1429.png)
6. On the application screen, toggle to the live tab as shown below.

![C00024.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-246b1f3c-89ab-b43c-93cf-4022ce302da2.png)
7. Record the Client ID and Client Secret to use later.

![C00025.png](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/uuid-34f10eb9-ea3c-3487-4e14-2cbf19a3d27a.png)

Proceed to [Connecting Paypal with Securiti](/latest/docs/connecting-paypal-with-securiti).
