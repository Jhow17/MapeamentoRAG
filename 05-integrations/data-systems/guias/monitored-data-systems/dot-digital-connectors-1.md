# Dot Digital

The Dot Digital connector lets you integrate your Dotdigital marketing platform with Securiti to fulfill Data Subject Rights (DSR) requests for data stored within your account. Dot Digital falls under the category of monitored, scanless data systems. For more information, see [data systems classification](/v1/docs/managing-data-system-connectors).

## Supported connector types and modules

| Connector type | Supported modules |
| --- | --- |
| Securiti Cloud | DSR Automation using APIs |

## Supported authentication methods

Dot Digital uses basic authentication with username and password to connect to Securiti.

## Connect Dot Digital with Securiti

To configure the Dot Digital connector, perform the procedures in the following topics:

1. [Acquire authentication credentials in Dot Digital](/v1/docs/acquire-authentication-credentials-in-dot-digital)
2. [Connecting Dot Digital with Securiti](/latest/docs/connecting-dot-digital-with-securiti)
3. [Fulfilling DSR requests for Dot Digital](/v1/docs/fulfilling-dsr-requests-for-dot-digital)
