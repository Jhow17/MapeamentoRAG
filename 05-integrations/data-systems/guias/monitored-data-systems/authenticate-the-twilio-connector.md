# Authenticate the Twilio connector

You must authenticate your Twilio data system to connect with Securiti.

## Basic authentication for Twilio connector

Follow the procedure below to acquire the **Account SID** and **Auth Token** for Twilio.

1. Log in to your Twilio account.
2. From the top right corner of the page, click **Admin** and select **Account Management** from the menu.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/authenticate-the-twilio-connector-en-image-nxdg5k9q.png)
3. In the **General Settings** page, record the **Account SID** value to use later.

![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/authenticate-the-twilio-connector-en-image-sqkqy0g0.png)
4. From the left navigation menu, select the **API Keys & Tokens**.
5. On the bottom of the **API Keys & Tokens** page, record the **Auth Token** value to use later. ![](https://cdn.us.document360.io/37f15d15-eeac-4caf-9724-25918cca6992/Images/Documentation/authenticate-the-twilio-connector-en-image-83y48izh.png)
6. Proceed to [Connecting Twilio with Securiti](/latest/docs/connecting-twillo-with-securiti).
