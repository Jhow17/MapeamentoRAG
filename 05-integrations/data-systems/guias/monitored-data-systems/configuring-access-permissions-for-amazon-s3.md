# Configuring Access permissions for Amazon S3

This article covers the general and access permissions for supported features of Amazon S3.

## Data Access Intelligence

> [!NOTE]
> Note
> 
> If Data Access Governance module is not available in your Securiti tenant, contact your Customer Success Manager or Account Representative to confirm whether it is included in your plan

In the **Access** tab, enable the toggle to run access scans on your Amazon Data system Instance. Access scans monitors your Data system to help you enforce least privilege access on your Sensitive data. The scan runs in **Monitor** mode, requiring read-only privilege to your Data system.

For more information, see [Data Access Governance for Amazon S3](/latest/docs/amazon-services).

## Breach Analysis

In the **Configuration** tab, under the **Breach** section, mark the **Breach Analysis** checkbox to initiate enhanced analysis for any reported incident for your data system and get an automated report for remediation.
