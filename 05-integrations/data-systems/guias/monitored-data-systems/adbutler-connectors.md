# Ad Butler

Ad Butler is a set of software-driven messenger clients for businesses to contact both prospective and present customers through a proprietary application, a website, social media, or via email.

Ad Butler has a general policy of automatically deleting all data pertaining to a visitor if that visitor has not recorded in the coming nine months. As part of their pledged compliance with GDPR, they have enabled data exportation and deletion rights (for residents of the European Union) to be used at the user's own will.

Ad Butler falls under the category of [Monitored, Scanless](/latest/docs/managing-data-system-connectors) data systems.
